# How we use AI tools

Example Firm LLP (fictional). Statement for Client B (fictional).

> DRAFT. Not approved for release.

## For your matters

This section reflects your written terms on AI dated 2026-10-05 (Engagement letter addendum on AI). Your terms permit AI-assisted tools on your matters. Your terms rule out these tools on your matters: the translation tool and the legal research and drafting tool. Your terms bar entering personal data into AI tools. No tool in use is approved to receive it. One of the two tools your terms restrict is checked against your terms before use on a matter. Your terms require us to tell you when we use these tools: by written notice at the start of a matter where a tool will be used. Where your terms are stricter than the rest of this statement, we follow your terms.

## What we use

We use four AI-assisted tools in client work: contract review, legal research and drafting, transcript summaries and translation. Two are in firmwide use and two are in a limited pilot. Each tool has a named owner at the firm who answers for it.

## What we use it for, and what we do not

We use these tools for: first-pass review of contracts against our clause checklist; research memos and first drafts of brief sections; summaries of deposition and hearing transcripts; working translations of public and internal documents. One of four tools in use is not approved to receive client confidential information. No tool in use is approved to receive personal data or health information.

## How your information is protected

No tool we use allows what we enter to be used to train a model. Each tool approved for client confidential information is covered by a written agreement with its provider, recorded in our tool registry. We enter your confidential information only into tools that our registry approves for client confidential information.

## How work is checked

Every tool in use has a written review step: a person at the firm reviews the tool's output before anyone relies on it. We test tools against a fixed set of documents with known answers. Every tool in use that this test applies to has a passing result on record. For each tool whose output may reach a court filing, we have a written step for an attorney to verify citations before filing and a set place where that verification is logged. We review tools every 180 days. Each tool in use has a review on record within that period. We tell you before we use one of these tools to draft part of a court filing in your matter.

## How we bill for AI-assisted work

We bill for the time our lawyers and staff spend, including time spent reviewing and correcting tool output. We do not bill for time a tool saved, and we do not add a separate charge for the tools described here.

## Your terms and opting out

Before a tool is used on a matter, we check that client's written terms on AI. Each tool approved for client confidential information has this check, and each check was confirmed to work in the past 90 days. You can tell us not to use these tools on your matters, or set conditions on their use. Write to your relationship partner or the Office of General Counsel. We record your instruction in the matter intake record and apply it to work done after we confirm receipt.
