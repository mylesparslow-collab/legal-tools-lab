# DRAFT - Tender of defense and request for independent counsel (synthetic)

Re: Estate of R. M. v. Larkspur Glen Care Center, LLC - Policy TSI-GL-2291047

The policy was in force on the date of the incident, with a policy period of 01/01/2026 to 01/01/2027 (POL-0001:4).

The insuring agreement provides that Tidewater “will have the right and duty to defend the insured against any suit seeking those damages.” (POL-0002:6-7)

Tidewater's reservation of rights letter relies on the abuse exclusion, which it quotes as barring coverage for “Bodily injury arising out of the actual or threatened abuse or neglect of any person in the care, custody or control of any insured.” (POL-0003:8-9)

In fact Endorsement 1 restores coverage for abuse or neglect claims, subject to a sublimit of $500,000 each claim (POL-0004:8).

That sublimit “does not apply to the cost of defense.” (POL-0004:10)

Endorsement 1 also states that Tidewater “shall defend such claims subject to a reservation of rights.” (POL-0004:13)

Because Tidewater defends under a reservation of rights on the enhanced remedies issue, the insured requests independent counsel. The policy commits Tidewater to pay “reasonable fees at the rates we actually pay to counsel we retain in the ordinary course of business” (POL-0005:13-14).

The self-insured retention is $50,000 each claim (POL-0001:9), and the professional services exclusion “is deleted.” (POL-0006:6)
