FORM TS 00 01 - SECTION I - COVERAGES
1. Insuring Agreement
We will pay those sums that the insured becomes legally obligated to pay as damages
because of bodily injury or property damage to which this insurance applies. We will
have the right and duty to defend the insured against any suit seeking those damages.
However, we will have no duty to defend the insured against any suit seeking damages
to which this insurance does not apply.
2. Exclusions
This insurance does not apply to:
a. Expected or Intended Injury. Bodily injury or property damage expected or intended
from the standpoint of the insured.
b. Contractual Liability. Bodily injury or property damage for which the insured is
obligated to pay damages by reason of the assumption of liability in a contract.SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT - FICTIONAL PERSONS AND ENTITIES - NOT A REAL RECORD
