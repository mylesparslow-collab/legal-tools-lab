Prop 65 Notice Desk

A first-pass desk for a Proposition 65 sixty-day notice. It reads the Attorney General's public notice database for what similar matters have settled for, runs the date and dose arithmetic from rules stored as data, and checks every record cite in a draft against the source page.

Personal portfolio demo built by a non-attorney. Not legal advice and not a firm tool. Settlement figures are public Attorney General data. Every case, chart, policy and lab report shown below is synthetic and every name in them is fictional.
