GULLWING HARBOR DISTRICT
Finance Department

Request for Proposals
Bond Counsel and Disclosure Counsel Services
RFP No. FIN-2026-03

Article 1.  About this request

Gullwing Harbor District (the "District") plans to issue revenue bonds in 2027
to pay for a breakwater repair and marina improvements. The District wants a
firm to serve as bond counsel and disclosure counsel for the issue.

The District's Finance Director, Evangeline Marchetti, is the contact for this
request. The Harbor Commission has five members: Chair Rupert Halvorsen,
Vice Chair Noor Abdelrahman, and Commissioners Felix Oyama, Clementine Radcliffe
and Jasper Thibodeaux. The District's municipal advisor is Lowtide Advisory
Partners LLC.

Article 3.  Who is eligible

A firm is eligible only if:

  (1) the attorney who will sign the opinion has been admitted to practice for
      fifteen years or more;
  (2) the firm is listed in a nationally recognized directory of bond counsel;
      and
  (3) the firm has served as bond counsel on five or more revenue bond issues
      for ports, harbors or similar enterprises since 2020.

Article 4.  What the proposal must contain

Organize the proposal into the tabs below. Tabs 1 to 6 may not be longer than
twelve pages in total. Resumes go in Tab 8 and do not count.

  Tab 1 - Transmittal letter, signed by a partner.
  Tab 2 - The firm. Give a short description of the firm and its public
          finance practice.
  Tab 3 - The people. Name the attorneys who will do the work and what each
          will do.
  Tab 4 - Comparable financings. List revenue bond issues on which the firm
          served as bond counsel or disclosure counsel in the last five years.
  Tab 5 - How you would handle this financing, including the schedule you
          would propose and how you would coordinate with the municipal
          advisor.
  Tab 6 - Three client references.
  Tab 7 - Fees. Quote a fixed fee for bond counsel services and a fixed fee
          for disclosure counsel services, each contingent on closing, and
          hourly rates for other work.
  Tab 8 - Resumes.
  Tab 9 - Conflicts. Tell us about any relationship with Lowtide Advisory
          Partners LLC or with any underwriter that may bid on the bonds.

Article 5.  Paperwork

Include these completed documents behind Tab 9:
  - Proposer's Affidavit (District Form 10), notarized
  - Statement of Insurance (District Form 11)
  - Acknowledgment of any addenda

Article 6.  How proposals will be judged

  Weight 35% - Experience with comparable financings
  Weight 25% - Qualifications of the attorneys assigned
  Weight 20% - Fees
  Weight 10% - Proposed handling of the financing
  Weight 10% - References

Article 7.  Insurance

Professional liability insurance with a limit of $5 million per claim is
required. The District does not require other coverage for this engagement.

Article 8.  Sending in your proposal

Send one PDF, no larger than 20 MB, to finance@gullwingharbor.example. Use a
font no smaller than 11 points. The District will not consider a proposal that
arrives after the time shown in Article 2.
