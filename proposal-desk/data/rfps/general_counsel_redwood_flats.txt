REDWOOD FLATS WATER DISTRICT
Request for Proposals: General Counsel Legal Services
RFP 26-GC-01

SECTION I - BACKGROUND

Redwood Flats Water District (the "District") provides water service to about
18,500 connections. The District is governed by a five-member Board of
Directors. The District's General Manager is Ottoline Brannigan. The Board
members are President Cassius Elmwood, Vice President Mireille Dunmore, and
Directors Anselm Torvald, Beatrix Okonkwo and Lucien Farraday.

The District's long-time general counsel is retiring. The District seeks a firm
to serve as general counsel beginning in early 2027.

SECTION II - TIMELINE

  Release of RFP .......................... November 2, 2026
  Deadline for questions .................. November 20, 2026, 3:00 p.m.
  District responses issued ............... November 25, 2026
  Proposal submission deadline ............ December 10, 2026, 2:00 p.m.
  Interviews with Board committee ......... December 17, 2026
  Board award (tentative) ................. January 14, 2027

SECTION III - SERVICES REQUESTED

  A. Attend regular Board meetings (second Thursday of each month) and
     committee meetings on request.
  B. Advise the Board and staff on open meeting and public records questions,
     conflict of interest questions and Board governance.
  C. Advise on water rates and charges, water supply contracts and groundwater
     matters.
  D. Draft and review contracts, including public works contracts, and advise
     on bidding.
  E. Advise on personnel matters.
  F. Advise on real property matters, including easements.

SECTION IV - MINIMUM REQUIREMENTS

  A. The lead attorney shall have no fewer than eight (8) years admitted to
     practice in the State of Westmark.
  B. The firm shall have at least three (3) attorneys available to serve the
     District.
  C. The firm shall have represented at least two special districts as general
     counsel in the last five years.

SECTION V - PROPOSAL REQUIREMENTS

Proposals shall be organized under the following headings, in this order.

  A. Letter of Transmittal. A letter signed by a principal of the firm, naming
     the person authorized to negotiate an agreement.
  B. Firm Qualifications. Describe the firm's history, size, office locations
     and its work for special districts.
  C. Assigned Personnel. Name the lead attorney and other attorneys who would
     serve the District, and state each person's role. Attach resumes.
  D. Special District Experience. Describe the firm's experience as general
     counsel to special districts, and in particular water agencies.
  E. Work Plan. Describe how the firm would deliver the services in Section
     III, including meeting attendance, turnaround times and how the firm
     keeps the Board informed.
  F. Client References. Provide a minimum of four (4) references from public
     agency clients.
  G. Potential Conflicts. Identify any current or former client whose interests
     may conflict with the District's, and any relationship with a Board member
     or District employee.
  H. Compensation. Propose a fee arrangement. State hourly rates by attorney
     and any retainer.
  I. Transition. Describe how the firm would take over from retiring counsel.
  J. Exceptions to Agreement. State any exception to the District's standard
     professional services agreement (Exhibit 2).

SECTION VI - FORMS AND CERTIFICATIONS

The following shall be submitted with the proposal. Failure to submit any of
them is grounds for rejection.

  1. Exhibit 1 - Proposer Certification (signed)
  2. Exhibit 3 - Non-Collusion Affidavit (notarized)
  3. Exhibit 4 - Conflict of Interest Disclosure Form
  4. Certificate of insurance or a letter from the proposer's broker

SECTION VII - SELECTION

Criteria and weights:

   Experience with special districts and water matters (30 points)
   Qualifications of assigned personnel (25 points)
   Work plan and responsiveness (20 points)
   Compensation (15 points)
   References (10 points)

SECTION VIII - INSURANCE

The successful proposer shall carry professional liability (errors and
omissions) coverage with limits of at least $1,000,000 per claim and $2,000,000
aggregate; general liability coverage of at least $1,000,000 per occurrence;
and automobile liability coverage of at least $500,000.

SECTION IX - SUBMITTAL

Deliver one (1) unbound original, six (6) bound copies and one (1) USB drive
containing a PDF copy in a sealed envelope marked "General Counsel Proposal" to
the District Secretary, Redwood Flats Water District, 400 Cistern Road, Redwood
Flats. Proposals sent by email or fax will not be considered. Proposals are
limited to fifteen (15) pages, not counting resumes, the forms in Section VI
and the transmittal letter. Minimum font size is 11 point.
