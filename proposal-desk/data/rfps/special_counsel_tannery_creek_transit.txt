TANNERY CREEK TRANSIT AUTHORITY

REQUEST FOR PROPOSALS
SPECIAL COUNSEL - EASTLINE EXTENSION CAPITAL PROJECT
Solicitation No. TCTA-26-031

PART A - GENERAL INFORMATION

A.1 Purpose

Tannery Creek Transit Authority (the "Authority") seeks special counsel for the
Eastline Extension, a 6.4-mile light rail extension with four stations. Special
counsel will advise on construction contracting, real property acquisition and
claims.

A.2 Background

The Authority's design-build contractor for the first project segment is
Granite Spur Constructors, Inc. The Authority is in a dispute with Granite Spur
Constructors, Inc. over differing site condition claims, and has received a
claim from Bellweather Signal Systems LLC, a subcontractor. Several parcels
needed for the project are owned by Hollis Yard Properties LP, with which the
Authority is negotiating. Proposers should assume that these parties are
adverse to the Authority.

PART B - SCOPE OF WORK

B.1 Construction contracting. Advise on design-build contract administration,
change orders and claims avoidance.
B.2 Claims and disputes. Represent the Authority in the dispute resolution
process under the design-build contract.
B.3 Real property. Advise on right-of-way acquisition, including appraisals,
offers and easements.
B.4 Board support. Attend closed sessions of the Authority's Board on request.

PART C - PROPOSAL INSTRUCTIONS

C.1 Minimum Qualifications

C.1.1 The proposed lead attorney shall have not less than twelve (12) years of
admission to the bar.
C.1.2 The proposed lead attorney shall have served as lead counsel on at least
one public transit or rail capital project with a construction value over
$100 million.
C.1.3 The firm shall have an office in the Authority's service area or commit
to attending project meetings in person.

C.2 Required Proposal Sections

Each proposal shall contain the following sections, numbered as shown.

C.2.1 Executive Summary and Firm Profile. Summarize the proposal and describe
the firm, its size, its offices and its public agency practice.
C.2.2 Project Team. Identify the lead attorney and each team member, with
roles and time commitment. Attach resumes.
C.2.3 Relevant Experience. Describe up to five engagements as counsel on
public capital projects in the last ten years.
C.2.4 Project Approach. Describe how the firm would advise on design-build
contract administration, change orders, claims avoidance and right-of-way
acquisition.
C.2.5 References. Provide three references for engagements described in C.2.3.
C.2.6 Conflicts of Interest. Disclose any current or past representation of
the parties named in A.2 and any other actual or potential conflict.
C.2.7 Small and Local Business Participation. Describe the firm's local
presence and any participation by small or local firms.
C.2.8 Cost Proposal. Submit hourly rates and a not-to-exceed budget for the
first year as a separate file.
C.2.9 Staffing Continuity and Transition. Describe how the firm will keep the
team stable for the life of the project and how it would take over files
from current counsel.

C.3 Format

Proposals shall not exceed thirty (30) pages, excluding resumes, required
forms and the Cost Proposal. Text shall be no smaller than 11-point font with
margins of at least 1 inch.

C.4 Delivery

Submit proposals through the Authority's online procurement portal. Upload the
technical proposal and the Cost Proposal as separate PDF files. Hard copies
will not be accepted.
