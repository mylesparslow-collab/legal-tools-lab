QUILLFEATHER UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT
Human Resources Division

REQUEST FOR PROPOSALS
LABOR NEGOTIATIONS - CHIEF NEGOTIATOR AND RELATED LEGAL SERVICES
RFP #HR-2026-07


OVERVIEW

   Quillfeather Unified School District (the "District") seeks
proposals from law firms and qualified individuals to act as the
District's chief negotiator in successor contract negotiations with
its two employee organizations, the Quillfeather Teachers
Association and the Quillfeather Classified Staff Union, and to give
related labor and employment advice.  The District serves roughly
9,200 students at fourteen school sites.

   District contacts for this RFP:
        Dr. Marisol Everhart, Superintendent
        Leopold Achterberg, Assistant Superintendent, Human Resources

   Board of Trustees: Henrietta Solano (President), Dmitri Faulkner
   (Clerk), Yolanda Prescott, Bartholomew Nguyen and Odessa Kirkland.

IMPORTANT DATES

   RFP released:                 11/12/2026
   Last day to submit questions: 12/04/2026 (by 4 PM)
   Proposals due:                Fri., Dec. 11, 2026 - 12:00 noon
   Finalist interviews:          week of January 11, 2027
   Board approval:			 01/26/2027

   Dates after the proposal due date are estimates.

WHO MAY PROPOSE

   - The proposed chief negotiator must have at least seven (7)
     years of experience as a licensed attorney.
   - The proposed chief negotiator must have served as chief
     negotiator for a school district in at least three completed
     negotiations.
   - Proposers must be available for evening bargaining sessions.

WHAT TO SUBMIT

   Answer each question below.  Keep the question numbers.  Answers
   to Q1 through Q8 together shall not exceed 10 pages.

   Q1) Tell us about your firm: how long it has practiced, how
       many attorneys it has, where its offices are, and how much
       of its work is for school districts and other public
       employers.

   Q2) Who would be our chief negotiator and who would back that
       person up?  Attach resumes (not counted toward the page
       limit).

   Q3) Describe your experience at the bargaining table for public
       school employers in the last five years, including any
       negotiations that went to impasse or factfinding.

   Q4) How do you prepare for and run negotiations?  Describe your
       approach to costing proposals, keeping the Board informed in
       closed session, and communicating with employees during
       bargaining.

   Q5) List three references from school districts for which you
       have negotiated in the last five years.

   Q6) Do you or your firm have any relationship with the
       Quillfeather Teachers Association, the Quillfeather
       Classified Staff Union, or their parent organizations, or
       any other conflict of interest?  If so, describe it.

   Q7) State your proposed fees.  Tell us your hourly rates and
       whether you offer a flat fee per negotiation.

   Q8) Is there anything else the District should consider?  This
       question is optional.

   Also required with your proposal:

   * Signed Proposal Certification (Attachment 1)
   * Fingerprinting and Background Check Acknowledgment
     (Attachment 2)
   * Proof of professional liability insurance, minimum $1,000,000
     per claim

HOW WE WILL CHOOSE

   A committee will score written proposals on these factors:

      Experience in school district negotiations - 40
      Proposed negotiator and team - 25
      Approach - 15
      Fees - 20
      Interview performance (if interviews are held)
