CITY OF MARLOW RIDGE

REQUEST FOR PROPOSALS No. 2026-14
CITY ATTORNEY SERVICES

Issued: November 9, 2026


1. INTRODUCTION

The City of Marlow Ridge ("City") invites proposals from qualified law firms to
serve as City Attorney on a contract basis. The City is a general law city with
a population of about 41,000 and a five-member City Council. The City Manager is
Renata Oyelaran. The current contract for city attorney services ends on
June 30, 2027.

City Council:

  Vice Mayor Imani Castellanos
  Councilmember Greta Lindqvist

3. SCOPE OF SERVICES

The City Attorney will:

(a) attend all regular City Council meetings and Planning Commission meetings
    on request;
(b) advise the City Council and staff on open meeting, public records and
    conflict of interest questions;
(c) draft and review ordinances, resolutions and contracts;
(d) advise on land use, planning and code enforcement matters;
(e) advise on personnel matters and public works bidding;
(f) manage litigation and supervise outside counsel.

4. MINIMUM QUALIFICATIONS

4.1 The attorney proposed as City Attorney must have been admitted to practice
    law in the State of Westmark for at least ten (10) years.
4.2 The firm must maintain an office within Larkspur County.
4.3 The attorney proposed as City Attorney must have at least five (5) years of
    experience advising a city council or comparable governing body.
4.4 The firm must not have any pending claim against the City.

5. PROPOSAL CONTENT

Proposals must respond to each item below in the order listed and must use the
same numbering.

5.1 Cover Letter. Provide a cover letter signed by a person authorized to bind
    the firm.
5.2 Firm Overview. Describe the firm, including its history, size, office
    locations and public agency practice.
5.3 Proposed Team. Identify the attorney proposed as City Attorney and each
    attorney who would back up that person. Include resumes as an appendix.
5.4 Experience. Describe the firm's experience serving as city attorney or
    general counsel to public agencies during the past five years.
5.5 Approach. Describe how the firm will provide day-to-day services, including
    availability, response times and attendance at City Council meetings.
5.6 References. Provide at least three (3) references from current or former
    public agency clients, with a contact name, telephone number and email.
5.7 Conflicts of Interest. Disclose any actual or potential conflict of
    interest with the City, its officials or its employees.
5.8 Transition Plan. Describe how the firm would take over open matters from
    the current city attorney.
5.9 Fee Proposal. State the proposed monthly retainer, the services it covers
    and hourly rates for services outside the retainer.
5.10 Local Presence. Describe the firm's presence in the region.
5.11 Litigation and Discipline History. Disclose any malpractice claim or
    disciplinary action against the firm or any proposed attorney in the past
    five years.

8. INSURANCE

The selected firm must maintain the following insurance during the contract
term:

8.1 Professional liability insurance of not less than $2,000,000 per claim.
8.2 Commercial general liability insurance of not less than $1,000,000 per
    occurrence.
8.3 Workers' compensation insurance as required by law.

The proposal must include a statement that the firm can meet these insurance
requirements.

9. SUBMISSION INSTRUCTIONS

Submit one (1) electronic copy of the proposal in PDF format by email to
cityclerk@marlowridge.example. Paper copies will not be accepted. The proposal,
excluding resumes and required forms, must not exceed 25 pages. Use 12-point
font and one-inch margins. Late proposals will not be accepted. Direct
