E Work Plan

RFP E: Work Plan. Describe how the firm would deliver the services in Section III, including meeting attendance, turnaround times and how the firm keeps the Board informed.

We propose to serve Redwood Flats Water District with one lead attorney who attends every regular Board of Directors meeting and one backup attorney who knows the open files. Staff questions that do not need research are answered the same business day. Contracts and resolutions are reviewed within three business days unless staff asks for a faster turn. Before each meeting we review the agenda with the General Manager and flag open meeting, public records and conflict of interest questions. A monthly report lists open matters, hours and spending against the retainer. For a district that sets its own rates we also review rate studies and notices for rates and charges, and we advise on water supply contracts and public works bidding as they arise.

Source: answer A-0065, proposal P-2024-03, submitted 2024-08-23, outcome won, approved by Margit Solberg, review by 2027-08-23

I Transition

RFP I: Transition. Describe how the firm would take over from retiring counsel.

A transition has three steps: files, people and deadlines. We ask prior counsel for a status memo on each open matter and for the complete files. We introduce the team to staff and agree how requests will be sent and tracked. We confirm every litigation and contract deadline against the source documents. For continuity, the same lead attorney and backup attorney stay on the engagement for its full term.
