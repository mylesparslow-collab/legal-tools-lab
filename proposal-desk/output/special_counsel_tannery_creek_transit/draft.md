## C.2.4 Project Approach

> RFP C.2.4: Project Approach. Describe how the firm would advise on design-build contract administration, change orders, claims avoidance and right-of-way acquisition.

Claims avoidance starts with the contract. We make sure that notice, documentation and dispute resolution steps are clear and that staff are trained to follow them. When a change order request arrives we check entitlement first and price second, and we give the project manager a written recommendation. We prepare the Authority for the dispute resolution process under the design-build contract by keeping the project record in order from the first day. On real property we coordinate appraisals, offers and easements with the engineering schedule.
