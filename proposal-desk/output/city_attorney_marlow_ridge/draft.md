## 5.5 Approach

> RFP 5.5: Approach. Describe how the firm will provide day-to-day services, including availability, response times and attendance at City Council meetings.

We propose to serve City of Marlow Ridge with one lead attorney who attends every regular City Council meeting and one backup attorney who knows the open files. Staff questions that do not need research are answered the same business day. Contracts and resolutions are reviewed within three business days unless staff asks for a faster turn. Before each meeting we review the agenda with the City Manager and flag open meeting, public records and conflict of interest questions. A monthly report lists open matters, hours and spending against the retainer. For a city we also attend Planning Commission meetings on request and advise on land use and code enforcement.

## 5.8 Transition Plan

> RFP 5.8: Transition Plan. Describe how the firm would take over open matters from the current city attorney.

A transition has three steps: files, people and deadlines. We ask prior counsel for a status memo on each open matter and for the complete files. We introduce the team to staff and agree how requests will be sent and tracked. We confirm every litigation and contract deadline against the source documents. For continuity, the same lead attorney and backup attorney stay on the engagement for its full term.
