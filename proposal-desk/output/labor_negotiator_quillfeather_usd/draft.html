RFP Q1: Tell us about your firm: how long it has practiced, how many attorneys it has, where its offices are, and how much of its work is for school districts and other public employers.

Q4 Approach

RFP Q4: How do you prepare for and run negotiations? Describe your approach to costing proposals, keeping the Board informed in closed session, and communicating with employees during bargaining.

Our approach to negotiations has four parts: preparation, costing, communication and closing. In preparation we compare the District's agreements with those of similar employers. In costing we build a model with the business office so that every proposal has a total cost before it is passed across the table. We agree ground rules on communicating with employees at the first session and we draft updates for the District to send. At closing we draft the tentative agreement language and check it against the table notes before signature.

Source: answer A-0092, proposal P-2025-03, submitted 2025-09-05, outcome won, approved by Priya Raghunathan, review by 2028-09-05
