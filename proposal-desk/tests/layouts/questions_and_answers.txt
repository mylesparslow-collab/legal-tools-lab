PINE HOLLOW LIBRARY DISTRICT

Request for Proposals: Employment Counsel
Questions and Answers for Interested Firms

Who is asking?

Pine Hollow Library District (the "District") runs four branch libraries. The
Library Director is Odalys Brennecke. The Board of Trustees is chaired by
Board President Ignace Holloway. Staff are represented by the Pine Hollow
Library Workers Union.

When are things due?

The District released this request on Tuesday, the 1st of December 2026.
Send questions to the Library Director by Thursday, the 10th of December 2026
at noon. The District will post its answers on December 15th, 2026. Proposals
are due no later than 2:00 p.m. on January 14th, 2027. Interviews will be held
on January 26th, 2027, and the Board expects to award the contract at its
meeting on February 9th, 2027.

Who can apply?

A firm can apply only if its lead attorney has been admitted to practice for
at least eight (8) years, and the firm has at least four (4) attorneys who
practice employment law.

What must the proposal cover?

Answer each numbered item.

#1  Tell us about the firm and its offices.
#2  Who would do the work? Attach resumes.
#3  Describe your experience advising public libraries or similar agencies.
#4  How would you handle a grievance arbitration from start to finish?
#5  Give three references.
#6  Disclose any conflict of interest.
#7  State your hourly rates.

You must also tell us whether any attorney on the team has been disciplined.

What forms are required?

Return Form 1, Signature Sheet, and Form 2, Non-Collusion Statement.

How will proposals be scored?

Experience is worth 40 points, the team 30 points, approach 20 points and
price 10 points.

What insurance is needed?

The firm must carry professional liability insurance of at least $1,000,000
and general liability insurance of at least $2,000,000.

How do we submit?

Email one PDF to rfp@pinehollowlibrary.example. Proposals may not exceed
fifteen (15) pages, not counting resumes. Use 12-point type. Faxed proposals
will not be accepted.
