A. INTRODUCTION

Ashdown Valley Community College District (the "District") operates two
colleges, Pellham College and Sorrel Canyon College, and an adult education
center. The District enrolls about 21,000 students each year and employs about
1,400 faculty and staff. It is governed by a seven-member Board of Trustees.

The Board of Trustees is composed of Board President Nadira Whitcombe, Vice
President Osric Tavener, Clerk Imelda Rasmussen, and Trustees Corwin Addai,
Livia Montrose, Thaddeus Okafor-Lind and Priya Venkataraman. The Chancellor is
Dr. Alaric Fenwick. The Vice Chancellor, Administrative Services, is Marguerite
Solberg, who is the District's contact for this RFP.

The District's contract with its present general counsel ends on 31 March
2027. The District invites proposals from qualified law firms to serve as
general counsel for an initial term of three years, with two optional one-year
extensions.

Proposers may not contact any Trustee about this RFP. All contact must go
through the Vice Chancellor at purchasing@ashdownvalleyccd.example.

C. SCOPE OF SERVICES

The firm selected will act as the District's general counsel. The work is
expected to include the following.

   (i)    Attend the regular monthly meeting of the Board of Trustees, held on
          the second Tuesday of each month at 6:00 p.m., and special meetings
          and closed sessions as requested.
   (ii)   Advise the Board, the Chancellor and administrators on governance,
          open meeting and public records questions, and conflicts of
          interest.
   (iii)  Advise on student matters, including discipline, records,
          accommodation of students with disabilities and complaints of
          discrimination or harassment.
   (iv)   Advise on personnel matters for academic and classified employees,
          including evaluation, discipline and dismissal, leaves and
          accommodation, and represent the District in administrative
          hearings.
   (v)    Review and draft contracts, including purchasing, construction,
          technology and instructional service agreements.
   (vi)   Advise on facilities matters, including the District's bond-funded
          building program, bidding and construction claims.
   (vii)  Represent the District in litigation not covered by the District's
          risk pool, when assigned.
   (viii) Provide up to four training sessions each year for trustees and
          managers.

Collective bargaining is handled by separate counsel and is not part of this
RFP. The District's faculty are represented by the Ashdown Valley Faculty
Association and its classified staff by the Ashdown Classified Employees
Union.


D. MINIMUM QUALIFICATIONS

A proposal that does not show each of the following will not be scored.

   (i)   The attorney proposed as lead counsel shall have been a member in
         good standing of the State Bar of Westmark for at least ten (10)
         years.
   (ii)  The firm shall employ at least five (5) attorneys who practice
         education law or public agency law.
   (iii) The firm shall have served as general counsel to at least three
         community college districts or school districts within the past
         seven years.
   (iv)  The firm shall maintain a staffed office within Calloway County.


E. CONTENT OF PROPOSAL

Proposals shall respond to each item below, in the order given, and shall
repeat the item number and heading before each response.

   (i)    Transmittal Letter. Provide a letter of no more than two pages,
          signed by a person with authority to bind the firm, that names the
          firm's contact person and states that the proposal is valid for
          120 days.
   (ii)   Firm Description. Describe the firm's history, ownership, size and
          office locations, and the share of its practice devoted to public
          education clients.
   (iii)  Proposed Attorneys. Identify the lead counsel, the backup counsel
          and every other attorney who would regularly work for the District.
          For each, state years of practice, relevant experience and the
          percentage of time available to the District. Include resumes as an
          appendix.
   (iv)   Education Law Experience. Describe the firm's experience over the
          last five years in each area listed in Section C, and list the
          community college districts and school districts the firm now
          serves as general counsel.
   (v)    Service Approach. Explain how the firm would handle day-to-day
          requests for advice, including expected response times, who answers
          the telephone after hours, and how work is assigned among attorneys.
   (vi)   How would the firm staff two urgent matters arising in the same
          week, for example a student expulsion hearing and a public records
          lawsuit?
   (vii)  References. Give the name, title, telephone number and email
          address of five public education clients for whom the firm has
          worked in the last three years. At least two must be community
          college districts.
   (viii) Conflicts of Interest. Disclose any present or past representation
          of the Ashdown Valley Faculty Association, the Ashdown Classified
          Employees Union, or any Trustee or employee of the District, and
          any other matter that could present a conflict. State how the firm
          would resolve each.
   (ix)   Fee Proposal. State the hourly rate for each attorney and
          paralegal, any proposed monthly retainer and the services it
          covers, rates for travel time, and all costs the firm would bill
          separately. State whether rates are fixed for the initial term.
   (x)    Transition Plan. Describe the steps the firm would take in its first
          ninety days, including the transfer of open files from present
          counsel.
   (xi)   Claims and Discipline. Has the firm, or any attorney named in the
          proposal, been the subject of a malpractice claim or disciplinary
          action in the last ten years? If so, explain.

In addition to the numbered items, every proposal must include a one-page
organization chart showing the attorneys who would serve the District.

A proposer that takes exception to any term of the District's standard
agreement for legal services (Appendix 1) must list each exception on a
separate page headed "Exceptions". Exceptions raised after the due date will
not be considered.

G. EVALUATION AND SELECTION

A committee appointed by the Chancellor will read and score the proposals.
Proposals will be scored as follows: demonstrated experience in community
college and education law, forty percent (40%); qualifications and
availability of the proposed attorneys, twenty-five percent (25%); service
approach and responsiveness, fifteen percent (15%); fees, fifteen percent
(15%); references, five percent (5%).

The committee may invite up to four firms to interview. Interviews are not
separately scored. The committee will recommend one firm to the Board of
Trustees, which makes the final decision and may reject all proposals.


H. INSURANCE

Before the agreement is signed, the selected firm shall provide certificates
showing the following coverage, and shall keep it in force for the term of the
agreement: lawyers' professional liability insurance of not less than
$3,000,000 per claim and $5,000,000 in the annual aggregate; commercial
general liability insurance of not less than $1,000,000 per occurrence;
workers' compensation insurance as required by law; and automobile liability
insurance of not less than $1,000,000 combined single limit. With its
proposal, the firm need only provide a letter from its broker or a sample
certificate.
