PART ONE
THE AUTHORITY AND THIS REQUEST

1.1  Kestrel Ridge Joint Powers Insurance Authority (the "Authority") is a
     risk-sharing pool formed by 34 cities and special districts. It provides
     liability, property and workers' compensation coverage to its members
     under memoranda of coverage adopted by its Board of Directors.

1.2  The Authority seeks a law firm to serve as coverage counsel. Coverage
     counsel advises the Authority's staff and its Claims Committee on
     whether claims against members are covered, drafts reservation of rights
     and denial letters, reviews proposed amendments to the memoranda of
     coverage, monitors the work of defense counsel on large losses, and
     represents the Authority in coverage disputes with members and with
     reinsurers.

1.3  The Authority's Executive Director is Rosamund Achebe. Its Claims
     Manager is Tobias Ferreira. The officers of the Board of Directors are
     President Halden Yoshimura, Vice President Clementine Abara and
     Treasurer Lucius Pemberton. The Authority's third-party claims
     administrator is Stonebridge Claims Services, Inc., and its excess
     coverage is placed through Marlin Reach Reinsurance Company.

1.4  Questions may be sent only to the Claims Manager, in writing, at
     rfp@kestrelridgejpia.example.

PART THREE
PROPOSAL CONTENTS

The proposal shall contain the following, under these numbers.

3.1  Letter of Interest.  A signed letter stating the firm's interest, the
     name of the attorney who would serve as lead coverage counsel, and the
     firm's agreement to the Authority's form of legal services agreement or
     a list of the changes it would ask for.

3.2  The Firm.  The firm's size, structure and offices, the number of
     attorneys who practice insurance coverage law, and the firm's history
     of work for public entity risk pools.

3.3  Personnel.  The names and qualifications of the lead coverage counsel
     and each other attorney proposed, the work each would do, and a
     statement of each attorney's current caseload.

3.4  Coverage Experience.  A description of the firm's work in the last five
     years on coverage opinions, reservation of rights letters, coverage
     litigation and reinsurance disputes for public entity pools or
     insurers. Identify three written coverage opinions (with privileged
     content removed) that the Authority may ask to see.

3.5  Method of Service.  How the firm would deliver coverage opinions within
     the Authority's standard of ten business days, how it would handle
     requests marked urgent, and how it would report to the Claims Committee
     each quarter.

3.6  Client References.  Three references from risk pools or insurers, with
     the name and direct contact details of a person familiar with the
     firm's work.

3.7  Training Program.  A proposed annual program of training for member
     agency staff on claims reporting and coverage, with sample materials.

3.8  Conflicts.  A list of every member of the Authority that the firm has
     represented in the last five years, every matter in which the firm has
     acted for or against Stonebridge Claims Services, Inc. or Marlin Reach
     Reinsurance Company, and a statement of how any conflict would be
     handled. The list of members is in Appendix A.

3.9  Pricing.  Hourly rates by attorney and paralegal; a proposed flat fee
     per written coverage opinion; and any annual cap the firm is willing to
     offer.

3.10 Additional Information.  Anything else the firm wishes the Authority to
     consider, in no more than one page.

PART FOUR
ELIGIBILITY

4.1  The Authority will consider only firms that meet all of these
     conditions.

     a.  Lead coverage counsel has been licensed to practice law for twelve
         (12) or more years.
     b.  Lead coverage counsel has given coverage advice to a public entity
         risk pool or a liability insurer for seven (7) or more years.
     c.  The firm has three (3) or more attorneys whose practice is mainly
         insurance coverage.
     d.  Neither the firm nor any proposed attorney is defending a member of
         the Authority in a claim that the Authority covers.

PART FIVE
REQUIRED PAPERWORK

5.1  Each proposal must include the following documents, completed and
     signed where a signature is called for. They are provided in Appendix B.

     Document 1    Proposal Cover Sheet
     Document 2    Certification of Non-Collusion
     Document 3    Conflict of Interest Questionnaire
     Document 4    Certificate of Insurance (or broker's letter)

5.2  The Authority may reject a proposal that omits any required document.

     Method of service (3.5) ................................ 15 pts
     Pricing (3.9) .......................................... 15 pts
     Training program (3.7) ................................. 10 pts
     Client references (3.6) ................................ 5 pts

     may be used to adjust scores for Personnel and Method of service.

PART SEVEN
INSURANCE THE FIRM MUST CARRY

7.1  Professional liability (errors and omissions): $2,000,000 each claim,
     $4,000,000 aggregate.
7.2  Commercial general liability: $1,000,000 each occurrence.
7.3  Workers' compensation: statutory limits.
7.4  Cyber liability: $1,000,000 each claim.

PART EIGHT
FORMAT AND DELIVERY

8.1  Proposals are limited to 25 pages, single-spaced, in a font no smaller
     than 11 point, with one-inch margins. The documents required by Part
     Five, resumes and the sample coverage opinions are outside the limit.

8.2  Deliver one (1) signed original and five (5) copies, together with a
     flash drive holding the proposal in PDF, in a sealed box or envelope
     labelled "RFP KR-2027-LEGAL-02", to: Claims Manager, Kestrel Ridge Joint
     Powers Insurance Authority, 880 Aerie Court, Suite 300, Halvering.

8.3  Proposals sent by email or facsimile will not be accepted. A proposal
     received after the time in Part Two will be returned unopened.

PART NINE
TERMS OF THIS REQUEST

9.1  This request does not commit the Authority to award a contract or to
     pay any cost of responding.
9.2  The Authority may accept a proposal as submitted or negotiate changes.
9.3  All proposals become the property of the Authority and are subject to
     public disclosure after award.
9.4  The initial contract term is three years beginning July 1, 2027.
9.5  A proposer may withdraw its proposal at any time before the due date by
     written notice to the Claims Manager.
