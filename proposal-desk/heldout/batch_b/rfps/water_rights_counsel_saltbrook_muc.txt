SCHEDULE 1.  BACKGROUND AND SERVICES
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Saltbrook Municipal Utilities Commission (the "Commission") supplies water
and wastewater service to the City of Saltbrook and nearby unincorporated
areas. Its water comes from surface water diverted from the Tamsel River
under rights dating from 1911 and 1954, from four groundwater wells, and from
a purchase contract with the Upper Tamsel Irrigation District.

The Commission needs special counsel for three things. One: a pending
proceeding before the state water board in which downstream users, led by
the Lower Tamsel Growers Association, ask the board to cut back the
Commission's 1954 right. Two: renegotiating the purchase contract with the
Upper Tamsel Irrigation District, which expires in 2029. Three: general advice
on water rights, groundwater sustainability planning, drought restrictions and
dealings with state and federal water regulators.

The Commission has five Commissioners: President Evadne Thornbury, Vice
President Silas Okoro, and Commissioners Maren Castellanos, Dorian Whitfield
and Josephine Nakamura. The General Manager is Lorcan Asante. The Commission's
General Counsel, Petra Vandermeer, will supervise special counsel.

Proposers must treat the Lower Tamsel Growers Association and the Upper Tamsel
Irrigation District as parties adverse to the Commission.

SCHEDULE 2.  THRESHOLD REQUIREMENTS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Proposals are screened against these thresholds before scoring. A proposal
that fails any threshold is set aside.

  T1  Lead attorney: admitted to practice 12 years or longer.
  T2  Lead attorney: has appeared as counsel of record in at least three
      contested water rights proceedings before a state water agency or a
      court.
  T3  Firm: at least 2 attorneys other than the lead attorney with five or
      more years of water law practice.
  T4  Firm: no current client relationship with either adverse party named
      in Schedule 1.

SCHEDULE 4.  SUPPLEMENTAL QUESTIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Answer the following in a separate section headed "Supplemental Questions".
These answers are scored as part of R6 and R3.

  What is the firm's view of the main risks to the Commission's 1954 right,
  based only on the public record of the water board proceeding?

  If the lead attorney became unavailable for a month during the hearing,
  who would take over, and what has that person done that is comparable?

  Describe one matter in which the firm negotiated a long-term water
  purchase or transfer agreement for a public supplier. What terms did the
  firm obtain on price adjustment and shortage sharing?
