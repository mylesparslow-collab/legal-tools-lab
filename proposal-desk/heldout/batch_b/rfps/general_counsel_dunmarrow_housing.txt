REQUEST FOR PROPOSALS AS ISSUED
==============================================================================

1.0  INTRODUCTION

1.1  The Housing Authority of the County of Dunmarrow (the "Authority") owns
and manages 640 units of public and affordable housing, administers about
3,900 housing vouchers, and develops new affordable housing through
affiliated partnerships. It is governed by a seven-member Board of
Commissioners, two of whom are residents of Authority housing.

1.2  The Authority requests proposals from law firms to serve as (a) general
counsel and (b) affordable housing development counsel. A firm may propose
for both or for either one.

1.3  The Authority's Executive Director is Marisol Etxeberria. The Deputy
Executive Director for Real Estate is Cormac Adebayo. The Procurement
Officer, Thea Lindgren, is the sole point of contact for this RFP and may be
reached at procurement@dunmarrowhousing.example. The Chair of the Board of
Commissioners is Augustin Pereira and the Vice Chair is Noelle Hathersage.

1.4  The Authority's present development partners include Greystone Hollow
Community Builders and Fernbank Housing Partners LP. The Authority is in
litigation with a former contractor, Tallis & Rook Construction Co. Proposers
must disclose any relationship with these organizations under Section 4.3.8.

2.0  TIMETABLE

2.1  The Authority expects to follow the timetable below.

         RFP issued                          December 9, 2026
         Pre-proposal conference (remote)    December 16, 2026 at 1:30 PM
         Deadline for questions              December 22, 2026 at 5:00 PM
         Responses to questions              January 6, 2027
         Deadline to submit proposals        January 20, 2027 at 3:00 PM
         Oral interviews                     February 3, 2027
         Board of Commissioners action       February 24, 2027

2.2  Attendance at the pre-proposal conference is encouraged but is not
required.

3.0  SERVICES

3.1  General counsel services.  Attend the monthly meeting of the Board of
Commissioners; advise on governance, open meetings, public records, ethics
and procurement; advise on public housing and voucher program rules, lease
enforcement, reasonable accommodation requests and fair housing; advise on
personnel matters; review contracts; supervise unlawful detainer counsel; and
manage litigation.

3.2  Development counsel services.  Represent the Authority and its
affiliates in the acquisition, financing, construction and conversion of
affordable housing, including tax credit partnerships, tax-exempt bonds,
ground leases, regulatory agreements, loan documents for public lenders, and
the repositioning of public housing.

3.3  The Authority spent about $410,000 on these services in its last fiscal
year. That figure is given for information only and is not a budget.

4.0  PROPOSAL REQUIREMENTS

4.1  Minimum qualifications.  The following are pass or fail.

4.1.1  The firm's proposed lead general counsel must have at least ten (10)
years of experience as a licensed attorney, and at least five of those years
advising housing authorities or other public agencies.

4.1.2  A firm proposing for development counsel must have closed, as counsel
to a housing authority or its affiliate, at least four tax credit financings
in the last six years.

4.1.3  The firm must be able to attend Board meetings in person in the City
of Dunmarrow.

4.1.4  The firm must not be debarred or suspended from contracting with any
public agency.

4.2  Organization.  Proposals must follow the order of Section 4.3 and use
its headings.

4.3  Narrative.

4.3.1  Letter of transmittal.  Identify the firm, state whether it proposes
for general counsel, development counsel or both, and name the person
authorized to commit the firm. An authorized person must sign the letter.

4.3.2  Firm background.  Describe the firm, its offices and number of
attorneys, and its practice representing housing authorities.

4.3.3  Key personnel.  Name the lead general counsel, the lead development
counsel and the other attorneys who would do the work. Describe the
qualifications of each and attach resumes.

4.3.4  General counsel experience.  Describe the firm's experience providing
the services in Section 3.1 to housing authorities or comparable agencies.

4.3.5  Development and finance experience.  Describe the firm's experience
with the services in Section 3.2. List the affordable housing transactions
closed in the last six years, giving for each the number of units, the
financing sources and the firm's role.

4.3.6  Resident services and relocation experience.  Describe the firm's
experience advising on relocation of residents during rehabilitation and on
resident services programs.

4.3.7  References.  Provide at least three references from housing
authorities or public agencies, with current contact details.

4.3.8  Conflicts of interest.  Disclose any relationship with the
organizations named in Section 1.4, with any Commissioner or employee of the
Authority, and with any developer, lender or investor active in Dunmarrow
County. Explain how the firm screens for conflicts in development work.

4.3.9  Fee schedule.  Provide hourly rates for each attorney and paralegal.
For development work, also propose a fixed fee per transaction for a typical
tax credit closing and say what it includes.

4.3.10  Staffing plan and availability.  Explain how the firm would cover
Board meetings, urgent requests and simultaneous transaction closings, and
describe the firm's plan to take over open matters from current counsel in
the first sixty days.

4.4  Litigation history.  Every proposal must also state whether the firm
has been sued for professional negligence in the past five years and how
each such claim was resolved.

5.0  FORMS

5.1  The forms listed here are part of the proposal and must be completed
and signed. A proposal without them is non-responsive.

         HA Form 1    Proposer Profile and Certification
         HA Form 2    Non-Collusive Affidavit
         HA Form 3    Certification Regarding Debarment and Suspension
         HA Form 4    Local Hiring Certification
         HA Form 5    Conflict of Interest Disclosure

5.2  A current certificate of professional liability insurance must also be
enclosed.

6.0  EVALUATION

6.1  An evaluation panel will rate proposals using these criteria and
maximum points.

         General counsel experience ......................... 25
         Development and finance experience ................. 25
         Key personnel ...................................... 20
         Fee schedule ....................................... 15
         Staffing plan and availability ..................... 10
         Resident services and relocation experience ........ 5

6.2  The panel may hold oral interviews with the highest rated firms and may
adjust its ratings afterwards. The Authority may award general counsel work
and development counsel work to different firms.

7.0  PROPOSAL FORMAT AND SUBMISSION

7.1  The narrative (Sections 4.3.1 through 4.3.10 and 4.4) shall not exceed
30 pages. Resumes, forms and the insurance certificate are not counted
against the limit. Text shall be in 12 point type with margins of one inch.

7.2  Submit one (1) original, four (4) copies and one (1) electronic copy on
a USB drive. Place them in a sealed envelope or box marked "RFP 27-003
Legal Services" with the firm's name and address.

7.3  Deliver proposals to: Procurement Officer, Housing Authority of the
County of Dunmarrow, 300 Tannersfield Road, Dunmarrow. Proposals may be
mailed or hand delivered. Emailed and faxed proposals will be rejected. The
Authority's clock at the front desk decides whether a proposal is on time.

8.0  INSURANCE

8.1  The firm selected shall maintain: (a) professional liability insurance
of not less than $2,000,000 per claim; (b) commercial general liability
insurance of not less than $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000
aggregate; (c) automobile liability insurance of not less than $500,000; and
(d) workers' compensation insurance as required by law.

9.0  GENERAL TERMS

9.1  The Authority reserves the right to reject any or all proposals, to
waive informalities and to negotiate terms with one or more proposers.

9.2  The contract term will be three years with two one-year renewal
options at the Authority's discretion.

9.3  Proposals are public records after the Board of Commissioners acts.

9.4  Protests must be received by the Executive Director within ten (10)
calendar days after the notice of award.
