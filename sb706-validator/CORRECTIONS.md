# Corrections to the research report (Tool 5)

Checked on 2026-11-25 against leginfo.legislature.ca.gov: the chaptered text of SB 706 (Stats. 2023, ch. 500) and the current code display for Pub. Cont. Code ch. 4.7, ch. 4.1, ch. 2.9, and § 7201.

## 1. Code sections for SB 706

- Report says: Pub. Cont. Code §§ 22170–22174
- Primary text: SB 706 (Stats. 2023, ch. 500) added chapter 4.7, §§ 22185–22185.11. Sections 22170–22174 are chapter 4.1, added by SB 991 (Stats. 2022, ch. 243), which covers water agencies only, allows 15 projects, and is repealed January 1, 2029.
- Effect: Every section cite in the report's Tool 5 entry points at the wrong chapter.

## 2. Ten-project cap

- Report says: § 22171
- Primary text: § 22185.1. Section 22171 is the 15-project cap for water agencies.
- Effect: Wrong section; the number 10 is right.

## 3. Skilled and trained workforce

- Report says: §§ 22172.5, 2600
- Primary text: § 22185.3(b), which points to chapter 2.9 (§ 2600 et seq.). Section 22172.5 is SB 991's reporting section.
- Effect: Wrong section. The report also leaves out the three project labor agreement exceptions in § 22185.3(b)(2).

## 4. Phase 2 off-ramp

- Report says: “GMP off-ramp IP clauses (PCC § 22173)”
- Primary text: The off-ramp is § 22185.5(c). It has no intellectual property clause. It says the replaced entity's design professionals keep sole liability for their errors and omissions if the agency uses their complete and stamped designs. Section 22173 is SB 991's savings clause.
- Effect: Wrong section and a clause the statute does not contain.

## 5. Subcontract threshold

- Report says: Competitive bidding for subcontracts over 0.5% of design-build contract value, § 22172(c)
- Primary text: § 22185.6(b): applies only when the contract value is $10,000,000 or more; the base is the contract price allocable to construction work; the process is public notice, qualification criteria, and a best value award, which may include prequalification or short-listing. Subcontractors listed in the statement of qualifications are excepted.
- Effect: Wrong section, missing the $10M trigger, wrong base, and “competitive bidding” overstates the required process.

## 6. Report to the Legislature

- Report says: § 22174
- Primary text: § 22185.8, due no later than December 31, 2028, with ten listed items. Section 22174 is SB 991's repeal clause.
- Effect: Wrong section; the report gives no due date.

## 7. Retention

- Report says: 5% cap under § 7201
- Primary text: Chapter 4.7 has its own rule in § 22185.7: 5% cap if payment and performance bonds are required in the solicitation, no retention on work to establish the guaranteed maximum price, and subcontract retention may not exceed the prime rate except after a bond notice. Section 7201 is the general 5% rule and has a substantially-complex-project exception.
- Effect: Right number, incomplete source; the no-retention rule for phase 1 is missing.

## 8. “Apprenticeship ratios (§ 2601)”

- Report says: DIR skilled-and-trained apprenticeship ratios
- Primary text: § 2601(d)(2) sets the share of skilled journeypersons who must be apprenticeship graduates: 60%, or 30% for fifteen listed trades, with teamster work exempt. It is tested monthly by headcount or by hours (§ 2601(d)(4)).
- Effect: Right section, loose description. These are graduate percentages, not apprentice-to-journeyperson ratios.

## 9. Scope

- Report says: “any public-works project exceeding $5,000,000”
- Primary text: Projects on state-owned or state-operated facilities are excluded (§§ 22185(d), 22185.9). “Special district” is not defined in the chapter.
- Effect: Overbroad. Whether a given transit or healthcare district qualifies needs a legal answer.

## 10. “RFQ best-value rubrics”

- Report says: Best value selection at the RFQ stage
- Primary text: The design-build entity is chosen by qualifications-based selection, where price is not the sole factor (§§ 22185(h), (i), 22185.3). Best value is the standard for subcontract awards (§ 22185.6(b)(3)) and for a replacement award after the off-ramp (§ 22185.5(c)(1)).
- Effect: Mislabels the selection standard.

## 11. Gov. Code § 1090 / Sahlolbei and CCP § 860

- Report says: Cited as conflict-of-interest disqualifiers and as the validation challenge route
- Primary text: Chapter 4.7 cites neither. Its own conflict rule is § 22185.2 (the agency must develop organizational conflict-of-interest guidelines).
- Effect: Not verified in this build. The engine flags the fact pattern for attorney review and applies no rule from those sources.

## 12. Effective dates

- Report says: Effective January 1, 2024 through January 1, 2030
- Primary text: Confirmed: effective January 1, 2024; repealed as of January 1, 2030 (§ 22185.11).
- Effect: Correct. The chapter does not say what happens to contracts in progress on the repeal date.
