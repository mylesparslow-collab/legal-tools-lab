OSTRANDER VALLEY RECREATION AND PARK DISTRICT (fictional)
REQUEST FOR QUALIFICATIONS: PROGRESSIVE DESIGN-BUILD SERVICES
AQUATIC CENTER AND FIELDHOUSE

1. Project description. The District seeks a design-build entity for a new
   aquatic center and fieldhouse of roughly 38,000 square feet on District land.
2. Evaluation. Statements of qualifications will be reviewed by a five-member
   panel. The methodology is described in Attachment B.
3. Evaluation factors. The District expects to consider team experience,
   technical design and construction expertise, and a preliminary cost estimate.
4. Final selection. The panel may interview some or all respondents before
   recommending a final selection to the Board.
5. Template. Respondents shall complete Attachment A, which asks for a listing
   of shareholders, a financial statement, licenses held, evidence of capacity
   to obtain payment and performance bonds, and organizational documents.
6. Labor. The District is party to a project labor agreement renewed in 2024.
7. Submittal. Statements are due at the District office by 2:00 p.m. on the
   date stated in the notice.
