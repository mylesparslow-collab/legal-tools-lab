AI tool adoption baseline

Period 2026-09-07 to 2026-12-06 (13 complete weeks, 2026-W37 to 2026-W49). Lawyers are associate, counsel, partner. Groups under 5 people are not shown.

Summary

  • Over the last 4 weeks (2026-W46 to 2026-W49), 43% of lawyers used an approved AI tool in a given week (337 of 777 lawyer-weeks).
  • In the latest week, 2026-W49, 87 of 193 lawyers were active (45%).
  • 77 of 203 lawyers (38%) are regular users: active in at least 3 of the last 4 weeks.
  • The lowest week was 2026-W44 at 18% (30 of 170).
  • Against the plan (programme month 0): month 6 target of 50%, not yet; month 12 target of 70%, not yet.
  • Pilot gate of 50% of pilot-practice lawyers active weekly: Disputes pass at 70% (90 of 128 lawyer-weeks); Employment not yet at 47% (37 of 78 lawyer-weeks); Competition cannot tell (fewer than 5 people in the group).
  • By practice, weekly use among lawyers runs from 17% in Real estate (16 of 94 lawyer-weeks) to 70% in Disputes (90 of 128). 2 practices are not shown because a group is too small to report.
  • By office, weekly use among lawyers runs from 13% in Lakeshore (8 of 60 lawyer-weeks) to 52% in Harbour Quay (78 of 150). 2 offices are not shown because a group is too small to report.
  • 97 of 195 Assistant A licences (50%) and 211 of 286 Assistant B licences (74%) had no use in the last 30 days.
  • Use is concentrated: the median active person logged 16 interactions in 4 weeks, and the top tenth of users (14 people) account for 55% of all interactions.
  • In the survey (160 responses), 63% of responding lawyers (71 of 112) said they used an approved tool in the last 7 days. Logs for the same week, 2026-W48, show 46% of all lawyers (89 of 195). The widest gap is in Real estate: 79% claimed (11 of 14) against 21% logged (5 of 24).
  • The most common limit on use reported in the survey is "I am not sure what is allowed" (53 of 160 responses).
  • 152 of 11215 export rows were dropped and 1625 were repaired. Each is itemised under data quality.

Definitions

Weekly active
At least one logged interaction with an approved tool in an ISO week (Monday to Sunday). The denominator is the roster, less people on leave for every weekday of that week, not yet started, or left. "Last 4 weeks" pools those weeks, so the unit is person-weeks.
Regular use
Active in at least 3 of the last 4 weeks, among people eligible in at least 3 of them.
Any use in period
At least one interaction in the whole period, among people on the roster at period end.
Per-tool and any-tool figures
Every figure for a named tool (any use, weekly active, regular use, unused licences, interactions, features) counts only people who hold that tool's licence. Figures for "any approved tool" count everyone in the population, so use by someone without a licence appears there and under data quality, and in no per-tool figure.
Interactions and features
Per person over the last 4 weeks, among people of any role on the roster at period end who had at least one interaction in those weeks.
Unused licence
A licence held at period end with no interaction with that tool in the last 30 days.
Survey gap
Share of survey respondents in a group who said they used an approved tool in the last 7 days, minus the logged weekly active rate for that group in the same week. Groups are compared; people are not matched.
Not shown
Any figure describing fewer than 5 people is withheld, and so is one further figure where a total would otherwise reveal it. This is a privacy choice. This report has no individual-level output.

Full definitions are in METRICS.md (version 1.0). Targets are the firm's own inputs. No comparison with other organisations is made. Personal portfolio demo; not legal advice.
