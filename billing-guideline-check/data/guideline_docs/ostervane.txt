Part A. How we expect matters to be staffed

We expect lean staffing, and we will pay for no more than 2 of your lawyers at any hearing, deposition or mediation. We will question any day on which one person bills more than 12 hours. Please obtain our approval before spending more than 5 hours on legal research for a matter.

Part B. What your invoices should look like

Record time in increments of 0.1 hour. Do not block bill: an entry exceeding 1.0 hour must describe a single task. We cannot pay for entries that are too generic to evaluate, for example "work on discovery" or "attention to matter". Each fee entry needs a task code and an activity code. If the same entry appears twice we will pay it once.

[Page 2]

Part C. Rates

Bill at the rates on the agreed rate card and no higher. We need 60 days written notice before any rate increase applies to our matters.

Part D. Things we do not pay for

We do not pay for clerical tasks or for time spent preparing, reviewing or correcting invoices. We do not reimburse overhead such as word processing, overtime, postage and subscription databases. Pass third-party costs through at cost, without mark-up.
