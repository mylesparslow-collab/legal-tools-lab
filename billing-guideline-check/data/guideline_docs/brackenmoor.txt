Article I. Compensation

Section 1.1 Not-to-exceed amount

Total compensation under this Agreement shall not exceed the not-to-exceed amount stated in the task order, and Counsel shall not be paid for any amount above it without a written amendment. Fees billed to any phase of the approved budget shall not exceed the amount allotted to that phase.

Section 1.2 Rates and personnel

Counsel shall bill only at the hourly rates listed in Exhibit B. Services performed by personnel not listed in Exhibit B shall not be compensated.

[Page 2]

Section 2.1 Time records

Time shall be recorded in increments of one-tenth of an hour. Any entry of more than 0.5 hour that combines two or more tasks shall be returned unpaid. Each time entry shall identify the phase of the approved budget by task code. Time submitted more than 90 days after the date of service shall not be paid. The Agency shall not pay twice for the same service, and duplicate entries shall be struck.

Section 2.2 Non-compensable time

Travel time shall not be compensated unless the General Counsel has approved the travel in writing in advance. Secretarial, clerical and file-management tasks are included in the hourly rates and shall not be billed separately.

[Page 3]

Article III. Reimbursable costs

Meals shall be reimbursed at no more than $45.00 per person per day and lodging at no more than $160.00 per night. Each cost item of $10.00 or more shall be supported by a receipt.
