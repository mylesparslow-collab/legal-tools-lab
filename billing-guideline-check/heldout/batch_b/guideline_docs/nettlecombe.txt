Thanks for asking before the first bill rather than after. Here is how we work.

Keep time in six-minute blocks, so 0.1 of an hour. Anything over 18 minutes (0.3) that covers more than one task comes back to you. Same for entries that don't say anything: "attention to matter", "draft correspondence" and the like.

Stick to the rates in the engagement letter. Double entries get paid once.

Eleven hours is the most I'll accept from one person in a day without a call first. No more than two of your people at any deposition, hearing or mediation. Lawyers talking to each other inside your firm: 0.3 max per entry. Ask me before legal research on a file goes past 5 hours.

Travel time gets billed at half your normal rate. On the road, meals are $75 a head per day and hotels $250 a night, tops.

Don't bill us for clerical work, conflict checks or training your own people. No overhead on the bill either: postage, word processing, phone charges, office supplies, online research fees. Outside vendor charges come through at cost.

Keep your receipts, because I may ask for them, but you don't need to attach them.

Try to get time to us within 60 days. I'm not going to bounce older time yet, but that's where we're headed.

Our fiscal year ends March 31, so please get February and March time in promptly.
