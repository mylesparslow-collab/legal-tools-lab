WHEREAS the Authority is a public body answerable for the prudent use of its funds, and WHEREAS Counsel has agreed to be bound by the following terms on every assignment, the parties agree as set out below.

(a) Ceiling. The Authority's obligation to pay Counsel on any assignment is limited to the ceiling amount fixed in the assignment order, and Counsel proceeds at its own risk as to any fees or costs that would carry total billings past that ceiling. Where an assignment order apportions the ceiling amount among stages, billings attributable to a stage shall be held within the sum apportioned to it.

(b) Persons and rates. Services shall be charged at the rates enumerated in the assignment order and not otherwise. Charges for the services of any individual not named in the assignment order will be refused.

(c) Recording of time. Time is to be reported in tenths of an hour (six-minute units). The Authority's auditors will inquire into the records of any timekeeper three-quarters or more of whose entries (eight entries being the minimum examined) are stated in whole hours. A time entry shall describe a single service, and the Authority declines to pay for composite entries in which two or more services share one time figure. Each time entry shall carry the task code of the stage of work it belongs to. The Authority places no fixed limit on the hours a timekeeper may work in a day.

(d) Timeliness and repetition. Charges presented later than sixty (60) days after the service was rendered are barred. No service shall be charged more than once, and any charge found to be repeated shall be deducted. Counsel shall submit its invoices within thirty (30) days of the close of each calendar quarter and shall retain its time records for four (4) years.

(e) Research and attendance. Counsel shall obtain the written concurrence of the Chief Legal Officer before devoting in excess of four (4) hours to legal research on an assignment. The Authority will compensate the attendance of not more than one (1) attorney at any deposition, hearing, mediation or arbitration session.

(f) Travel. Travel time, where the Chief Legal Officer has approved the travel in advance, is compensated at one-half of the timekeeper's rate; unapproved travel time is not compensated.

(g) Disbursements. Meals taken while travelling for the Authority are reimbursed to a limit of $65.00 per person per day, and overnight accommodation to a limit of $225.00 per night. A receipt shall accompany every disbursement exceeding $25.00. Expert and consultant fees above $5,000 require a purchase order before the expense is incurred.
