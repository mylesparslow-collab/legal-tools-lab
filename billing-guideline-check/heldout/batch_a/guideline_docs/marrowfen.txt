MARROWFEN SANITARY DISTRICT

Agreement for Special Counsel Services
Exhibit C: Compensation, Invoicing and Reimbursement Standards

ARTICLE 1. COMPENSATION LIMITS

1.01 The parties acknowledge that the District's Board of Directors has appropriated a fixed sum for each engagement, and accordingly the aggregate of all fees and costs invoiced on a matter shall at no time surpass the maximum compensation set out in the applicable engagement letter unless and until the Board has approved an increase by written amendment.

1.02 Where the engagement letter divides the maximum compensation among phases of work, Counsel shall not invoice against any phase a sum greater than the portion assigned to that phase, it being understood that savings in one phase do not carry over to another without the General Manager's written consent.

1.03 Hourly rates are those appearing in the fee schedule attached to the engagement letter, and no timekeeper shall be billed at a rate higher than the rate there shown for that timekeeper.

1.04 The District will not pay for the time of any person who does not appear by name in the fee schedule.

1.05 Absent the General Manager's prior written consent, not more than four (4) timekeepers shall record time to a single matter in any one billing month.

- 2 -

ARTICLE 2. TIME RECORDS

2.01 All time shall be kept contemporaneously and stated in units of one-tenth (0.1) of an hour.

2.02 Each entry shall be confined to one activity; an entry exceeding one and one-half (1.5) hours that aggregates distinct activities under a single time figure will be disallowed in its entirety, the District being unable to assess the reasonableness of the time devoted to each.

2.03 Every time entry shall bear the task code corresponding to the phase of work to which it relates.

2.04 Time submitted more than one hundred twenty (120) days after the date on which the work was performed is deemed waived.

2.05 Counsel shall review each invoice before submission to ensure that no service appears on it more than once; any repeated entry will be deducted.

2.06 The District does not impose a ceiling on the number of hours a timekeeper may record in a single day, but Counsel should expect questions where the hours recorded appear disproportionate to the task.

ARTICLE 3. TIME NOT COMPENSATED

3.01 Time spent in transit is compensable only where the General Counsel has authorized the trip in writing before it is taken; authorized transit time is paid at the timekeeper's ordinary rate.

3.02 The hourly rates are understood to absorb the cost of secretarial support, filing, photocopying, calendaring and similar administrative functions, none of which shall appear as a time charge.

- 3 -

ARTICLE 4. COSTS

4.01 Subsistence while away overnight on District business is reimbursable up to forty dollars ($40.00) per person per day for meals and one hundred fifty dollars ($150.00) per night for lodging.

4.02 Counsel shall furnish a receipt for every cost item in excess of $20.00.

4.03 Costs advanced to third parties on the District's behalf shall be invoiced in the amount actually paid, without surcharge or handling fee.

4.04 Invoices shall be rendered monthly and addressed to the General Manager, and shall be paid within forty-five (45) days of receipt.

4.05 Counsel shall maintain professional liability coverage of not less than $2,000,000 throughout the engagement.
