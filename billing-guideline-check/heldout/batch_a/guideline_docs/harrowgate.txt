HARROWGATE CASUALTY EXCHANGE
Panel Counsel Billing Requirements: Quick Reference Sheet (rev. 7)
Fictional document written for a software test

Sheet 1 of 2

ITEM                   REQUIREMENT
---------------------  ------------------------------------------------------------
Who may bill           Only people the claims examiner has cleared in writing for the claim.
Depositions etc.       One attorney paid per deposition, mediation, arbitration session or court hearing; a second needs the examiner's OK beforehand.
In-firm discussions    Capped at 0.2 hr per entry, per person.
Time units             Six-minute units (0.1 hr); no quarter hours.
Half-hour habits       A timekeeper with 80% or more of entries (6 entries minimum) landing on the half hour or the hour gets audited.
One entry, one task    Each entry covers a single task with its own time; lumped entries are cut.
Descriptions           Say what was done and to what; "attention to file", "follow up" and similar get zero.
Codes                  Task code and activity code on fee lines, expense code on cost lines; all mandatory.
Long days              Over 9.0 hrs by one person in one day is not paid without a written reason.
Stale time             Work more than 75 days old at invoice date is not paid.
Repeats                Same entry twice: the second one is struck.

Sheet 2 of 2

ITEM                   REQUIREMENT
---------------------  ------------------------------------------------------------
Rates                  The approved rate sheet for the claim is the ceiling.
Rate changes           45 days' written notice to the examiner before a new rate may be billed.
Not billable           Secretarial and file-room work; training and onboarding; conflicts clearance; preparing or fixing bills.
Legal research         Up to 2.5 hrs per claim without asking; beyond that, examiner pre-approval.
Travel                 Paid at half the approved rate.
Overhead               Not reimbursed: postage, word processing, local telephone, office supplies.
Receipts               Required for any single charge of $35.00 or more.
Food and lodging       Meals max $55.00 per person per day; hotel max $190.00 per night.
Third-party costs      At cost, no uplift.
Invoice frequency      Quarterly, or at $5,000 of unbilled time, whichever comes first.
In-house copies        No more than $0.10 a page.
Reports                Status report to the examiner every 90 days.
