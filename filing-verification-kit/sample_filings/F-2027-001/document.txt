OPPOSITION TO MOTION FOR SUMMARY ADJUDICATION

The Supreme Court has made the point directly: “A staffing agency that controls the work schedule shares responsibility for recording the start and end of each rest interval.” (Halvorsen v. Bright Harbor Staffing, Inc. (2024) 97 Cal.5th 210, 223.) The court added that “[w]here the employer's own records are silent, a factfinder may draw a reasonable inference from the pattern of omissions.” (Id. at 230.)
Automated records deserve no presumption of accuracy: “[C]ourts should not presume that a timekeeping system is accurate merely because it is automated . . . .” Quillfeather Mfg. Co. v. Dunmore, 977 F.4th 1180, 1187 (9th Cir. 2025).
