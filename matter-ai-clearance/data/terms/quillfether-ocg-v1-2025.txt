QUILLFETHER SYSTEMS (fictional)
Working with outside counsel
2025

How we work

We want short advice we can act on. Call before you write a long memo.

AI and automation

Quillfether encourages outside counsel to use AI tools where they reduce cost without reducing quality.

Counsel must disclose on each invoice which tasks were performed with AI assistance.

We expect efficiency gains from AI to be passed through to us in lower fees.

Counsel must not allow our source code or product roadmaps to be used for training any third-party model.

Human review of AI output is required in every case.

Our own engineers use AI coding assistants; counsel should assume that documents we send may contain AI-generated text.

Company employees are prohibited from sharing privileged advice with AI chatbots, and counsel should remind them of this when sending advice.

Invoices

Send invoices monthly. We pay in 45 days.
