DRENNICK FREIGHT LINES (fictional)
Guidelines for Outside Counsel
Revised June 2025

1. Budgets and staffing

A budget is required for every matter expected to exceed the threshold in Schedule A. Counsel must obtain prior written approval before retaining any expert, consultant or vendor.

2. Confidentiality

Counsel must not disclose Company information to any third party without the Company's consent. Counsel shall retain all matter files for seven years after the matter closes.

3. Invoices

Invoices must describe each task separately. The Company's e-billing system uses automated rules to reject entries that do not comply with these guidelines.

4. Reporting

Counsel must notify the Company promptly of any significant development, including any settlement demand.
