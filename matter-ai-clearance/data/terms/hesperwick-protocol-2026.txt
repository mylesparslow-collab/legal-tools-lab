HESPERWICK JOINT DEFENSE GROUP (fictional)
Protocol for shared work product
April 2026

1. Shared document set

Each member firm will keep the shared document set in the group workspace.

2. AI tools

2.1 Members of the joint defense group must not upload shared work product to any AI tool that is not approved by all members.

2.2 Each member will tell the others which AI tools it uses on the shared document set.

2.3 Summaries of privileged joint defense communications may not be generated by AI.

3. Costs

Shared costs are split equally.
