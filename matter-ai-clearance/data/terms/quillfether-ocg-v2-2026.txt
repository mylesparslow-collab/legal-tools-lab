AI and automation

Effective March 1, 2026, outside counsel must obtain written approval from the Quillfether Legal Operations team before using any generative AI tool on a new matter.

Approval will list the specific tools cleared for that matter; no other tool may be used.

Counsel must continue to identify AI-assisted tasks on each invoice.

Our confidential information must not be used to train or improve any model, and prompts containing it must be deleted within 14 days.

Tools hosted outside the United States or Canada are not acceptable.

A qualified lawyer remains accountable for all work product, whether or not AI was used.

Quillfether no longer encourages unsupervised use of AI tools for first drafts of court filings.

Send invoices monthly. We pay in 45 days.
