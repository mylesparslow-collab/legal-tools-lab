MARROWFINCH OUTFITTERS (fictional)
Outside Counsel Policy
March 2024

Section 5. Information security

5.1 Counsel must protect Company information with reasonable safeguards and report any security incident within 48 hours.

5.2 The use of generative AI tools, including public chatbots, on Company matters is prohibited.

5.3 Counsel must not enter Company documents, facts or strategy into any AI system.

5.4 This restriction does not apply to legal research databases that the Company has approved in writing.

Section 6. Billing

6.1 The Company pays for work that moves the matter forward.
