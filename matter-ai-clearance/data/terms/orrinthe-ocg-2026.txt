ORRINTHE BIOLOGICS (fictional)
Outside Counsel Guidelines
2026

7. Artificial intelligence

7.1 Orrinthe does not permit outside counsel to use generative artificial intelligence, in any form, on its matters.

7.2 This includes tools embedded in word processors, email and document management systems.

7.3 If counsel learns that AI was used on an Orrinthe matter contrary to this policy, counsel must report it to the Deputy General Counsel within two business days.

7.4 Orrinthe will revisit this position as the technology and its own policies mature.

7.5 Counsel's e-discovery vendors may continue to use technology-assisted review that Orrinthe approved before the date of these guidelines.

8. Travel

Travel time is billed at half rate.
