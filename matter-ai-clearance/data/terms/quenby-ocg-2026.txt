QUENBY VALE HEALTH SYSTEM (fictional)
Outside Counsel Guidelines
Effective January 1, 2026

1. Staffing

Matters must be staffed leanly. The Company's prior written consent is required before adding any timekeeper who is not listed in the engagement letter.

2. Billing

Invoices must be submitted monthly through the e-billing portal. The Company does not pay for summer associate time, internal conferences, or training of Firm personnel.

3. Use of artificial intelligence

3.1 The Firm must notify the Company's Legal Department in writing before using any generative artificial intelligence tool on a Company matter.

3.2 The Firm shall ensure that no Company information is used to train, fine-tune or otherwise improve any artificial intelligence model.

3.3 Only tools on the Firm's approved list, as shared with the Company at the start of each matter, may be used with Company information.

3.4 Company information entered into an AI tool must be stored and processed within the United States and deleted from the tool within thirty days after the task is complete.

3.5 A licensed attorney must review all AI output for accuracy before it is used in any work product delivered to the Company.

3.6 Patient information must not be entered into any AI tool unless the Company has confirmed in writing that the tool is covered by an appropriate agreement with the Firm.

3.7 The Company will review these guidelines each year and may issue further requirements for AI tools.

4. Media

The Firm may not speak to the press about any Company matter.
