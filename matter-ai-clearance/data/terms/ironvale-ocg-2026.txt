IRONVALE BRIDGE CAPITAL (fictional)
Outside Counsel Guidelines
February 2026

4. Technology

4.1 Outside counsel may use only those AI platforms listed in Appendix C (Approved Technology).

4.2 Use of any AI platform not on that list requires advance approval from Ironvale's Chief Legal Officer.

4.3 Deal documents and investor information placed in an AI platform must remain in data centers located in the United States.

4.4 AI vendors may keep prompts and outputs for no longer than seven days and solely for abuse monitoring.

4.5 No Ironvale data may be used to develop or improve any AI vendor's products.

4.6 Counsel should bill AI-assisted work at the rate of the timekeeper who reviewed it, not as a separate technology charge.

4.7 Ironvale's deal teams are not required to disclose their own use of AI to counsel.

4.8 Counsel is not required to seek approval for AI features built into the e-signature platform that Ironvale itself provides.

5. Staffing

Deal teams must be approved in advance.
