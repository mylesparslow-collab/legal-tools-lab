SABLEMERE WATER DISTRICT (fictional)
Requirements for special counsel
Adopted October 2025

A. General

Special counsel reports to the General Manager and attends Board meetings on request.

B. Artificial intelligence

The District discourages the use of artificial intelligence tools for legal work on District matters.

Counsel should exercise caution and professional judgment before relying on any AI-generated content.

The District may adopt a formal AI policy during the term of this agreement, and counsel agrees to comply with it once adopted.

Records created with AI tools may be public records and must be kept in accordance with the District's records retention schedule.

Counsel must tell the District's General Manager if AI tools are used to prepare any document presented to the Board.

C. Rates

Rates are fixed for the fiscal year.
