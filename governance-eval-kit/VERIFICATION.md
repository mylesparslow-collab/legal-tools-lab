# How the law in this kit was checked

- Every statute and regulation cited in the checklists, the deadline rules and the corpus answer keys is listed in `verified_authorities.json` (153 sections) with the date it was read and where.
- California sections were read on leginfo.legislature.ca.gov, federal regulations on ecfr.gov, and court holidays on courts.ca.gov/about/court-holidays, all on November 25, 2026.
- Tests fail if a checklist item, deadline rule or answer-key defect cites a section that is not in the register.
- The reading was done by an AI assistant, not an attorney. The register records that a section exists and says what the checklist item says. It is not an opinion on how a court would apply it, and it does not cover case law.
- Statutes change. Re-read the sections before relying on anything here after the date above.
