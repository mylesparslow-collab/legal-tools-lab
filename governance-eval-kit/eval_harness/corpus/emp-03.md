"CRO" means the Company's Chief Revenue Officer, who on the date of this Agreement is Anselm Trowbridge-Okafor.

"Good Reason" means, without Executive's written consent, (a) a reduction of her Base Salary by more than five percent (5%) or of her Target Variable Compensation by more than ten percent (10%), other than an across-the-board reduction that applies to all vice presidents; (b) a material reduction of her duties or a change in her title to one below vice president; (c) a requirement that she relocate her home office outside California or more than thirty (30) miles from Dunmore Shoals; or (d) a material breach of this Agreement by the Company; provided that Executive has given the Company written notice within thirty (30) days after the condition first arises, the Company has not cured it within thirty (30) days after the notice, and Executive resigns within thirty (30) days after the cure period.

"Termination Date" means the date on which Executive's employment with the Company ends.

"Western Region" means the States of California, Oregon, Washington, Nevada, Arizona, Idaho, Utah, Hawaii and Alaska, as the Company may reasonably realign the region at the start of a fiscal year under Section 3.3.

## Article 2. Position and Duties

2.1 **Position.** The Company will employ Executive as Vice President of Sales, Western Region, beginning on June 14, 2027 (the "Start Date"). Executive will report to the CRO.

2.2 **Responsibilities.** Executive will lead and manage the Company's sales activity in the Western Region, including (a) hiring, training and managing a team that is expected to consist of eight account executives, two regional sales managers and three solutions consultants; (b) building and maintaining an accurate pipeline and forecast in the Company's customer relationship management system; (c) personally leading the Company's pursuit of strategic accounts in the Western Region with expected ARR above $400,000; (d) negotiating customer orders within the Company's Deal Desk Policy and price book; (e) working with the Company's channel partners and system integrators in the Western Region; (f) representing the Company at trade shows and customer events; and (g) carrying out such other duties consistent with her position as the CRO assigns.

2.3 **Authority Limits.** Executive may not sign a customer agreement, grant a discount above the level permitted by the Deal Desk Policy, promise a product feature or delivery date that is not on the Company's published roadmap, or agree to non-standard payment, termination or liability terms, without the approval that the Company's Signature Authority Policy requires. Executive will not enter into any side letter or oral understanding with a customer.

2.4 **Work Location.** Executive will work from her home office in Dunmore Shoals, California. The Company has no office in California, and maintaining a home office suitable for confidential calls and video meetings is a requirement of the position. Executive is expected to travel within the Western Region about forty percent (40%) of the time and to travel to the Company's headquarters in Fenwick Hollow, North Carolina about four times each fiscal year for quarterly business reviews and the annual sales kickoff.

2.5 **Full-Time Service.** Executive will devote her full business time and attention to the Company during her employment. Executive may engage in charitable and community activities, manage her personal investments, and continue as an unpaid adviser to the Dunmore Shoals Women in Technology Mentoring Circle, so long as those activities do not interfere with her duties or involve the use of Protected Information.

2.6 **At-Will Employment.** Executive's employment with the Company is at will and may be ended by Executive or by the Company at any time, with or without Cause, and with or without notice. Only a written agreement signed by Executive and the Company's Chief Executive Officer, and approved by the Board, can change the at-will status of the employment.

2.7 **Executive's Assurances.** Executive confirms that she is not subject to any agreement that would prevent her from working for the Company, that she has returned all property and confidential information of her previous employers, and that she will not use or disclose any such information in her work for the Company. The Company does not want, and Executive will not bring, any customer list, price list, pipeline report or other confidential record of a previous employer.

**Part B. Calculation of the Quarterly Bonus.** The Quarterly Bonus for each fiscal quarter has two components.

1. New ARR component (eighty percent (80%) of the quarterly target, or $36,000). "New ARR" means ARR from orders signed by new customers, and increases in ARR from orders signed by existing customers, that are booked in the Western Region during the fiscal quarter under the Bookings Policy. Attainment is New ARR for the quarter divided by the quarterly Quota. The component pays as follows:

| Attainment of quarterly Quota | Payout as a percentage of the component target |
| --- | --- |
| Below 50% | 0% |
| 50% to 100% | Equal to the attainment percentage |
| Above 100% up to 125% | 100% plus 2 percentage points for each point of attainment above 100% |
| Above 125% | 150% plus 3 percentage points for each point of attainment above 125%, to a maximum payout of 300% |

2. Retention component (twenty percent (20%) of the quarterly target, or $9,000). The component pays one hundred percent (100%) if trailing-twelve-month net revenue retention for the Western Region, measured on the last day of the quarter, is at least 108%; fifty percent (50%) if it is at least 102% but less than 108%; and nothing if it is below 102%.

**Part C. When a Quarterly Bonus is Earned and Paid.**

1. A Quarterly Bonus is earned on the last day of the fiscal quarter, and only if Executive is employed by the Company on that day. If Executive's employment ends for any reason before the last day of a fiscal quarter, no Quarterly Bonus, and no pro rata part of one, is earned for that quarter.

2. The Company will calculate each Quarterly Bonus after it closes its books for the quarter and will pay it, with a written statement showing the calculation, no later than forty-five (45) days after the end of the quarter.

3. An order counts toward New ARR in the quarter in which it is signed by both parties and accepted by the Company's Deal Desk. If an order is cancelled under a contractual right before the Quarterly Bonus for the quarter is calculated, it does not count.

4. Executive may dispute a bonus statement by written notice to the CRO within sixty (60) days after receiving it, and the Company's Chief Financial Officer will review the dispute and respond in writing within thirty (30) days. This paragraph does not shorten any period that the law allows for bringing a claim.

**Part D. Bonus after Termination.** If the Termination Date falls after the last day of a fiscal quarter and before the Quarterly Bonus for that quarter has been paid, the Company will pay that earned Quarterly Bonus only after Executive has signed and delivered a general release of all claims against the Company in the form the Company provides, and no such Quarterly Bonus will be payable if Executive declines to sign the release.

**Part E. Home Office and Regional Travel.** Executive's Base Salary and Target Variable Compensation have been set at a level that takes into account the costs of a field-based role. Executive shall therefore bear, at her own cost and without reimbursement, all expenses of her home office (including internet service, mobile phone service, printing and office supplies) and all expenses of automobile travel within the Western Region, including mileage, fuel, tolls and parking.
