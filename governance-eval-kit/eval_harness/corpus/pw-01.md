# CONSTRUCTION AGREEMENT: HARLOW FLATS PUMP STATION REHABILITATION AND FORCE MAIN REPLACEMENT PROJECT

**District Contract No. THW-2026-014**

This Construction Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of February 10, 2026 (the "Effective Date") by and between the **Tarn Hollow Water and Sanitation District**, a California special district and local agency with its principal office at 2150 Quillfeather Road, Tarn Hollow, California (the "District"), and **Brindlecombe Constructors, Inc.**, a California corporation with its principal place of business at 88 Ostler Yard Lane, Vennick, California (the "Contractor"). The District and Contractor are each a "Party" and together the "Parties".

## RECITALS

A. The District is a special district formed under California law that owns and operates a wastewater collection and conveyance system serving approximately 41,000 residents in the unincorporated communities of Tarn Hollow, Lower Pellam and Harlow Flats. The District is a local agency and the awarding body for the work described in this Agreement.

B. The District's Harlow Flats Pump Station, located at 600 Sedgewater Lane, was placed in service in 1974 and has reached the end of its useful life. The 16-inch ductile iron force main that conveys flow from the pump station to the Coldharbour Interceptor has experienced four breaks since 2021.

C. On November 18, 2025 the District's Board of Directors adopted Resolution No. 2025-37 approving the plans and specifications for the Harlow Flats Pump Station Rehabilitation and Force Main Replacement Project (the "Project") and authorizing a call for sealed bids. The Project is a public work, is paid for in whole out of public funds of the District, and is subject to the public works provisions of the California Labor Code and the Public Contract Code.

D. Bids were publicly opened on January 8, 2026. Contractor submitted the lowest responsive bid in the amount of Fourteen Million Eight Hundred Sixty-Two Thousand Five Hundred Dollars ($14,862,500), and on January 27, 2026 the Board of Directors, by Resolution No. 2026-04, found Contractor to be the lowest responsive, responsible bidder and awarded the contract to Contractor.

E. Contractor holds a Class A General Engineering Contractor license issued by the Contractors State License Board (License No. 000417, expiring March 31, 2027) and is registered with the Department of Industrial Relations as a public works contractor (Registration No. PW-LR-0000417).

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants set out below, the Parties agree as follows.

## ARTICLE 2. SCOPE OF WORK

**2.1 General.** Contractor shall furnish all labor, materials, equipment, tools, transportation, supervision and services necessary to perform and complete the Work in a good and workmanlike manner and in strict accordance with the Contract Documents. A summary of the Work appears in Exhibit A.

**2.2 Principal Elements.** Without limiting the Plans and Technical Specifications, the Work includes:

(a) demolition of the existing dry-pit pumps, motor control center, standby generator and discharge header at the Pump Station;

(b) rehabilitation of the existing 22-foot-deep concrete wet well, including surface preparation and application of a polyurethane lining system;

(c) furnishing and installing three new 150-horsepower pumps, variable frequency drives, a new motor control center, a 500-kilowatt diesel standby generator with a sound-attenuated enclosure, and a supervisory control and data acquisition panel;

(d) installing approximately 7,140 linear feet of 18-inch Force Main by open-cut trenching at depths ranging from 6 feet to 18 feet below existing grade, including shoring, dewatering, bedding, backfill and pavement restoration;

(e) installing approximately 310 linear feet of 30-inch steel casing by jack-and-bore beneath the Pellam Slough crossing, with launching and receiving pits approximately 24 feet deep;

(f) constructing a new junction structure at the Coldharbour Interceptor;

(g) temporary bypass pumping sufficient to convey a peak wet weather flow of 4.2 million gallons per day throughout construction; and

(h) testing, startup, commissioning, operator training and site restoration.

**2.3 Contractor's Examination.** Contractor represents that before submitting its Bid it visited the Site, reviewed the Contract Documents, and reviewed the geotechnical data report prepared by Loamstead Geotechnical dated August 2025, which the District made available to bidders for information.

**2.4 Permits and Fees.** The District has obtained the encroachment permit from the County of Wrenmoor and the streambed alteration agreement for the Pellam Slough crossing. Contractor shall obtain and pay for all other permits and licenses required for the Work, including the excavation permit required by the Division of Occupational Safety and Health, and shall comply with the conditions of all permits.

**2.5 Standard of Performance.** Contractor shall perform the Work with the degree of skill and care ordinarily exercised by experienced general engineering contractors performing work of similar size and complexity in California. Contractor shall assign Orrin Tasker-Lowe as project manager and Dessa Wynfield as superintendent, and shall not replace either without the District Engineer's written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

**2.6 Continuous Operation.** The Pump Station is an essential public facility. Contractor shall plan and perform the Work so that wastewater service is maintained at all times, and shall not shut down, bypass or isolate any part of the District's system except in accordance with a shutdown plan approved in writing by the District Engineer at least ten (10) working days in advance.
