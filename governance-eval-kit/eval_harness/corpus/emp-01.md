**6.1 Accrued Obligations.** On any termination of Executive's employment the Company will pay or provide the Accrued Obligations. **6.2 Qualifying Termination outside a Change in Control Period.** If a Qualifying Termination occurs outside a Change in Control Period, then, subject to Section 6.5, the Company will provide Executive the following (together the "Severance Benefits"):

(a) continued payment of the Base Salary, at the rate in effect immediately before the Termination Date (ignoring any reduction that gives rise to Good Reason), for twelve (12) months after the Termination Date, in installments on the Company's regular payroll dates beginning on the first payroll date after the Release becomes effective, with the first installment to include any amount that would have been paid earlier but for the wait for the Release;

(b) a pro rata portion of the Annual Bonus for the Fiscal Year in which the Termination Date falls, based on actual performance for the full Fiscal Year and the number of days Executive was employed during it, paid when annual bonuses are paid to other senior executives;

(c) if Executive properly elects continuation coverage under COBRA, payment or reimbursement of the premiums for Executive and Executive's covered dependents, at the active employee rate, until the earliest of twelve (12) months after the Termination Date, the date Executive becomes eligible for group health coverage from another employer, and the date Executive is no longer eligible for COBRA coverage; and

(d) accelerated vesting of the portion of the Option and of any other time-based equity award that would have vested during the six (6) months after the Termination Date had Executive remained employed.

**6.3 Qualifying Termination during a Change in Control Period.** If a Qualifying Termination occurs during a Change in Control Period, then, subject to Section 6.5 and in place of the benefits in Section 6.2, the Company will provide Executive (a) a lump sum equal to eighteen (18) months of Base Salary plus one hundred fifty percent (150%) of the Target Bonus, paid within ten (10) business days after the Release becomes effective (or, if later, within ten (10) business days after the Change in Control); (b) the COBRA benefit described in Section 6.2(c) for up to eighteen (18) months; and (c) full accelerated vesting of the Option and of every other outstanding time-based equity award, with any performance-based award vesting at the greater of target and actual performance measured through the Change in Control.

**6.4 Death or Disability.** If Executive's employment ends because of death or Disability, the Company will, in addition to the Accrued Obligations, pay Executive or Executive's estate a pro rata Target Bonus for the Fiscal Year in which the Termination Date falls, within sixty (60) days after the Termination Date.

**6.5 Release Requirement.** Executive's right to receive any payment or benefit under this Article 6, including the Accrued Obligations described in clauses (i) and (ii) of Section 1.1(a), is conditioned on Executive signing the Release and the Release becoming effective and irrevocable within sixty (60) days after the Termination Date. **6.6 No Mitigation; No Offset.** Executive is not required to look for other employment or otherwise to mitigate the amounts payable under this Article 6, and, except as stated in Section 6.2(c), those amounts will not be reduced by what Executive earns from another employer.

**6.7 Other Terminations.** If Executive's employment ends for Cause, or by resignation without Good Reason, the Company's only obligation is to pay or provide the Accrued Obligations, and unvested equity awards will be forfeited under the terms of the Plan.

**6.8 Sole Remedy.** The payments and benefits in this Article 6 are in place of any severance under any other plan, policy or practice of the Company.

## ARTICLE 10. TAX MATTERS
