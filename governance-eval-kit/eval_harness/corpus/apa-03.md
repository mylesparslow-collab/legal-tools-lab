This PORTFOLIO ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "Agreement") is dated as of February 1, 2027 (the "Effective Date") and is made by and among the following parties.

The "Sellers":

- CEDARWICK NURSING OPERATIONS, LLC, a California limited liability company ("SNF Seller");
- CEDARWICK ARBORS OPERATIONS, LLC, a California limited liability company ("RCFE Seller"); and

The "Buyers":

- TALLGRASS MERIDIAN SNF OPCO, LLC, a California limited liability company ("SNF Buyer");

## RECITALS

WHEREAS, Property Seller owns two adjoining parcels of land in Orrin Flats, California, improved with a skilled nursing facility (the "SNF Parcel") and a residential care facility for the elderly (the "RCFE Parcel"), which share a central kitchen, an emergency generator, a storm drainage basin and a common driveway;

WHEREAS, SNF Seller leases the SNF Parcel from Property Seller and operates on it the 72-bed skilled nursing facility known as Cedarwick Commons Nursing and Rehabilitation (the "Skilled Nursing Facility") under a license issued by the California Department of Public Health ("CDPH"), and the Skilled Nursing Facility is certified for participation in Medicare and Medi-Cal;

WHEREAS, RCFE Seller leases the RCFE Parcel from Property Seller and operates on it the residential care facility for the elderly known as The Arbors at Cedarwick, comprising 64 apartments with a licensed capacity of 80 residents (the "Assisted Living Facility"), under a license issued by the California Department of Social Services ("CDSS");

WHEREAS, SNF Seller employs 104 persons at the Skilled Nursing Facility and RCFE Seller employs 58 persons at the Assisted Living Facility, and each has employed at least those numbers, within a variance of five persons, throughout the twelve (12) months before the Effective Date; certain dietary, maintenance and business office personnel serve both Facilities;

WHEREAS, the sale of the Assisted Living Facility will result in a new license being issued to RCFE Buyer, and the sale of the Skilled Nursing Facility is a change of ownership requiring the approval of CDPH; and

WHEREAS, Sellers desire to sell and Buyers desire to purchase both Facilities and both parcels as a single, indivisible transaction;

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE 2. THE TRANSACTION

**2.1 Purchase and Sale.** Subject to the terms of this Agreement, at the Closing:

(a) Property Seller shall sell and convey to Property Buyer fee title to the SNF Parcel and the RCFE Parcel, each as described in Exhibit A, together with all improvements, fixtures, building systems, easements and appurtenances (the "Real Property"), and the existing leases of the parcels to the Operating Sellers shall terminate at the Transfer Time without payment by any party;

(b) SNF Seller shall sell and assign to SNF Buyer all of its assets used in the Skilled Nursing Facility, other than Excluded Assets, including furniture and equipment, the rehabilitation gym equipment, supplies and inventory, resident and business records, the Contracts identified in Part 1 of Schedule 2.1 as assumed, the trade name "Cedarwick Commons Nursing and Rehabilitation," telephone numbers, web addresses, goodwill, resident trust funds, and, to the extent assignable, permits and the Provider Agreements (the "SNF Assets"); and

(c) RCFE Seller shall sell and assign to RCFE Buyer all of its assets used in the Assisted Living Facility, other than Excluded Assets, including furniture and equipment, supplies and inventory, the 24-passenger activity bus, resident and business records, the resident admission agreements, the Contracts identified in Part 2 of Schedule 2.1 as assumed, the trade name "The Arbors at Cedarwick," telephone numbers, web addresses, goodwill and, to the extent assignable, permits (the "RCFE Assets").

All assets shall be conveyed free of Liens other than, as to the Real Property, the Permitted Title Exceptions.

**2.2 Excluded Assets.** The following are not sold (the "Excluded Assets"): cash and deposit accounts (other than resident trust funds and resident deposits credited to Buyers); accounts receivable for services rendered before the Transfer Time; rights to rate adjustments, supplemental payments, cost report settlements and quality assurance fee credits for periods before the Transfer Time; the organizational records and tax returns of each Seller; insurance policies and their proceeds; the name "Halloran"; Contracts not assumed; proprietary policy manuals and software of Seller Parent identified on Schedule 2.2; and Sellers' rights under this Agreement.

**2.3 Assumed Liabilities.** Each Operating Buyer shall assume only (a) obligations first arising after the Transfer Time under the Contracts and resident agreements assigned to it; (b) obligations to residents for trust funds and deposits actually transferred or credited to it; and (c) obligations it expressly undertakes in Articles 8 and 9. Property Buyer shall assume the obligations of the owner under the Permitted Title Exceptions arising after the Closing.

**2.4 Excluded Liabilities.** Buyers do not assume, and Sellers shall pay and discharge, every other liability of any Seller, including: liabilities for resident care before the Transfer Time; overpayments, recoupments, penalties, citations and fines relating to periods before the Transfer Time (subject to Section 8.3 as to mechanics); wages, premiums, vacation, benefits and payroll taxes of Employees through the Closing Date; trade payables; taxes; indebtedness; and all liabilities under the leases between Property Seller and the Operating Sellers.

**2.5 Single Transaction.** Buyers are not obligated to purchase, and Sellers are not obligated to sell, less than all of the Real Property, the SNF Assets and the RCFE Assets, except as Sections 4.3 and 4.4 expressly provide.

## ARTICLE 3. PURCHASE PRICE

**3.1 Purchase Price.** The total purchase price is Thirty-One Million Two Hundred Fifty Thousand Dollars ($31,250,000) (the "Purchase Price"), allocated as follows: $12,100,000 to the SNF Parcel; $10,400,000 to the RCFE Parcel; $5,400,000 to the SNF Assets; and $3,350,000 to the RCFE Assets. A further allocation among asset classes for purposes of Section 1060 of the Code is set out in Schedule 3.1 and binds the parties for all tax reporting.

**3.2 Deposit.** Within three (3) Business Days after the Effective Date, Buyers shall deposit $450,000 with Escrow Holder. If Buyers do not terminate this Agreement under Section 5.1, Buyers shall deposit a further $450,000 with Escrow Holder within two (2) Business Days after the Inspection Period ends. The amounts deposited, with interest, are the "Deposit." The Deposit shall be credited to the Purchase Price at the Closing.

**3.3 Balance.** At the Closing, Buyers shall deposit with Escrow Holder the Purchase Price, less the Deposit, and adjusted for the prorations and credits in Section 3.5, by wire transfer.

**3.4 Indemnity Escrow.** At the Closing, Escrow Holder shall retain $1,500,000 of the Purchase Price (the "Indemnity Fund") and hold it for eighteen (18) months under the terms summarized in Exhibit D as security for Sellers' obligations under Article 10.

**3.5 Prorations.** Revenues and expenses of each Facility and of the Real Property shall be prorated as of the Transfer Time, with Sellers entitled to revenues and responsible for expenses attributable to the period before the Transfer Time and Buyers afterward. The proration shall cover real property taxes and assessments (including the Thistledown Parkway Landscape and Lighting District assessment), utilities, amounts under assumed Contracts, private-pay resident charges, the skilled nursing facility quality assurance fee, and prepaid license and permit fees to the extent Buyers receive the benefit. Buyers shall receive a credit for all resident security deposits, prepaid charges and, for the Assisted Living Facility, the refundable portion of any community fee. A preliminary settlement statement shall be agreed at the Closing and a final reconciliation completed within one hundred twenty (120) days afterward; any dispute over the reconciliation that the parties cannot resolve within thirty (30) days shall be decided by Pemberley Oakes Accountancy Corporation, whose decision is final and whose fee is shared equally.

## ARTICLE 4. CLOSING

**4.1 Closing.** The closing of the transactions (the "Closing") shall occur through escrow with Escrow Holder on the last Business Day of the calendar month in which the conditions in Section 4.2 are satisfied or waived, but not earlier than the Anticipated Closing Date and not later than the Outside Date (the "Closing Date"). Operational and financial responsibility for the Facilities passes at 12:00:01 a.m. on the first day of the month following the Closing Date (the "Transfer Time").

**4.2 Conditions.**

(a) Conditions for the benefit of Buyers. Buyers need not close unless: (i) each representation of Sellers is true in all material respects when made and at the Closing; (ii) Sellers have performed their covenants in all material respects; (iii) CDPH has approved the CHOW Application in writing and has issued, or confirmed in writing that it will issue effective as of the Transfer Time, a license to SNF Buyer; (iv) Escrow Holder is committed to issue owner's title insurance policies for the two parcels in the amounts allocated to them, subject only to Permitted Title Exceptions; (v) neither Facility is subject to a license revocation or suspension proceeding, a denial of payment for new admissions, an appointed temporary manager or receiver, or a pending termination of a Provider Agreement; (vi) the average daily census for the calendar month preceding the Closing is at least 61 at the Skilled Nursing Facility and at least 66 at the Assisted Living Facility; and (vii) no order prohibits the Closing.

(b) Conditions for the benefit of Sellers. Sellers need not close unless: (i) each representation of Buyers is true in all material respects when made and at the Closing; (ii) Buyers have performed their covenants in all material respects and delivered the Purchase Price; and (iii) no order prohibits the Closing.

**4.3 Outside Date Election.** The parties acknowledge that Sellers have contracted to redeploy the sale proceeds and cannot remain in operation indefinitely. If CDPH has not approved the CHOW Application by the Outside Date, SNF Seller may, at its election, deliver possession and operational control of the Skilled Nursing Facility to SNF Buyer on the Outside Date, and SNF Buyer shall accept such delivery and close the purchase of the SNF Assets, notwithstanding the absence of CDPH approval. SNF Seller shall give Buyers at least ten (10) Business Days' written notice of an election under this Section.

**4.4 Assisted Living Facility.** RCFE Buyer shall use diligent efforts to obtain its license before the Anticipated Closing Date. The transfer of the RCFE Assets and the RCFE Parcel shall occur at the Closing concurrently with the transfer of the SNF Assets, whether or not CDSS has then issued a license or provisional license to RCFE Buyer for the Assisted Living Facility. The parties shall cooperate after the Closing on any remaining licensing steps.

**4.5 Closing Documents.** At the Closing the parties shall deliver, as applicable: grant deeds for the two parcels; bills of sale; assignment and assumption agreements for assumed Contracts, resident agreements and intangible assets; vehicle titles; lease termination agreements for the two operating leases; a FIRPTA certificate and California withholding forms for each Seller; a certificate of release from the California Employment Development Department for each Operating Seller (or an agreed holdback of $50,000 per Operating Seller pending its issuance); officer's certificates as to the conditions in Section 4.2; evidence of authority; the certified resident census and trust fund accountings described in Article 8; and the escrow agreement for the Indemnity Fund. Escrow Holder shall close in accordance with the joint escrow instructions in Exhibit B.

**4.6 Costs.** Sellers shall pay county transfer tax, standard owner's title premiums and one-half of escrow fees. Buyers shall pay extended coverage and endorsement premiums, lender's title premiums, survey costs, recording fees, sales tax on tangible personal property (collected and remitted by the Operating Sellers, with receipts furnished to Buyers), regulatory application fees and one-half of escrow fees.

**7.7 Pre-Closing Advisory Services.** On the Effective Date SNF Seller and Advisor shall enter into the Pre-Closing Advisory Services Agreement, the terms of which are set out in Exhibit C.

## ARTICLE 8. OPERATIONS TRANSFER

**8.1 Resident Funds.** At the Transfer Time, SNF Seller shall transfer all resident trust funds to SNF Buyer, and RCFE Seller shall transfer all resident personal funds and refundable deposits to RCFE Buyer, each with a true and complete written accounting certified by the transferring Operating Seller. Each Operating Buyer shall acknowledge receipt in writing and hold the funds as required by Legal Requirements. The Operating Sellers remain liable for any shortfall or misapplication before the Transfer Time.

**8.2 Records.** Each Operating Seller shall leave at its Facility all resident, clinical and operating records. Each Operating Buyer shall preserve them for the required retention periods and give Sellers, their insurers and their auditors access and copies for cost reports, audits, claims and other proper purposes, in compliance with privacy laws.

**8.3 Provider Agreements; Recoupments.** As between the parties, SNF Seller bears all liabilities under the Provider Agreements for periods before the Transfer Time and SNF Buyer bears those for periods afterward, whatever position a Governmental Authority takes on successor liability. A party from which an amount is recouped that is the other's responsibility shall be reimbursed within fifteen (15) days after written demand with supporting remittance advices. SNF Seller shall timely file all cost reports for periods ending at or before the Transfer Time.

**8.4 Accounts Receivable.** Sellers retain their pre-Transfer Time receivables. Payments that identify dates of service are applied accordingly. Unidentified payments received in the first forty-five (45) days after the Transfer Time are applied to the oldest balance, and afterward to the most recent. Each party shall remit misdirected payments monthly with a report, and may inspect the other's collection records for one (1) year.

**8.5 Admission Practices.** To maintain consistent documentation during the period in which Sellers' tail insurance responds to claims, SNF Buyer shall, for twenty-four (24) months after the Transfer Time, continue the admission practices of the Skilled Nursing Facility described in Schedule 8.5 and shall not change them without the written consent of SNF Seller, which may not be unreasonably withheld.

**8.6 Shared Services.** From the Transfer Time the central kitchen, the generator and the drainage basin shall continue to be governed by the Campus Reciprocal Easement Agreement. The Operating Buyers shall settle between themselves the cost of shared dietary, maintenance and business office personnel.

**8.7 Transition Services.** For one hundred twenty (120) days after the Transfer Time, Sellers shall give Buyers read-only access to Sellers' billing and payroll systems for historical data relating to the Facilities, and shall make the regional controller available for up to twenty (20) hours a month, at no charge.

## ARTICLE 10. INDEMNIFICATION AND GUARANTIES

**10.1 Survival.** Representations survive for eighteen (18) months after the Closing Date, except that those in Sections 6.1(a), (c), (k) and (l) and 6.2(a) and (d) survive for the statute of limitations plus sixty (60) days and those in Sections 6.1(d), (e) and (f) survive for four (4) years.

**10.2 Sellers' Indemnity.** Sellers shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Buyers, their Affiliates and their respective owners, officers, employees and lenders from every Loss arising out of (a) a breach of Sellers' representations; (b) a breach of Sellers' covenants; (c) the Excluded Liabilities; or (d) the ownership or operation of the Real Property and the Facilities before the Transfer Time.

**10.3 Buyers' Indemnity.** Buyers shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Sellers, their Affiliates and their respective owners, officers and employees from every Loss arising out of (a) a breach of Buyers' representations; (b) a breach of Buyers' covenants; (c) the liabilities assumed under Section 2.3; or (d) the ownership or operation of the Real Property and the Facilities after the Transfer Time.

**10.4 Thresholds and Caps.** Sellers have no liability under Section 10.2(a) until Losses exceed $125,000 in total, and then from the first dollar, and Sellers' total liability under Section 10.2(a) is limited to $3,125,000. Neither limit applies to fraud, to the representations in Sections 6.1(a), (c), (k) and (l), or to Section 10.2(b), (c) or (d).

**10.5 Claims.** The party seeking indemnity shall give written notice with reasonable detail promptly after it becomes aware of a claim. The indemnifying party may assume the defense of a third-party claim within twenty (20) days after notice, with counsel reasonably acceptable to the other party, and may not settle on terms other than the payment of money with a full release without the indemnified party's consent. Matters before CDPH, CDSS or a payor that affect an Operating Buyer's license or certification shall be controlled by that Operating Buyer.

**10.6 Indemnity Fund; Exclusive Remedy.** Buyers shall recover first from the Indemnity Fund. After the Closing, indemnification under this Article is the exclusive monetary remedy for breach of this Agreement, except in the case of fraud.

**10.7 Seller Parent Guaranty.** Seller Parent unconditionally guarantees the payment and performance of Sellers' obligations under this Article, as a primary obligor, and waives notice of acceptance, presentment and any requirement that Buyers first proceed against Sellers or the Indemnity Fund. Seller Parent shall maintain a net worth of at least $5,000,000 until the third anniversary of the Closing Date.

**10.8 Buyer Parent Guaranty.** Buyer Parent unconditionally guarantees the payment of the Purchase Price at the Closing and the performance of Buyers' obligations under this Article.
