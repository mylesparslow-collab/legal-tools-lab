This Agreement for Construction Services ("Agreement") is dated September 22, 2026 and is between **SABLE RIDGE TRANSIT DISTRICT**, a transit district and local public agency organized under the laws of the State of California ("District"), with administrative offices at 900 Lanternworks Boulevard, Pell Harbor, California, and **KESTREL YARD CONSTRUCTION GROUP, INC.**, a California corporation ("Contractor"), with offices at 2255 Gantry Street, Morrowfield, California.

## RECITALS

WHEREAS, the District is a special district and local agency that provides fixed-route bus and paratransit service in Pell Harbor, Morrowfield and the unincorporated Sable Ridge area, and operates a fleet of 164 buses from two operating divisions;

WHEREAS, the District's Division 2 Operations and Maintenance Facility at 4400 Tannery Loop Road, Pell Harbor (the "Facility") was built in 1988, and its vehicle lifts, fueling system, electrical service and bus wash no longer meet the needs of the fleet or of the District's plan to place battery-electric buses in service beginning in 2028;

WHEREAS, the District's Board of Directors approved the Division 2 Bus Maintenance Facility Modernization and Zero-Emission Readiness Upgrade (the "Project") in its fiscal year 2026-27 capital budget, to be paid for entirely from the District's Measure T local transportation sales tax revenues and state transit capital funds, and no federal funds are programmed for the Project;

WHEREAS, the District issued Invitation for Bids No. 26-09 on June 15, 2026, opened sealed bids on August 6, 2026, and on September 9, 2026, by Board Resolution No. 2026-58, awarded the contract for the Project to Contractor as the responsible bidder submitting the lowest responsive bid, in the amount of Twenty-Two Million Seven Hundred Forty Thousand Dollars ($22,740,000); and

WHEREAS, Contractor is licensed by the Contractors State License Board as a Class A General Engineering and Class B General Building Contractor (License No. 000933) and is registered with the Department of Industrial Relations to perform public work (Registration No. PW-LR-0000933);

NOW, THEREFORE, the District and Contractor agree as follows.

## SECTION 2. SCOPE OF THE WORK

**2.01 General.** Contractor shall furnish everything necessary to construct and complete the Project in accordance with the Contract Documents.

**2.02 Elements of the Work.** The Work includes the following principal elements, described more fully in the Specifications and summarized in Exhibit A:

(a) removal of six existing in-ground hydraulic lifts in maintenance bays 3 through 8 and installation of six new heavy-duty in-ground lifts, including demolition of slab and excavation of lift pits to a depth of approximately nine (9) feet;

(b) closure and removal of two 20,000-gallon underground diesel storage tanks and the fuel island, under a closure permit from the Halewood County Environmental Health Division, with excavation to approximately fourteen (14) feet;

(c) a new 4,000-ampere, 480-volt electrical service, switchgear and underground duct banks to serve forty (40) future battery-electric bus charging positions, including installation of the first twelve (12) overhead pantograph dispensers and six (6) 180-kilowatt charging cabinets;

(d) replacement of the drive-through bus wash and water reclamation system;

(e) a new clean-agent and water-mist fire suppression system in the maintenance bays, and upgraded gas detection and ventilation;

(f) replacement of the maintenance building roof, skylights and four make-up air units;

(g) renovation of the parts storeroom, tire shop and technician locker rooms; and

(h) yard paving repairs, restriping and replacement of the vehicle gate and access control system.

**2.03 Operating Facility.** The Facility dispatches approximately 118 buses each weekday and operates twenty-four hours a day. Contractor shall perform the Work in the phases set out in Exhibit A so that at least four maintenance bays, one fueling position (until the temporary fueling plan takes effect) and all yard circulation lanes remain available to the District at all times. Contractor shall coordinate daily with the District's Division 2 Maintenance Superintendent, Yusra Feldspar, and shall attend a weekly coordination meeting.

**2.04 Site Investigation.** Contractor acknowledges that it had the opportunity to inspect the Facility before bidding and to review the record drawings, the geotechnical report by Stonechat Geosciences dated February 2026 and the tank closure work plan. Contractor's rights under Section 7 with respect to conditions that differ from that information are not affected by this acknowledgment.

**2.05 Permits.** The District has paid for and obtained the building permit from the City of Pell Harbor and the tank closure permit. Contractor shall obtain all other permits and approvals, including air district permits for the temporary generator, fire department permits for the suppression system, and the utility's approval of the service interconnection.
