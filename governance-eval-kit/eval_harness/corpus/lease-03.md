# Industrial Lease (Net): Kestrel Flats Logistics Park, Building 3, Unit B

_Fictional document for software testing. All parties, people, addresses and facts are invented. Not legal advice and not a form._

This Industrial Lease ("Lease") is made as of December 14, 2026 ("Execution Date") between Osprey Gantry Industrial Fund II, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company qualified to do business in California ("Lessor"), and Second Table Furniture Bank, a California nonprofit public benefit corporation ("Lessee").

## Preliminary Statements

1. Lessor owns the industrial park known as Kestrel Flats Logistics Park in Port Marisco, Almendra County, California, which consists of four concrete tilt-up warehouse buildings with a total of 412,000 square feet (the "Park").

2. Lessee collects donated household furniture, stores and refurbishes it, and distributes it without charge to families leaving shelters and transitional housing. Lessee needs warehouse space with dock-high loading and a small office.

3. Lessee has delivered to Lessor, before signing this Lease, a written notice and self-attestation that Lessee is a nonprofit organization with fewer than twenty (20) employees and is therefore a qualified commercial tenant. Lessor acknowledges that it has received the notice, and the parties agree that Lessee is a "qualified commercial tenant" for all purposes of this Lease.

4. Before Lessee signed this Lease, Lessor gave Lessee a written notice that Lessee may inspect any supporting documentation for the building operating costs charged under this Lease. Lessee acknowledges that it has received that notice.

5. The obligations of Lessee are guaranteed by Wren Hollow Community Foundation, a California nonprofit public benefit corporation that is Lessee's principal funder ("Guarantor"), under the Guaranty attached as Schedule 5.

The parties agree as follows.

## 2. Lease of Premises

2.1 Grant. Lessor leases the Premises to Lessee, and Lessee leases them from Lessor, for the Lease Term. "Lease Term" means the Initial Term and, if Lessee exercises the Extension Option, the Extension Term. The square footages in Section 1 are agreed figures and are not subject to remeasurement.

2.2 Shared Areas. Lessee may use, along with other occupants of the Park, the drive aisles, truck courts, car parking lots, landscaped areas, fire lanes, storm water basins and shared utility facilities of the Park (the "Shared Areas"), in accordance with the Park Rules. Lessor may change the Shared Areas if the change does not materially reduce truck access to Lessee's dock doors or reduce the number of trailer stalls and car stalls available to Lessee under Sections 1.11 and 1.12.

2.3 Delivery Condition. Lessor shall deliver the Premises on the Start Date vacant, broom clean, with the roof watertight and with the dock doors, dock levelers, warehouse lighting, electrical service, plumbing, fire sprinkler system and office heating and air conditioning units in good working order. Lessor shall, before the Start Date and at its cost, (a) repaint the office, (b) replace the office carpet with Park-standard carpet tile, and (c) replace the warehouse lamps that are burned out. If Lessee notifies Lessor within sixty (60) days after the Start Date that an item was not in the condition this Section requires, Lessor shall correct it at Lessor's cost and shall not charge the cost as an Operating Expense.

2.4 No Other Warranty. Lessee has inspected the Premises. Apart from Section 2.3 and Lessor's continuing obligations under Section 8.1, Lessee accepts the Premises in their existing condition, and Lessor makes no promise that the Premises are suitable for Lessee's operations.

2.5 Early Occupancy. Lessee may enter the Premises from February 15, 2027 to install racking and move in inventory. During early occupancy Lessee is bound by this Lease but owes no Base Rent or Operating Expense Charge. Lessee shall pay for its utilities from the date it enters.

## 3. Term, Month-to-Month Tenancy and Extension

3.1 Initial Term. The Initial Term starts on the Start Date and ends on the End Date. If Lessor cannot deliver possession on the Start Date, this Lease is not void, Lessor is not liable for damages, Rent does not start until delivery, and the End Date does not change. If Lessor has not delivered possession by April 15, 2027, Lessee may cancel this Lease by written notice before delivery and recover all money it has paid.

3.2 Month-to-Month Tenancy After the Lease Term. If Lessee stays in possession after the Lease Term ends and Lessor accepts Rent for any period after that date, Lessee's tenancy shall be from month to month on the terms of this Lease that can apply to a monthly tenancy, at the Base Rent that applied in the last month of the Lease Term. Lessor or Lessee may end the monthly tenancy by written notice given at least thirty (30) days before the termination date. Lessor may increase the Base Rent for the month-to-month tenancy by twenty percent (20%) over the Base Rent then being paid, and may do so more than once in any year, by giving Lessee thirty (30) days' written notice before each increase takes effect.

3.3 Extension Option.

(b) Lessee's notice is effective only if, on the date it is given, no Default exists and Lessee has not been more than ten (10) days late in paying Base Rent more than twice in the preceding twelve (12) months.

3.4 Holding Over Against Lessor's Wishes. If Lessee stays in possession after the Lease Term ends, or after a monthly tenancy has been ended by notice, and Lessor has not accepted Rent for the period and has told Lessee in writing that it objects, then Lessee is a tenant at sufferance. For each day of that occupancy Lessee shall pay one-thirtieth of one hundred fifty percent (150%) of the monthly Base Rent last in effect, together with the Operating Expense Charge, and Lessee shall be liable for Lessor's actual damages from the delay in recovering the Premises.

## 4. Rent

4.1 Base Rent. Lessee shall pay Base Rent monthly in advance, on or before the first day of each month, to the account or address that Lessor specifies. Lessee shall pay Base Rent for March 2027 when it signs this Lease.

4.2 Operating Expense Charge. This is a net lease. In addition to Base Rent, Lessee shall pay its share of Operating Expenses as Section 5 and Schedule 2 provide (the "Operating Expense Charge").

4.3 Rent Defined. "Rent" means Base Rent, the Operating Expense Charge and every other amount that Lessee is required to pay Lessor under this Lease. Rent is payable without prior demand and without deduction or offset, except where this Lease says otherwise.

4.4 Late Charge and Interest. If Lessor has not received an installment of Rent within five (5) days after it is due, Lessee shall pay a single late charge of five percent (5%) of the late installment, which the parties agree is a reasonable estimate of the processing and other costs Lessor will incur. Unpaid Rent also bears interest at ten percent (10%) per year, or the maximum rate the law allows if lower (the "Default Rate"), from the thirtieth (30th) day after the due date until it is paid.

4.5 Rent Tax. Lessee shall pay any city or county business license or rental tax measured by the Rent it pays, if the tax is by its terms payable by tenants.

## 5. Operating Expenses

5.1 Payment of Estimates. For each calendar year, Lessor shall estimate the Operating Expense Charge and Lessee shall pay one-twelfth of the estimate on the first day of each month with Base Rent. The estimate for 2027 is stated in Section 1.7. Lessor shall deliver each later estimate, with a budget by line item, at least thirty (30) days before the year begins.

5.2 What Is Included. "Operating Expenses" are the costs described in Part A of Schedule 2, less the exclusions in Part B of Schedule 2.

5.3 Allocation. Lessor shall allocate Operating Expenses to Lessee in proportion to floor area, as follows: Lessee's Building Share of Operating Expenses that relate only to the Building (such as the Building's roof, fire sprinkler system and property insurance), and Lessee's Park Share of Operating Expenses that relate to the Park as a whole (such as landscaping, storm water facilities, Park lighting and real property taxes assessed on the Park as a single parcel). The only exception is the cost of maintaining the shared truck courts, which is allocated under Part C of Schedule 2.

5.4 Year-End Statement. Within ninety (90) days after each calendar year ends, Lessor shall give Lessee a statement of actual Operating Expenses for the year by line item, the allocation to Lessee, and the amount Lessee has paid. Lessee shall pay a shortfall within thirty (30) days. Lessor shall apply an overpayment to the next Rent due, or pay it to Lessee within thirty (30) days if the Lease Term has ended.

5.5 Lessee's Right to Documents. Lessor shall provide Lessee, within thirty (30) days after Lessee's written request, with supporting documentation for the Operating Expenses charged to Lessee, including dated and itemized quotes, contracts, receipts and invoices from the providers of the goods or services, and the tax bills and insurance premium notices. Lessor shall not charge for this. If the documentation does not support an amount that Lessee has paid, Lessor shall refund that amount within thirty (30) days.

5.6 Real Property Taxes. Real property taxes include general and special taxes and assessments on the Park, and any increase that follows a sale or new construction. They do not include Lessor's income or franchise taxes, or penalties for Lessor's late payment. Lessor shall pay assessments that can be paid in installments over the longest period allowed, and only the installments that fall due in the Lease Term are included.

5.7 Lessee's Property Taxes. Lessee shall pay when due any taxes assessed on its equipment, racking, vehicles and other personal property. Lessee may apply for any welfare or other exemption available to it as a nonprofit organization, and Lessor shall sign the forms that the Almendra County Assessor reasonably requires for that purpose. Any reduction in real property taxes that results from Lessee's exemption shall be credited to Lessee alone.

## 6. Security Deposit

6.1 Amount and Purpose. Lessee shall pay the Security Deposit to Lessor when it signs this Lease. The Security Deposit secures Lessee's performance of this Lease. Lessor may apply it, to the extent reasonably necessary, to cure Lessee's failure to pay Rent, to repair damage to the Premises that Lessee caused, or to clean the Premises when the tenancy ends. Lessor may commingle the Security Deposit with other funds and owes no interest on it.

6.2 Replenishment. If Lessor applies part of the Security Deposit while this Lease is in effect, Lessee shall pay Lessor the amount applied within fifteen (15) days after written request.

6.3 Refund. Lessor shall refund the unapplied balance of the Security Deposit to Lessee within thirty (30) days after the later of (a) the date Lessee surrenders possession of the Premises to Lessor and (b) the date on which Lessor has completed all repair, restoration and removal work that Schedule 4 required Lessee to perform and Lessee did not perform. Lessor shall send with the refund an itemized list of the amounts it applied.

6.4 Transfer. If Lessor conveys the Park, it shall transfer the Security Deposit to the new owner and tell Lessee in writing that it has done so and the name and address of the new owner. Lessor then has no further responsibility for the Security Deposit.

## 7. Use of Premises

7.1 Permitted Use. Lessee shall use the Premises only for the Permitted Use. Retail sales to the public from the Premises are not allowed, except for up to four (4) benefit sale days in each calendar year on dates that Lessee gives Lessor at least twenty (20) days in advance, and for which Lessee obtains any permit the City of Port Marisco requires.

7.2 Legal Requirements. "Legal Requirements" means all laws, ordinances, building and fire codes, air and water quality rules, permits and recorded restrictions that apply to the Park or to Lessee's operations. Lessee shall comply with Legal Requirements that apply because of its particular use of the Premises, its racking and storage configuration, or work that it does in the Premises. Lessee shall obtain any high-pile storage permit that the Port Marisco Fire Protection Bureau requires for its racking. Lessor shall comply with Legal Requirements that apply to the structure of the Building and the Shared Areas regardless of the identity of the occupant.

7.3 Access for Persons with Disabilities. The Premises have not undergone inspection by a Certified Access Specialist. Lessor shall make, at its cost, any accessibility improvement to the Shared Areas, the accessible car stalls, and the path of travel to the office entrance of the Premises that a public authority orders during the Lease Term, unless the order results from an alteration by Lessee or from Lessee's opening the Premises to the public on benefit sale days, in which case Lessee shall pay for the improvement. Lessee shall make, at its cost, any accessibility improvement inside the Premises that is required because of its alterations, its volunteer program or its employment of any person.

7.4 Floor Load and Racking. Lessee shall not place a load on the warehouse slab greater than five hundred (500) pounds per square foot. Racking shall be installed by a licensed contractor, anchored as its manufacturer specifies, and kept at least eighteen (18) inches below the sprinkler deflectors, or such greater distance as the fire code requires for the goods stored.

7.5 Outside Storage. Lessee shall not store furniture, pallets, donation bins or refuse outside the Building, except in closed trailers parked in Lessee's trailer stalls and in the enclosed dumpster area assigned to Unit B.

7.6 Refinishing Work. Lessee may strip, sand, stain and paint furniture in the Premises only in the area marked "Workshop" on Schedule 1, with dust collection and ventilation that meet Legal Requirements. Lessee shall keep finishes, solvents and adhesives in listed flammable-storage cabinets, in quantities no greater than the fire code allows for the Building's occupancy class without a hazardous occupancy permit.

7.7 Hazardous Substances.

(a) "Hazardous Substance" means any substance, material or waste that a Legal Requirement regulates because it is toxic, flammable, corrosive or otherwise hazardous to health or the environment.

(b) Lessee shall not release any Hazardous Substance in the Park, and shall not bring any Hazardous Substance to the Premises other than the workshop materials that Section 7.6 allows, forklift batteries and propane cylinders, and ordinary cleaning products, each handled and disposed of as Legal Requirements direct. Lessee shall not accept donated appliances that contain refrigerants, or donated paint, pesticides or pool chemicals.

(c) Lessee shall promptly clean up, to the standard that the agency with jurisdiction requires, any release of a Hazardous Substance caused by Lessee or its employees, volunteers, donors, contractors or visitors, and shall indemnify and defend Lessor against all resulting claims, penalties and costs.

(d) Lessor shall indemnify and defend Lessee against all claims, penalties and costs that result from Hazardous Substances that were in, on or under the Premises before Lessee first entered, or that migrate to the Premises from elsewhere and that Lessee and its invitees did not release. Lessee has no duty to investigate or clean up those substances.

(e) Lessor has given Lessee a copy of the Phase I environmental site assessment of the Park prepared by Tidewrack Environmental Consultants dated May 20, 2025. This Section 7.7 survives the end of the Lease Term.

7.8 Rules. Lessee shall follow the Park Rules in Schedule 3. Lessor may amend the Park Rules on thirty (30) days' written notice if the amendment is reasonable, applies to all occupants and does not take away a right that this Lease gives Lessee.

## 8. Maintenance

8.1 Lessor's Work. Lessor shall keep in good condition and repair the roof structure and membrane, the foundation, the floor slab (other than damage from overloading or from Lessee's equipment), the load-bearing and exterior walls, the main fire sprinkler risers and monitoring system, the utility lines up to the point where they enter the Premises, and the Shared Areas. The cost of this work is an Operating Expense to the extent Schedule 2 allows, except that Lessor shall bear, and not charge to Lessee, the cost of structural repairs to the roof structure, foundation and load-bearing walls.

8.2 Lessee's Work. Lessee shall keep the rest of the Premises in good condition and repair, at its cost, including the dock doors, dock levelers, bumpers and seals, the grade-level door, interior walls, the office finishes, restrooms, lighting, electrical distribution within the Premises, plumbing within the Premises, the branch sprinkler lines that Lessee alters, and the two office heating and air conditioning units. Lessee shall have the dock equipment serviced twice a year and the heating and air conditioning units serviced four times a year by licensed contractors, and shall send Lessor the service reports on request.

8.3 Major Replacements. If a heating and air conditioning unit or a dock leveler cannot be repaired at a cost less than half the cost of replacing it, Lessor shall replace it. Lessee shall then pay Lessor each month, for the rest of the Lease Term, an amount equal to the replacement cost divided by the number of months in the useful life of the item, with interest on the unamortized cost at seven percent (7%) per year. This Section does not apply if the replacement is needed because Lessee did not carry out the servicing that Section 8.2 requires, in which case Lessee pays the full cost.

8.4 Lessor May Perform. If Lessee does not carry out maintenance or a repair that this Lease requires within thirty (30) days after written notice (or at once, in an emergency that threatens the Building or safety), Lessor may do the work, and Lessee shall pay the reasonable cost plus eight percent (8%) for supervision within thirty (30) days after it receives an invoice and supporting documents.

8.5 Statutory Rights Waived. Lessee waives its rights under Civil Code sections 1932(1), 1941 and 1942 to make repairs at Lessor's expense or to end this Lease because repairs have not been made.

## 9. Utilities and Services

Lessee shall arrange and pay for electricity, gas, water, sewer, refuse collection, telephone, internet and alarm monitoring for the Premises, in its own name wherever the provider permits. Water for the Building is on one meter, and Lessor shall bill Lessee for water according to a submeter reading at the rate that the Port Marisco Water Utility charges Lessor. Lessor does not provide security for the Premises. An interruption in any utility is not an eviction and does not reduce Rent, unless Lessor or its contractor negligently caused it and it continues for more than three (3) business days after Lessee notifies Lessor, in which case Rent abates from the fourth business day until service resumes to the extent that Lessee cannot use the Premises.

## 10. Alterations

10.1 Approval. Lessee shall obtain Lessor's written approval before making any change to the Premises, other than installing racking that complies with Section 7.4 and making non-structural interior changes that cost less than $25,000 in a calendar year, do not need a building permit and do not touch the roof, the slab, the sprinkler system or the exterior. Lessor shall not unreasonably withhold or delay approval of non-structural changes.

10.2 How Work Is Done. Lessee shall give Lessor plans for work that needs approval, use licensed and insured contractors, obtain permits, and give Lessor at least ten (10) days' notice before work begins so Lessor can post notices of non-responsibility. Roof penetrations may be made only by Lessor's roofing contractor, at Lessee's cost. Lessee shall not core or cut the slab without approval.

10.3 Liens. Lessee shall pay for all work done for it and shall discharge or bond over any mechanics' lien within twenty (20) days after it learns of the lien.

10.4 Solar and Rooftop Equipment. Lessor reserves the roof for its own solar energy system and other equipment. Lessee may not place equipment on the roof. Any solar installation by Lessor shall not interfere with Lessee's use of the Premises more than is reasonably necessary during construction.

## 11. Insurance, Waiver and Indemnity

11.1 Insurance Lessee Must Carry. From the date it first enters the Premises, Lessee shall carry:

(a) commercial general liability insurance of at least $2,000,000 per occurrence and $4,000,000 in the aggregate, which may be provided partly by an umbrella policy, covering the Premises, the benefit sale days and Lessee's volunteers;

(b) automobile liability insurance of at least $1,000,000 combined single limit covering Lessee's box trucks and hired and non-owned vehicles;

(c) workers' compensation insurance as the law requires, and volunteer accident coverage;

(d) property insurance covering its racking, equipment, tools, vehicles and donated inventory for their replacement cost; and

(e) pollution legal liability insurance of at least $1,000,000 per incident covering the workshop operations.

11.2 Terms of Lessee's Policies. Lessee's liability policies shall name Lessor, its property manager and its lender as additional insureds and shall be primary and non-contributing. All policies shall be written by insurers authorized in California with a financial strength rating of A- VII or better. Lessee shall give Lessor certificates and the additional insured endorsements before it enters the Premises and before each renewal.

11.3 Insurance Lessor Must Carry. Lessor shall insure the Building for its full replacement cost under a special form policy, and shall carry commercial general liability insurance for the Shared Areas and loss of rents insurance for twelve (12) months. Lessor may carry earthquake, flood and environmental coverage. The premiums are Operating Expenses.

11.4 Release and Waiver of Subrogation. Lessor and Lessee each release the other from liability for damage to the releasing party's property that is caused by a peril covered by the property insurance this Lease requires the releasing party to carry, even if the other party's negligence caused it. Each shall have its property insurer waive its right of subrogation.

11.5 Lessee's Indemnity. Lessee shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Lessor and its members, managers, property manager and lender from and against claims, losses and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising from (a) injury or damage occurring in the Premises or on the dock apron behind them, (b) the acts or omissions of Lessee or its employees, volunteers, donors, contractors or visitors in the Park, or (c) a breach of this Lease by Lessee. This indemnity does not apply to the extent that a claim results from the negligence or willful misconduct of Lessor or its agents.

11.6 Lessor's Indemnity. Lessor shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Lessee and its directors, officers, employees and volunteers from and against claims, losses and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising from the negligence or willful misconduct of Lessor or its agents or contractors in the Park, or from a breach of this Lease by Lessor. This indemnity does not apply to the extent that a claim results from the negligence or willful misconduct of Lessee or its agents.

11.7 Limits on Lessor's Liability. Except for its gross negligence, willful misconduct or breach of this Lease, Lessor is not liable for damage to goods stored in the Premises from roof leaks, sprinkler discharge, flooding, theft or the acts of other occupants. Lessee shall look only to Lessor's interest in the Park and its proceeds to satisfy any claim against Lessor.

## 12. Damage by Casualty

12.1 Repair. If the Premises are damaged by fire or other casualty, Lessor shall tell Lessee in writing within thirty (30) days how long repairs are expected to take. If the expected time is one hundred eighty (180) days or less after the casualty, Lessor shall repair the Building and the improvements it owns, and this Lease continues.

12.2 Right to End Lease. If the expected time is more than one hundred eighty (180) days, either party may end this Lease by notice to the other within twenty (20) days after Lessor's notice. Lessor may also end this Lease if the casualty is not covered by the insurance Lessor is required to carry, or happens in the last six (6) months of the Lease Term, unless in the latter case Lessee exercises an unexpired Extension Option within twenty (20) days after the casualty. Lessee may end this Lease if repairs are not substantially finished within two hundred forty (240) days after the casualty.

12.3 Rent During Repairs. Rent abates in proportion to the area of the Premises that Lessee cannot use, from the date of the casualty until Lessor's repairs are substantially finished.

12.4 Waiver. The parties intend this Section 12 to govern in place of Civil Code sections 1932(2) and 1933(4), which Lessee waives.

## 13. Taking

If a public authority takes all of the Premises, or enough of the Premises, the dock apron or the truck court that Lessee cannot reasonably continue its operations, this Lease ends when possession passes. If a smaller part is taken, Rent shall be reduced in proportion to the floor area taken and Lessor shall restore what is left to a usable whole to the extent of the award it receives. The award for the land and Building belongs to Lessor. Lessee may claim separately for its moving expenses and for fixtures and equipment that it owns. Lessee waives Code of Civil Procedure section 1265.130.

## 14. Assignment and Subletting

14.1 Prohibition. Lessee's tenancy is personal to Lessee, whose charitable purpose was a material reason for the Base Rent that Lessor agreed to. Lessee shall not assign this Lease or any interest in it, whether voluntarily or by operation of law, shall not sublet all or any part of the Premises, and shall not permit any other person or organization to occupy or share the Premises, and Lessor is not required to consider or respond to any request for consent to do so.

14.2 Effect of Attempt. An assignment or sublease made in violation of Section 14.1 is void and is a Default that cannot be cured.

14.3 Partner Agencies. Section 14.1 does not prevent the staff of social service agencies that refer families to Lessee from being present in the Premises to help those families choose furniture, so long as no agency keeps a desk, equipment or inventory there.

## 15. Lender Matters

15.1 Priority. This Lease is subordinate to the deed of trust now recorded against the Park in favor of Ironquay Savings Bank, and Lessor shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain a non-disturbance agreement from that lender within ninety (90) days after the Execution Date. This Lease shall be subordinate to a later deed of trust only if its beneficiary agrees in writing to recognize this Lease and not to disturb Lessee while no Default exists.

15.2 Attornment. Lessee shall attorn to anyone who succeeds to Lessor's interest by foreclosure or deed in lieu of foreclosure, if that person recognizes this Lease.

15.3 Estoppel. Each party shall, within fifteen (15) business days after the other's request, deliver a signed statement of the status of this Lease, including the dates to which Rent is paid, the amount of the Security Deposit and whether it knows of any default.

15.4 Financial Reports. Lessee shall give Lessor, within one hundred fifty (150) days after the end of each of Lessee's fiscal years, a copy of Lessee's annual financial statements and its most recent filed annual information return. Lessor may share them in confidence with its lender and any buyer of the Park.

## 16. Default

16.1 Default by Lessee. Each of the following is a "Default":

(a) Lessee does not pay Rent within five (5) days after Lessor gives written notice that it is overdue;

(b) Lessee does not maintain the insurance Section 11 requires and does not correct this within five (5) business days after written notice;

(c) Lessee violates Section 14.1;

(d) Lessee does not perform another obligation in this Lease within thirty (30) days after written notice describing the failure, or, where more than thirty (30) days are reasonably needed, does not start within thirty (30) days and finish with diligence;

(e) Lessee or Guarantor becomes the subject of a bankruptcy or insolvency case that, if involuntary, is not dismissed within sixty (60) days, or makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors; or

(f) Guarantor repudiates the Guaranty or dissolves without a successor that assumes the Guaranty.

A notice under paragraph (a), (b) or (d) also serves as the notice required by Code of Civil Procedure section 1161 if it meets the requirements of that section and is served as that section provides.

16.2 Lessor's Right to Terminate. If a Default occurs, Lessor may terminate Lessee's right to possession by any lawful method. In that case Lessor may recover from Lessee the amounts allowed by Civil Code section 1951.2, namely:

(a) the worth at the time of award of the unpaid Rent that had been earned at the time of termination;

(b) the worth at the time of award of the amount by which the unpaid Rent that would have been earned after termination until the time of award exceeds the amount of the rental loss that Lessee proves could have been reasonably avoided;

(c) the worth at the time of award of the amount by which the unpaid Rent for the balance of the term after the time of award exceeds the amount of the rental loss that Lessee proves could be reasonably avoided; and

(d) any other amount necessary to compensate Lessor for all the detriment proximately caused by Lessee's failure to perform this Lease.

The worth at the time of award under paragraphs (a) and (b) is computed with interest at the Default Rate, and under paragraph (c) by discounting at the discount rate of the Federal Reserve Bank of San Francisco at the time of award plus one percent (1%).

16.3 Lessor's Right to Continue Lease. ## Schedule 2. Operating Expenses

Part A. Included Costs. Operating Expenses are the reasonable costs that Lessor pays or incurs for the operation, maintenance, repair and insurance of the Building and the Park, in the following categories:

1. Real property taxes, as Section 5.6 describes them.

2. Premiums for the insurance that Lessor carries under Section 11.3, and deductibles of up to $25,000 per occurrence (or, for earthquake coverage, the deductible amortized over ten (10) years).

3. Maintenance, repair and periodic replacement of the roof membrane, skylights, gutters and downspouts, but not structural repairs to the roof structure.

4. Exterior painting and caulking of the Building, amortized over seven (7) years.

5. Maintenance, testing and monitoring of the fire sprinkler risers, fire pump and alarm system.

6. Landscaping, irrigation, tree care and weed control.

7. Sweeping, striping, sealing and repair of drive aisles, car parking lots and truck courts.

8. Exterior lighting of the Park and its electricity.

9. Storm water system maintenance, sampling and reporting.

10. Park security patrols and gate maintenance, if Lessor provides them.

11. A property management fee of three percent (3%) of the Base Rent collected from the Park.

12. The cost of a capital replacement of an item in this Part A, amortized over its useful life with interest at seven percent (7%) per year, with only the annual amortization included in each year.

Part B. Excluded Costs. Operating Expenses do not include:

1. Structural repairs to the roof structure, foundation and load-bearing walls.

2. Costs for which Lessor is reimbursed by insurance, by a warranty, by another occupant or by any other third party, which Lessor shall deduct in the year the reimbursement is received.

3. Costs that Lessee pays directly to a utility, contractor or other third party.

4. Depreciation, debt service and ground rent.

5. Commissions, legal fees and improvement costs for leasing space in the Park.

6. Costs of work or services for the sole benefit of another occupant.

7. Costs of correcting the conditions that Lessor must correct at its own cost under Section 2.3.

8. Costs of investigating or cleaning up Hazardous Substances that Lessee did not release.

9. Lessor's entity-level overhead, and wages of personnel above the level of Park manager.

10. Penalties and interest for Lessor's late payment of any bill.

Part C. Truck Court Allocation. The cost of sweeping, striping, sealing, lighting and repairing the shared truck court on the south side of the Building is not allocated by floor area. It is allocated among the occupants of the Building by trailer stall count, because wear on the truck court follows trailer traffic and not the size of each unit. Lessee has eight (8) of the forty (40) trailer stalls in that truck court, so Lessee's share of those costs is twenty percent (20%). Lessor keeps a written stall assignment schedule for the Building, a copy of which was given to Lessee before it signed this Lease, and Lessor shall give Lessee an updated copy whenever the assignments change. If Lessee's stall count changes, its share changes to match from the first day of the next month.

Part D. Timing of Charges. Lessor may include in any estimate or year-end statement, and Lessee shall pay its share of, any cost within Part A that Lessor incurred at any time during the Lease Term or during the thirty-six (36) months before the Start Date and has not previously billed to an occupant of the Premises, without regard to how long ago the cost was incurred.

Part E. Partial Years. For 2027 and for the year in which the tenancy ends, Operating Expenses are prorated by days. Lessee's obligation to pay, and Lessor's obligation to refund, amounts for periods within the tenancy continue after the tenancy ends.

## Schedule 3. Park Rules

1. Trucks and trailers shall use the Drayage Row truck entrance only. The posted speed limit in the Park is ten (10) miles per hour.

2. Trailers shall be parked only in the stalls assigned to the occupant. No trailer shall be left on the dock apron overnight unless it is at a dock door. For each trailer found parked in the Park outside the occupant's assigned stalls, the occupant shall pay Lessor $1,000 per trailer for each day or part of a day, as liquidated damages, which Lessor may add to the next installment of Rent without prior notice or an opportunity to move the trailer.

3. Truck engines shall not idle for more than five (5) minutes.

4. Drivers shall not sleep overnight in the Park.

5. Pallets, dunnage and broken furniture shall be kept inside the Building or in the occupant's enclosed dumpster area and removed at least weekly.

6. Forklifts and pallet jacks shall not be driven across landscaped areas or car parking lots. Propane cylinders shall be stored in a locked cage outside the Building in the place Lessor designates.

7. Nothing shall be attached to or hung from the roof structure, the sprinkler piping or the demising walls without Lessor's written approval.

8. Dock doors shall be kept closed when not in use during the hours of darkness.

9. Occupants shall keep the fire lanes, fire department connections and electrical rooms clear at all times.

10. Vehicle washing, oil changes and vehicle repairs are not allowed in the Park, except for emergency repairs needed to move a disabled vehicle.

11. Signs shall conform to the Park sign program. Each occupant may install one building-mounted identification sign above its office entrance and its name on the Park directory sign at Lessor's standard charge.

12. Collection bins for donations shall not be placed outside the Building.

13. Each occupant shall give the Park management office the names and telephone numbers of two persons who can respond to an alarm at any hour.

14. Animals shall not be kept in the Park, other than service animals.

6. Heating and air conditioning. Units in working order, with new filters and a service report dated within the last sixty (60) days.

7. Exterior. Signs removed and the wall patched and painted to match. Trailers, bins and vehicles removed from the Park. The dumpster area emptied.
