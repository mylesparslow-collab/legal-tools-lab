# EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT - CHIEF OPERATING OFFICER

_Fictional document for software testing. All parties, people, addresses and facts are invented. Not legal advice and not a form._

THIS EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT (the "Agreement") is made on March 8, 2027, between SABLEWOOD SENIOR LIVING PARTNERS, LLC, a California limited liability company whose principal office is at 915 Tannery Quay Boulevard, Suite 300, Brightmoor Landing, California ("Sablewood" or the "Company"), and DESMOND ACHTERBERG, an individual who resides in Brightmoor Landing, California (the "Executive").

## BACKGROUND

1. Sablewood, through its wholly owned operating subsidiaries, holds the licenses for and operates fourteen residential care facilities for the elderly in California, offering assisted living and memory care to about 1,260 residents (each such facility, and any facility that Sablewood or a subsidiary operates or manages in the future, a "Community").

2. The Company's Board of Managers (the "Board") wishes to employ the Executive as Chief Operating Officer, with responsibility for the day-to-day operation of the Communities, and the Executive wishes to accept that employment.

3. The Executive lives in California, will be based at the Company's principal office in Brightmoor Landing, California, will spend the rest of the Executive's working time at Communities located in California, and will live and work primarily in California for as long as this Agreement is in effect.

4. The parties wish to record the terms of the employment in this Agreement.

The parties therefore agree as follows.

## ARTICLE I - DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Definitions.** Capitalized terms used in this Agreement have the following meanings.

"**Annual Incentive**" means the annual cash incentive described in Section 4.2.

"**Base Salary**" means the salary described in Section 4.1, as increased from time to time.

"**Cause**" means (a) the Executive's conviction of, or plea of no contest to, a felony, or any crime of elder abuse, neglect, theft or dishonesty; (b) the Executive's loss of, or failure to obtain within the time allowed by the licensing agency, any criminal record clearance or exemption that the law requires for a person in the Executive's position; (c) the Executive's gross negligence or willful misconduct in performing the Executive's duties; (d) the Executive's willful failure to report, or willful concealment of, a reportable incident affecting the health or safety of a resident; (e) the Executive's material breach of this Agreement or of a material written Company policy, which, if it can be cured, is not cured within twenty (20) days after written notice from the Board; (f) the Executive's being barred from taking part in any federal or state health care program; or (g) the Executive's fraud on, or embezzlement or misappropriation of the property of, the Company or a resident.

"**Class P Units**" means the profits interest units of the Company described in Section 4.4.

"**Company Group**" means the Company and each of its direct and indirect subsidiaries.

"**Date of Termination**" means the last day on which the Executive is employed by the Company.

"**Developments**" has the meaning given in Section 8.1.

"**Disability**" means a physical or mental condition that leaves the Executive unable to perform the essential functions of the Executive's position, with or without reasonable accommodation, for more than one hundred fifty (150) days (whether or not consecutive) in any period of twelve (12) months, as determined in a manner consistent with applicable law.

"**Fiscal Year**" means the twelve months ending December 31.

"**Good Reason**" means any of the following that happens without the Executive's written consent: (a) a reduction of the Base Salary or of the target Annual Incentive percentage; (b) a material reduction of the Executive's title, authority or duties, or a requirement that the Executive report to anyone other than the Chief Executive Officer or the Board; (c) a move of the Executive's principal office to a place more than forty (40) miles from Brightmoor Landing, California; or (d) a material breach of this Agreement by the Company. A resignation is for Good Reason only if the Executive gives the Board written notice of the event within forty-five (45) days after it first occurs, the Company does not cure it within thirty (30) days after the notice, and the Executive resigns within sixty (60) days after the cure period ends.

"**LLC Agreement**" means the Third Amended and Restated Limited Liability Company Agreement of the Company dated August 19, 2025, as amended.

"**Proprietary Information**" has the meaning given in Section 7.1.

"**Sale of the Company**" has the meaning given in the LLC Agreement.

"**Separation Agreement**" means a separation agreement and release in the form attached as Exhibit A, with the blanks completed and with such changes as the Company reasonably makes to reflect changes in law after the date of this Agreement.

"**Severance Period**" means the nine (9) months that follow the Date of Termination or, if the Date of Termination falls within twelve (12) months after a Sale of the Company, the fifteen (15) months that follow the Date of Termination.

**1.2 Construction.** In this Agreement, "including" means "including but not limited to"; the singular includes the plural; a reference to a law is to that law as amended; and a reference to a Section, Schedule or Exhibit is to a section of, or schedule or exhibit to, this Agreement. Schedule A and Exhibit A form part of this Agreement.

## ARTICLE II - EMPLOYMENT

**2.1 Position and Start Date.** The Company will employ the Executive as its Chief Operating Officer beginning April 5, 2027 (the "Start Date"). The Executive will report to the Company's Chief Executive Officer, Ilse Varnadoe-Quist, or her successor (the "CEO").

**2.2 Duties.** The Executive will be responsible for the operation of the Communities, including (a) resident care and services, including oversight of the Vice President of Clinical Services and the regional directors of operations; (b) compliance with the licensing laws and regulations that apply to residential care facilities for the elderly, including staffing, training, medication management, incident reporting and the correction of deficiencies cited by the licensing agency; (c) occupancy, move-in and move-out processes and resident agreements; (d) dining, housekeeping, maintenance, life enrichment and transportation services; (e) Community-level budgets, labor management and purchasing; (f) emergency and disaster preparedness; (g) the opening of new Communities, including the two Communities now under development in Quarry Bend and Windom Ridge, California; and (h) other duties consistent with the position that the CEO or the Board assigns.

**2.3 Licensing Requirements.** The Executive will, before the Start Date, submit fingerprints and obtain the criminal record clearance that California law requires for persons who have contact with residents or who are responsible for the operation of a licensed facility, and will keep that clearance in effect. The Executive holds a current California certificate as an administrator of residential care facilities for the elderly and will keep it current at the Company's expense. The Company will pay all fees for fingerprinting, clearance, certificate renewal and required continuing education.

**2.4 Full Time.** The Executive will devote the Executive's full working time and best efforts to the Company Group. The Executive may serve on the board of the Coastal Range Elder Services Alliance, a trade association, and of one charitable organization, and may manage personal investments, so long as those activities do not interfere with the Executive's duties or create a conflict of interest. The Executive will not accept any gift, payment or other benefit from a vendor, referral source or resident of a Community except as the Company's Gifts and Gratuities Policy allows.

**2.5 Workplace.** The Executive's principal office will be at 915 Tannery Quay Boulevard, Brightmoor Landing, California. The Executive is expected to visit each Community at least once in each calendar quarter and to travel to other places in California as the business requires.

**2.6 Policies.** The Executive will comply with the Company's written policies as in effect from time to time, including its Resident Rights Policy, Elder Abuse Reporting Policy, Privacy and Security Policies and Employee Handbook. If a policy conflicts with this Agreement, this Agreement controls.

**2.7 Mandated Reporter.** The Executive acknowledges that the Executive is a mandated reporter of suspected elder and dependent adult abuse under California law and has signed the acknowledgment that the law requires. Nothing in this Agreement limits or delays any report that the law requires or permits the Executive to make.

## ARTICLE III - AT-WILL EMPLOYMENT

**3.1 At Will.** The Executive's employment has no fixed term and is at will. The Executive or the Company may end it at any time, for any lawful reason or for no reason, with or without Cause and with or without notice, subject to Articles V and VI. This at-will relationship can be changed only by a written agreement that is approved by the Board and signed by the Executive and the Chair of the Board.

**3.2 No Other Promises.** The Executive acknowledges that no officer, manager or member of the Company has promised the Executive employment for any set period, and that no handbook, policy or practice of the Company changes the at-will nature of the employment.

## ARTICLE IV - COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS

**4.1 Base Salary.** The Company will pay the Executive a Base Salary of Three Hundred Sixty Thousand Dollars ($360,000) a year, in equal installments every two weeks on the Company's regular paydays. The Board will review the Base Salary each year, beginning in January 2028, and may increase but not decrease it.

**4.2 Annual Incentive.** For each Fiscal Year the Executive may earn an Annual Incentive with a target of forty percent (40%) of Base Salary and a maximum of eighty percent (80%) of Base Salary, based on the measures and weights in Part 2 of Schedule A or, for later Fiscal Years, on measures that the Board sets by March 1 of the Fiscal Year after consulting the Executive. The Annual Incentive for Fiscal Year 2027 will be prorated from the Start Date. An Annual Incentive is earned on the last day of the Fiscal Year, provided the Executive is employed on that day, and is paid within thirty (30) days after the Company receives its audited financial statements for the Fiscal Year and in any case by March 15 of the following year.

**4.3 Sign-On Payment and Recruitment Costs.** The Company will pay the Executive a sign-on payment of Forty Thousand Dollars ($40,000), less withholding, with the first paycheck after the Start Date. The Company has also paid a search firm fee to recruit the Executive. If the Executive resigns for any reason before the first anniversary of the Start Date, the Executive shall pay the Company, as reimbursement of its recruitment costs, an amount equal to three (3) months of the Base Salary previously paid to the Executive.

**4.4 Class P Units.** Within sixty (60) days after the Start Date, and subject to Board approval and to the Executive signing the Company's standard unit grant agreement and a joinder to the LLC Agreement, the Company will grant the Executive 18,500 Class P Units, which are intended to be profits interests for federal income tax purposes, with a distribution threshold set by the Board at the time of grant. Sixty percent (60%) of the Class P Units will vest in five equal annual installments on the first five anniversaries of the Start Date, and forty percent (40%) will vest on a Sale of the Company in which the Company's Class A members receive a return of at least 2.25 times their invested capital, in each case if the Executive is still employed on the vesting date. All unvested time-based Class P Units will vest on a Sale of the Company if the Executive is still employed on the date of the sale. The treatment of Class P Units on termination of employment is governed by the unit grant agreement and Section 6.3(d).

**4.5 Benefits.** The Executive may take part in the health, dental, vision, life insurance, disability and 401(k) plans that the Company makes available to its senior officers, under the terms of those plans. The Company may change or end any plan so long as the change applies to senior officers generally.

**4.6 Vacation.** The Executive will accrue paid vacation at the rate of 6.16 hours for each biweekly pay period (about four weeks a year). Once the Executive's accrued and unused vacation reaches three hundred (300) hours, no further vacation will accrue until the Executive uses vacation and the balance falls below that cap. Accrued vacation is never forfeited, and on the Date of Termination, whatever the reason for the termination, the Company will pay the Executive for all accrued and unused vacation at the Executive's final rate of pay.

**4.7 Sick Leave and Holidays.** The Executive will receive paid sick leave and paid holidays under the Company's policies for senior officers, which will be at least as generous as California law requires.

**4.8 Expenses.** The Company will reimburse the Executive for all reasonable and necessary expenses that the Executive incurs in carrying out the Executive's duties, including lodging, meals, airfare, professional dues and the cost of a mobile phone and data plan used for Company business, in each case when the Executive submits receipts under the Company's expense policy. Travel by personal vehicle is governed by Part 3 of Schedule A.

**4.9 Indemnification.** The Company will defend and indemnify the Executive, and advance the Executive's expenses, to the fullest extent that the LLC Agreement and California law allow, against claims that arise from the Executive's good-faith performance of the Executive's duties, and will cover the Executive under its management liability and professional liability insurance policies during the employment and for six (6) years afterwards on the same terms as its other officers.

**4.10 Withholding.** All payments under this Agreement are subject to the withholding of taxes and other amounts that the law requires.

## ARTICLE V - TERMINATION

**5.1 Events of Termination.** The Executive's employment ends on the first of the following: (a) the Executive's death; (b) the date stated in a written notice from the Company that the employment is ended because of Disability, which may not be earlier than thirty (30) days after the notice; (c) the date stated in a written notice from the Company that the employment is ended for Cause; (d) the date stated in a written notice from the Company that the employment is ended without Cause; (e) the date of the Executive's resignation for Good Reason; and (f) the date of the Executive's resignation without Good Reason.

**5.2 Cause Procedure.** Before ending the employment for Cause under clause (c) or (e) of the definition of Cause, the Board will give the Executive written notice of the facts on which it relies and a reasonable chance to respond, in person or in writing, and with counsel if the Executive wishes. The Board may place the Executive on paid administrative leave while it considers the matter, and where a licensing agency or law enforcement agency so requires.

**5.3 Notice of Resignation.** The Executive is asked to give at least sixty (60) days' written notice of a resignation without Good Reason so that the Company can arrange a handover and make any filing that the licensing agency requires. The Company may relieve the Executive of duties during the notice period, or may end the employment sooner, in which case it will continue to pay the Base Salary for the rest of the notice period, up to sixty (60) days.

**5.4 Resignation of Positions.** On the Date of Termination the Executive will be treated as having resigned from every office and position that the Executive holds with any member of the Company Group, including as a manager or officer of any licensee subsidiary, and will sign the documents needed to update the Company's licensing records.

**5.5 Licensing Transition.** For thirty (30) days after the Date of Termination, the Executive will answer reasonable questions from the Company and from the licensing agency about matters that were pending on the Date of Termination. The Company will pay the Executive Two Hundred Dollars ($200) an hour for that time and will reimburse the Executive's expenses.

## ARTICLE VI - PAYMENTS AFTER TERMINATION

**6.1 Final Pay.** Whatever the reason for the termination, the Executive is entitled to Base Salary earned through the Date of Termination, any Annual Incentive earned for a Fiscal Year that ended before the Date of Termination and not yet paid, accrued and unused vacation, and unreimbursed business expenses. The Company will pay all earned and unpaid Base Salary, Annual Incentive and vacation pay within thirty (30) days after the Date of Termination.

**6.2 Termination for Cause, Resignation without Good Reason, Death or Disability.** If the employment ends for Cause, by resignation without Good Reason, or because of death or Disability, the Company will have no obligation to the Executive under this Agreement other than under Section 6.1 and Section 4.9, except that on death or Disability the Company will also pay a prorated target Annual Incentive for the Fiscal Year of termination within sixty (60) days after the Date of Termination.

**6.3 Termination without Cause or Resignation for Good Reason.** If the Company ends the employment without Cause, or the Executive resigns for Good Reason, then, if the conditions in Section 6.4 are met, the Company will provide the following in addition to the amounts in Section 6.1:

(a) the Base Salary, at the rate in effect on the Date of Termination, for the Severance Period, paid on the Company's regular paydays beginning with the first payday that falls after the Separation Agreement takes effect;

(b) a prorated Annual Incentive for the Fiscal Year of termination, based on actual results for the whole Fiscal Year, paid when annual incentives are paid to the Company's other officers;

(c) if the Executive elects to continue group health coverage under COBRA or the California continuation law, the employer share of the premium for the Executive and the Executive's covered dependents during the Severance Period or, if sooner, until the Executive is eligible for coverage with a new employer; and

(d) vesting of the time-based Class P Units that would have vested on the next anniversary of the Start Date after the Date of Termination.

**6.4 Conditions.** The benefits in Section 6.3 (but not the amounts in Section 6.1) are conditioned on the Executive signing the Separation Agreement, the Separation Agreement taking effect within sixty (60) days after the Date of Termination, and the Executive's continued compliance in all material respects with Articles VII and VIII. If that sixty (60) day period spans two calendar years, payments that are deferred compensation under Section 409A of the Internal Revenue Code will begin in the second year.

**6.5 No Duty to Mitigate.** The Executive need not look for other work, and, except as Section 6.3(c) provides, the benefits in Section 6.3 will not be reduced by other earnings.

**6.6 Exclusive Severance.** The benefits in Section 6.3 take the place of any severance under any other Company plan or policy.

## ARTICLE VII - PROPRIETARY INFORMATION

**7.1 Meaning.** "Proprietary Information" means non-public information of the Company Group that has value because it is not generally known, including resident and family information, referral source lists and terms, pricing and rate schedules, occupancy and labor data, care assessment tools, staffing models, development and acquisition plans, lender and landlord terms, financial statements, and the terms of contracts with vendors and health plans. It does not include information that is public other than through the Executive's breach, information that the Executive lawfully had before the Start Date, or the Executive's general skills and experience in senior living operations.

**7.2 Duty.** The Executive will not, during or after the employment, use Proprietary Information except to do the Executive's job for the Company Group, and will not disclose it to anyone outside the Company Group except as the job properly requires, as the law requires, or as the Company agrees in writing.

**7.3 Resident Privacy.** The Executive will protect the privacy of residents' personal and health information as the Company's Privacy and Security Policies and applicable federal and California law require, and will report any suspected breach of that information to the Company's Privacy Officer at once.

**7.4 Rights That Are Not Limited.** Nothing in this Agreement stops the Executive from (a) discussing or disclosing the Executive's own wages or working conditions, or those of other employees, as California law allows; (b) reporting suspected abuse or neglect, or any other possible violation of law, to the licensing agency, the long-term care ombudsman, adult protective services, law enforcement or any other government agency; (c) cooperating with or giving information to any such agency, and receiving an award for doing so; or (d) giving truthful testimony. Nothing in this agreement prevents you from discussing or disclosing information about unlawful acts in the workplace, such as harassment or discrimination or any other conduct that you have reason to believe is unlawful.

**7.5 Trade Secret Immunity Notice.** Federal law provides that an individual may not be held criminally or civilly liable under any federal or state trade secret law for disclosing a trade secret in confidence to a federal, state or local government official or to an attorney solely for the purpose of reporting or investigating a suspected violation of law, or in a complaint or other document filed under seal in a lawsuit or other proceeding. An individual who sues an employer for retaliation for reporting a suspected violation of law may disclose the trade secret to the individual's attorney and use it in the court proceeding, if any document containing the trade secret is filed under seal and the trade secret is not disclosed except under court order.

**7.6 Return of Materials.** On the Date of Termination the Executive will return all keys, badges, devices, records and other property of the Company Group and all copies of Proprietary Information, other than documents about the Executive's own pay, benefits and Class P Units.

## ARTICLE VIII - DEVELOPMENTS

**8.1 Meaning.** "Developments" means all care programs, assessment tools, training materials, operating manuals, software, data models, designs, marks, writings, inventions, improvements and ideas, whether or not they can be patented or registered.

**8.2 Ownership.** All Developments that the Executive makes or conceives, alone or with others, while employed by the Company belong to the Company, and the Executive assigns them to the Company. This assignment extends to Developments that the Executive creates on the Executive's own time, with the Executive's own equipment and without Proprietary Information, and that do not relate to the business of the Company Group, and the Executive waives the benefit of any law that would limit its scope.

**8.3 Disclosure and Help.** The Executive will tell the Company promptly about each Development and, during and after the employment, will sign the papers and give the reasonable help that the Company needs, at the Company's cost, to register and enforce its rights.

**8.4 Earlier Work.** The Executive has told the Company that the Executive owns a set of memory care staff training modules titled "Steady Hours", written in 2022, which are not Developments. The Executive grants the Company Group a royalty-free license to use those modules in the Communities for as long as the Executive is employed and for one year afterwards.

## ARTICLE IX - OTHER COVENANTS

**9.1 Reasons for Covenants.** The Executive agrees that the Executive will learn the operating methods, rates, referral relationships and expansion plans of the Company Group and that the covenants in this Article are a material part of what the Company is receiving for the compensation in Article IV.

**9.2 Competing Communities.** For eighteen (18) months after the Date of Termination, the Executive shall not own, manage, operate, consult for or be employed by any person that owns or operates an assisted living, memory care or independent living community located within fifty (50) miles of any Community. Ownership of less than one percent (1%) of the stock of a public company is not a breach of this Section.

**9.3 Trade Secrets.** The Executive will not use any trade secret of the Company Group to solicit residents, referral sources or employees away from the Company Group, or for any other purpose that the Company has not approved.

**9.4 Statements.** Subject to Section 7.4, the Executive will not knowingly make a false statement of fact that harms the reputation of the Company Group or its Communities, and the Company will direct the members of the Board and its officers not to knowingly make a false statement of fact that harms the reputation of the Executive.

**9.5 Cooperation in Proceedings.** After the Date of Termination the Executive will, on reasonable notice, cooperate with the Company in any licensing appeal, survey, investigation, claim or lawsuit that concerns events during the employment. The Company will pay the Executive's reasonable expenses and, after the first eight (8) hours in any month, Two Hundred Dollars ($200) an hour. Nothing in this Section requires the Executive to give anything other than truthful information.

## ARTICLE X - TAXES

**10.1 Section 409A.** This Agreement is to be read so that all payments are either exempt from or comply with Section 409A of the Internal Revenue Code. Each payment is a separate payment. Payments that depend on termination of employment will be made only on a separation from service within the meaning of Section 409A. If the Executive is a specified employee when the separation from service occurs, any payment of deferred compensation due in the following six (6) months will be held and paid in a lump sum, without interest, on the first payday after those six (6) months or, if sooner, after the Executive's death. Expense reimbursements will be paid by the end of the calendar year after the year in which the expense was incurred, and the amount reimbursed in one year will not affect the amount available in another.

**10.2 Parachute Payments.** If the Company is a corporation, or is treated as one, at the time of a Sale of the Company, and any payment to the Executive would be subject to the excise tax under Section 4999 of the Internal Revenue Code, the payments will be reduced to the highest amount that avoids the tax if, and only if, the reduction leaves the Executive with more after tax than the Executive would have kept without it. The Company's regular outside accountants will make the calculation at the Company's cost.

**10.3 Profits Interest Election.** The Executive will file an election under Section 83(b) of the Internal Revenue Code for the Class P Units within thirty (30) days after the grant and will give the Company a copy. The Executive has been told to seek advice from the Executive's own tax adviser and is not relying on the Company for tax advice.

## ARTICLE XI - DISPUTES

**11.1 Law.** California law governs this Agreement and every claim that arises from the employment.

**11.2 Courts.** Each party agrees that any lawsuit about this Agreement or the employment will be filed in a state or federal court in California whose district includes Brightmoor Landing, and consents to the jurisdiction of those courts.

**11.3 Mediation First.** Other than a request for an injunction or a filing with a government agency, neither party will file a lawsuit against the other until the parties have tried to settle the dispute in a mediation of at least one-half day before a neutral mediator. The Company will pay the mediator. If the mediation has not taken place within forty-five (45) days after one party asks for it in writing, either party may go to court. Any limitation period is tolled from the date of the written request until fifteen (15) days after the mediation ends or that forty-five (45) day period runs out.

**11.4 Injunctions.** Because a breach of Article VII or VIII could cause harm that money cannot repair, the Company may ask a court for an injunction as well as damages.

**11.5 Costs.** Each party pays its own legal fees and costs, unless a statute gives the winning party a right to recover them.

## ARTICLE XII - MISCELLANEOUS

**12.1 Notices.** A notice under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered by hand, by overnight courier or by certified mail. A notice to the Company is to be sent to its principal office, marked for the attention of the Chair of the Board, and a notice to the Executive is to be sent to the home address shown in the Company's personnel records. A notice takes effect when it is delivered or when delivery is refused.

**12.2 Whole Agreement.** This Agreement, with its Schedule and Exhibit, the LLC Agreement and the unit grant agreement, contains the whole agreement between the parties about the employment and replaces the term sheet dated February 12, 2027 and all other earlier discussions.

**12.3 Changes and Waivers.** A change to this Agreement, or a waiver of any of its terms, is valid only if it is in writing and signed by the Executive and, for the Company, by the Chair of the Board.

**12.4 Severability.** If a court finds any term of this Agreement unenforceable, the court is to enforce the term as far as the law allows and to enforce the rest of this Agreement as written.

**12.5 Successors.** The Executive may not assign this Agreement. The Company may assign it to a buyer of all or substantially all of its business, and will cause any such buyer to take on the Company's obligations in writing. Amounts owed to the Executive at death are payable to the Executive's estate.

**12.6 Survival.** Articles VI through XII and Section 4.9 continue after the employment ends.

**12.7 Counterparts.** The parties may sign this Agreement in counterparts and by electronic signature.

**12.8 Background Check and Work Eligibility.** The offer of employment recorded in this Agreement depends on the Executive completing the Company's lawful background check, obtaining the clearance described in Section 2.3, and showing that the Executive is eligible to work in the United States.

**12.9 Time to Review.** The Executive confirms that the Executive received this Agreement on February 26, 2027, has read it, and signs it of the Executive's own free will.
