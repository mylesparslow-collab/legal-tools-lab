# Shopping Center Lease (Triple Net): Alder Crossing Shopping Center, Suite C-7

This Shopping Center Lease (this "Lease") is dated as of January 12, 2027 (the "Effective Date") and is made between Tamsin Ridge Retail Partners, LLC, a California limited liability company ("Landlord"), and Finch & Thimble Fiber Arts, LLC, a California limited liability company ("Tenant").

## Recitals

A. Landlord owns the neighborhood shopping center commonly known as Alder Crossing Shopping Center, located at 4410 Pellam Orchard Road, Marrowfield, Verano County, California (the "Center"), which is anchored by a grocery store and contains approximately 92,500 square feet of gross leasable area in five buildings.

B. Tenant operates a retail yarn, fabric and craft supply shop with instructional classes, and wishes to lease space in Building C of the Center.

C. Before the Effective Date, Tenant delivered to Landlord a written notice, with a self-attestation of its number of employees, stating that Tenant is a qualified commercial tenant because it is a microenterprise with five or fewer employees. Landlord acknowledges receipt of that notice and agrees that, for purposes of this Lease, Tenant is a "qualified commercial tenant".

D. Before the Effective Date, and before Tenant signed this Lease, Landlord delivered to Tenant a separate written notice stating that Tenant may inspect any supporting documentation of the building operating costs that Landlord charges to Tenant under this Lease. Tenant acknowledges receipt of that notice.

E. Landlord is willing to lease the Premises to Tenant, and Tenant is willing to lease the Premises from Landlord, on the terms of this Lease.

The parties therefore agree as follows.

## Article 2. Definitions

In addition to terms defined elsewhere, the following terms have these meanings.

2.1 "Additional Rent" means every sum Tenant owes Landlord under this Lease other than Monthly Base Rent, including Tenant's Share of Operating Costs, Taxes and Insurance Costs.

2.2 "Common Areas" means the parts of the Center that Landlord designates from time to time for the non-exclusive use of tenants and their customers, including parking areas, driveways, sidewalks, landscaped areas, service corridors, trash enclosures, lighting facilities, pylon and monument sign structures, and shared utility lines.

2.3 "Default Rate" means ten percent (10%) per year, or the highest rate allowed by law if that rate is lower.

2.4 "Insurance Costs" means the premiums and other costs of the insurance Landlord carries for the Center under Article 11, including commercially reasonable deductibles.

2.5 "Laws" means all statutes, ordinances, codes, regulations, orders and recorded covenants that apply to the Center, the Premises or their use, as they exist now or are later enacted or amended.

2.6 "Lease Year" means each period of twelve (12) full calendar months beginning on the Commencement Date or an anniversary of it. If the Commencement Date is not the first day of a month, the first Lease Year also includes the partial month in which the Commencement Date falls.

2.7 "Operating Costs" has the meaning given in Section 5.2.

2.8 "Rent" means Monthly Base Rent and Additional Rent.

2.9 "Taxes" means all real property taxes and general and special assessments levied against the Center, including any increase caused by a change in ownership or new construction, any tax on rents that is levied in place of or in addition to real property taxes, and the reasonable cost of contesting any of them. Taxes do not include Landlord's income, franchise, gift, estate or inheritance taxes.

2.10 "Tenant Parties" means Tenant and its members, managers, employees, agents, contractors, instructors and invitees.

## Article 3. Premises and Term

3.1 Lease of Premises. Landlord leases the Premises to Tenant, and Tenant leases the Premises from Landlord, for the Term and on the terms of this Lease. The square footage figures in the Basic Lease Provisions are approximations that the parties accept as final. Rent will not be adjusted if a later measurement differs.

3.2 Common Areas. Tenant and its customers have the non-exclusive right to use the Common Areas with others entitled to use them, subject to the Rules and Regulations. Landlord may change the size, layout and location of the Common Areas and of buildings in the Center, close parts of the Common Areas temporarily for repairs, and build additional improvements, so long as Landlord does not materially and permanently reduce access to the Premises, the visibility of Tenant's storefront sign from the main drive aisle, or the number of parking spaces below the number that Laws require.

3.3 Delivery. Landlord shall deliver the Premises with Landlord's Work described in Exhibit B substantially complete. If the Delivery Date has not occurred by June 1, 2027 for any reason other than a delay caused by Tenant, Tenant may terminate this Lease by written notice given before delivery, and Landlord shall then promptly refund the Security Deposit and Prepaid Rent. That refund is Tenant's only remedy for late delivery.

3.4 Early Access. From the Delivery Date until the Commencement Date, Tenant may enter the Premises to build Tenant's Work and install fixtures and inventory. All terms of this Lease other than the obligation to pay Monthly Base Rent and Additional Rent apply during that period. Tenant shall pay for utilities used during that period.

3.5 Commencement Date Memorandum. Within fifteen (15) days after the Commencement Date, the parties shall sign a memorandum in the form of Exhibit F confirming the Delivery Date, the Commencement Date and the Expiration Date. A failure to sign the memorandum does not affect those dates.

3.6 Condition. Except for Landlord's Work and Landlord's repair obligations in Article 8, Tenant accepts the Premises in their condition on the Delivery Date. Landlord warrants that, on the Delivery Date, the roof will be free of leaks and the heating, ventilating and air conditioning unit serving the Premises, the electrical panel and the plumbing lines stubbed to the Premises will be in good working order. If Tenant gives written notice of a breach of that warranty within ninety (90) days after the Delivery Date, Landlord shall correct the item at Landlord's cost, and that cost shall not be included in Operating Costs.

## Article 4. Rent

4.1 Monthly Base Rent. Beginning on the Commencement Date, Tenant shall pay Monthly Base Rent in advance on the first day of each calendar month, without notice, demand, deduction or offset except as this Lease expressly allows. Rent for a partial month shall be prorated on the basis of the actual number of days in that month.

4.2 Place and Manner of Payment. Tenant shall pay Rent by electronic funds transfer to the account Landlord designates in writing, or by check delivered to Landlord's notice address if Landlord has not designated an account. Landlord shall give at least thirty (30) days' written notice of any change in the account.

4.3 Late Charge. Tenant acknowledges that late payment of Rent will cause Landlord to incur costs that are difficult to fix, including processing and accounting costs and charges that Landlord's lender may impose. If Landlord does not receive any installment of Rent on the date it is due, Tenant shall pay Landlord, without notice or grace period, a late charge equal to twenty percent (20%) of the overdue amount for each month or part of a month that the amount remains unpaid. The late charge is in addition to interest under Section 4.4, and both shall accrue on the same overdue amount. Acceptance of a late charge does not waive Tenant's default or limit Landlord's other remedies.

4.4 Interest. Any Rent not paid when due bears interest at the Default Rate from the due date until paid.

4.5 Returned Payments. If a payment by Tenant is returned for insufficient funds, Tenant shall reimburse the fee that Landlord's bank charges Landlord for the returned item. After two returned payments in any twelve (12) month period, Landlord may require that Rent for the following twelve (12) months be paid by cashier's check or wire transfer.

4.6 Application of Payments. Landlord may apply any payment first to accrued interest, next to other Additional Rent, and last to Monthly Base Rent, regardless of any designation by Tenant. No endorsement or statement on a check or in a letter accompanying a payment is an accord and satisfaction.

## Article 5. Operating Costs, Taxes and Insurance Costs

5.1 Net Lease. This Lease is a net lease. Tenant shall pay, as Additional Rent, Tenant's Share of Operating Costs, Taxes and Insurance Costs for each calendar year or partial calendar year in the Term, as this Article provides.

5.2 Operating Costs. "Operating Costs" means all costs that Landlord pays or incurs to own, operate, manage, maintain, repair, replace, clean, light, insure, secure and landscape the Common Areas and the parts of the Center that Landlord must maintain. Operating Costs include, for example:

(a) resurfacing, sealing, restriping and sweeping parking areas and driveways;

(b) landscaping, irrigation, tree trimming and pest control;

(c) Common Area utilities, lighting and re-lamping;

(d) trash removal and recycling for Common Area receptacles and shared enclosures;

(e) security patrols, if Landlord elects to provide them;

(f) maintenance and repair of roofs, exterior walls, storm drains, fire sprinkler mains and monitoring, and shared utility lines;

(g) wages, payroll taxes and benefits of on-site personnel, to the extent they work on the Center;

(h) a management fee not greater than four percent (4%) of the gross rents of the Center;

(i) the cost of a capital repair or replacement, amortized with interest at seven percent (7%) per year over its useful life as Landlord reasonably determines under generally accepted real estate accounting practice, with only the amortized amount for a year included in that year; and

(j) the cost of repairing damage to the Center caused by fire, vandalism, vehicle impact or other casualty.

5.3 Recoveries. Operating Costs under Section 5.2(j) and every other cost of repair or restoration shall be included in Operating Costs in full, whether or not Landlord later recovers insurance proceeds, warranty payments or reimbursement from another tenant or third party on account of the same cost, and Landlord shall not be required to credit any such recovery against Operating Costs.

5.4 Exclusions. Operating Costs do not include: (a) depreciation; (b) principal and interest on Landlord's loans and rent under any ground lease; (c) leasing commissions, tenant improvement allowances and the cost of preparing space for another tenant; (d) legal fees for disputes with other tenants or for negotiating leases; (e) costs that Tenant pays directly to a utility or other third party for the Premises; (f) fines or penalties caused by Landlord's violation of Laws; (g) costs of correcting defects in the original construction of the Center; (h) Landlord's general corporate overhead not related to the Center; and (i) the cost of removing Hazardous Materials that Tenant Parties did not bring to the Center.

5.5 Tenant's Share. "Tenant's Share" of Taxes and Insurance Costs is Tenant's Pro Rata Share. Tenant's Share of Operating Costs shall be the share that Landlord assigns to the Premises each year in such manner as Landlord determines in its sole discretion, which need not be proportionate to the floor area of the Premises or follow any fixed or written method, and which may differ from the shares assigned to other tenants of similar size.

5.6 Costs Incurred Within the Covered Period. Landlord shall include in Operating Costs for a calendar year only costs that Landlord incurred within the eighteen (18) months before the date of the statement on which they are billed, or that Landlord reasonably expects to incur within the twelve (12) months after that date.

5.7 Estimated Payments. Before each calendar year, or as soon afterward as practical, Landlord shall give Tenant a written estimate of Tenant's Share of Operating Costs, Taxes and Insurance Costs for that year. Tenant shall pay one-twelfth of the estimate with each installment of Monthly Base Rent. Until Landlord delivers a new estimate, Tenant shall continue to pay at the prior year's rate. Landlord may revise an estimate once during a calendar year by at least thirty (30) days' written notice.

5.8 Annual Statement. Within one hundred twenty (120) days after the end of each calendar year, Landlord shall deliver a statement showing the actual Operating Costs, Taxes and Insurance Costs for that year by category, the calculation of Tenant's Share, and the estimated payments Tenant made. If Tenant paid less than the actual amount, Tenant shall pay the difference within thirty (30) days after receiving the statement. If Tenant paid more, Landlord shall credit the excess against the next Rent due or, if the Term has ended, refund it within thirty (30) days.

5.9 Supporting Documents. Within thirty (30) days after Tenant's written request, Landlord shall provide Tenant with supporting documentation of the Operating Costs, Taxes and Insurance Costs charged to Tenant, including dated and itemized invoices, receipts, contracts or similar records. Tenant may make such a request at any time within two (2) years after receiving the statement to which the request relates. If the documents show that Tenant was overcharged, Landlord shall refund the overcharge within thirty (30) days.

5.10 Taxes on Tenant's Property. Tenant shall pay before delinquency all taxes levied on its trade fixtures, inventory, equipment and other personal property in the Premises. If any of those taxes are levied against Landlord or the Center, Tenant shall reimburse Landlord within thirty (30) days after receiving a copy of the tax bill.

5.11 Partial Years. Tenant's Share of Operating Costs, Taxes and Insurance Costs for the calendar years in which the Term begins and ends shall be prorated by the number of days of the Term in that year. This Article survives the end of the Term as to amounts for periods before the end of the Term.

## Article 6. Security Deposit

6.1 Deposit. On signing this Lease, Tenant shall deposit the Security Deposit with Landlord as security for Tenant's performance. Landlord is not required to hold the Security Deposit in a separate account or to pay interest on it.

6.2 Application. If Tenant defaults in the payment of Rent, Landlord may apply the Security Deposit to the unpaid Rent. Landlord may also apply the Security Deposit to repair damage to the Premises caused by Tenant, and to clean the Premises on the end of the tenancy, in each case in the amount reasonably necessary for that purpose. If Landlord applies any part of the Security Deposit during the Term, Tenant shall restore the applied amount within ten (10) business days after written demand.

6.3 Return. Landlord shall return the Security Deposit, or the portion of it that remains after any application permitted by Section 6.2, to Tenant within ninety (90) days after the Expiration Date, together with a written statement of any amounts applied.

6.4 Transfer. If Landlord transfers its interest in the Center, Landlord shall transfer the Security Deposit to the transferee and give Tenant written notice of the transfer and of the transferee's name and address. On that transfer and notice, Landlord is released from further liability for the Security Deposit.

## Article 7. Use and Operation

7.1 Permitted Use. Tenant shall use the Premises only for the Permitted Use and under the Trade Name. Tenant shall not use the Premises for any use that violates an exclusive use right or prohibited use listed in Exhibit C, Part 2, as that list exists on the Effective Date.

7.2 Operation. Beginning on the Commencement Date, Tenant shall keep the Premises open for business, fully stocked and staffed, at least from 10:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. Monday through Saturday and from 11:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. on Sunday, other than on New Year's Day, Easter Sunday, Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day. Tenant may close for up to five (5) additional days each Lease Year for inventory or staff training on at least ten (10) days' written notice to Landlord, and may close when casualty, condemnation or Laws make operation impractical.

7.3 Compliance with Laws. Tenant shall comply at its cost with all Laws that apply to Tenant's particular use of the Premises and to alterations Tenant makes. Tenant shall obtain and keep in force every license and permit needed for the Permitted Use.

7.4 Accessibility. Landlord is responsible, as an Operating Cost to the extent Article 5 allows, for making the Common Areas, including accessible parking stalls and the path of travel from the parking area to the storefront door of the Premises, comply with the Americans with Disabilities Act and Title 24 of the California Code of Regulations as those laws are enforced on the Delivery Date. Tenant is responsible, at its cost, for the compliance of the interior of the Premises with those laws, including any alteration to the interior that is triggered by Tenant's Work, by Tenant's later alterations or by Tenant's particular use. If a governmental authority requires work in the Common Areas solely because of an alteration that Tenant makes, Tenant shall reimburse the reasonable cost of that work within thirty (30) days after Landlord's invoice with supporting documents.

7.5 Restrictions. Tenant shall not (a) commit waste or a nuisance; (b) conduct an auction, fire sale, going-out-of-business sale or bankruptcy sale without Landlord's written consent; (c) place merchandise, racks or signs on the sidewalk, except for two sidewalk sale events per Lease Year that Landlord approves in advance; (d) use sound equipment that can be heard outside the Premises; or (e) do anything that would increase the premium for, or cause cancellation of, Landlord's insurance.

7.6 Hazardous Materials. Tenant shall not bring onto the Center any substance regulated as hazardous or toxic under Laws ("Hazardous Materials"), other than ordinary quantities of cleaning supplies and fabric dyes used and stored in compliance with Laws. Tenant shall indemnify Landlord against all claims, costs and liabilities that result from Hazardous Materials that Tenant Parties release in the Center. Landlord shall indemnify Tenant against all claims, costs and liabilities that result from Hazardous Materials present in the Premises on the Delivery Date or released in the Center by Landlord or its agents. This Section survives the end of the Term.

7.7 Rules and Regulations. Tenant shall comply with the Rules and Regulations in Exhibit C and with reasonable changes to them that Landlord delivers in writing, so long as the changes are enforced without discrimination and do not conflict with this Lease.

## Article 8. Maintenance and Repairs

8.1 Landlord's Obligations. Landlord shall maintain in good condition and repair, as an Operating Cost to the extent Article 5 allows, the foundation, structural walls, roof structure and roof membrane of Building C, the exterior walls (other than storefronts, doors and glass), the Common Areas, and utility lines that serve more than one tenant. Landlord shall begin a repair within a reasonable time after Tenant's written notice that the repair is needed.

8.2 Tenant's Obligations. Tenant shall keep the Premises and every part of them in good, clean and safe condition, and shall repair and replace as needed the storefront, plate glass, doors, interior walls, floor coverings, ceiling tiles, lighting, plumbing fixtures and lines exclusively serving the Premises from the point of connection, the electrical system within the Premises, and the heating, ventilating and air conditioning unit serving the Premises. Tenant shall keep in force a quarterly service contract for that unit with a licensed contractor and shall give Landlord a copy on request. If the unit must be replaced during the last two (2) Lease Years, Landlord shall replace it and Tenant shall pay each month, as Additional Rent, the cost of the replacement amortized over its useful life with interest at seven percent (7%) per year, for the months that remain in the Term.

8.3 Landlord's Right to Perform. If Tenant fails to make a repair it is required to make within thirty (30) days after Landlord's written notice, or sooner in an emergency, Landlord may make the repair and Tenant shall reimburse Landlord's reasonable cost, plus an administrative charge of ten percent (10%) of that cost, within thirty (30) days after an invoice with supporting documents.

8.4 Waiver. Tenant waives the benefit of Civil Code sections 1932(1), 1941 and 1942 and any similar law that would allow Tenant to make repairs at Landlord's expense or to terminate this Lease because of Landlord's failure to repair.

## Article 9. Alterations and Signs

9.1 Alterations. Tenant shall not make alterations to the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent, which Landlord shall not unreasonably withhold, condition or delay for alterations that do not affect the structure, the roof, the storefront or building systems. Tenant may, without consent but with ten (10) days' prior notice, make interior cosmetic alterations that cost less than $7,500 in any Lease Year.

9.2 Performance. Tenant shall cause all alterations to be made by licensed and insured contractors, in a good and workmanlike manner, with required permits, and in compliance with Laws. Tenant shall give Landlord at least ten (10) days' notice before work begins so that Landlord may post a notice of nonresponsibility.

9.3 Liens. Tenant shall keep the Center free of mechanics' liens arising from work done for Tenant. If a lien is recorded, Tenant shall cause it to be released or bonded within twenty (20) days after notice from Landlord.

9.4 Ownership and Removal. Alterations become part of the Premises when installed and shall remain on the end of the Term, unless Landlord, when it consents to an alteration, states in writing that the alteration must be removed. Tenant shall remove its trade fixtures, shelving, furniture and inventory on or before the end of the Term and repair any damage caused by removal.

9.5 Signs. Tenant shall install, at its cost and before opening for business, a storefront sign that meets the Sign Criteria in Exhibit D, and shall maintain it in good condition. Tenant may place one panel on the Pellam Orchard Road monument sign in the position shown on Exhibit D so long as Tenant is open and operating. Tenant shall not install any other exterior sign, banner or window graphic without Landlord's consent.

## Article 10. Utilities

Tenant shall pay directly to the provider for all electricity, gas, telephone, data and other utilities separately metered to the Premises, beginning on the Delivery Date. Water and sewer service to Building C is submetered, and Tenant shall pay Landlord for submetered water and sewer at Landlord's actual cost without markup, within thirty (30) days after each invoice. ## Article 11. Insurance and Indemnity

11.1 Tenant's Insurance. Tenant shall keep in force at its cost, beginning on the Delivery Date:

(a) commercial general liability insurance with limits of at least $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 in the aggregate, naming Landlord, its property manager and its lender as additional insureds;

(b) property insurance on a special form basis for the full replacement cost of Tenant's Work, alterations, trade fixtures, inventory and personal property;

(c) business income insurance covering at least six (6) months of loss;

(d) plate glass coverage, which Tenant may self-insure; and

(e) workers' compensation insurance as Laws require.

11.2 Policy Requirements. Tenant's insurers must be authorized to do business in California and rated at least A- VII by a nationally recognized rating service. Tenant shall deliver certificates of insurance before the Delivery Date and at least ten (10) days before each policy expires. Each policy must require the insurer to try to give Landlord thirty (30) days' notice of cancellation.

11.3 Landlord's Insurance. Landlord shall keep in force property insurance on a special form basis for the full replacement cost of the buildings in the Center, excluding tenants' property, commercial general liability insurance for the Common Areas, and rental loss insurance. Landlord may carry earthquake and flood insurance and other coverage that owners of similar centers in Verano County commonly carry.

11.4 Waiver of Subrogation. Landlord and Tenant each release the other, and waive their rights of recovery against the other, for loss or damage to their own property to the extent the loss is covered by the property insurance that this Lease requires the releasing party to carry, or that it actually carries. Each party shall cause its property insurer to waive subrogation against the other.

11.5 Tenant's Indemnity. Tenant shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Landlord and its members, property manager and lender from all claims, liabilities, damages and costs, including reasonable attorneys' fees, that arise from (a) an occurrence in the Premises, (b) the negligence or willful misconduct of Tenant Parties in the Center, or (c) Tenant's breach of this Lease, except to the extent caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Landlord or its agents.

11.6 Landlord's Indemnity. Landlord shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Tenant and its members and employees from all claims, liabilities, damages and costs, including reasonable attorneys' fees, that arise from the negligence or willful misconduct of Landlord or its agents in the Center, or from Landlord's breach of this Lease, except to the extent caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Tenant Parties.

11.7 Exemption of Landlord. Landlord is not liable for damage to Tenant's property or loss of Tenant's business income caused by fire, water, theft, acts of other tenants or interruption of utilities, unless the damage is caused by the gross negligence or willful misconduct of Landlord or its agents or by Landlord's breach of this Lease after notice and a reasonable time to cure.

## Article 12. Damage and Destruction

12.1 Restoration. If the Premises or Building C are damaged by fire or other casualty, Landlord shall promptly restore the building shell and Landlord's Work to the condition that existed before the casualty, subject to Section 12.2, and Tenant shall promptly restore Tenant's Work, alterations, fixtures and inventory and reopen for business.

12.2 Termination. Landlord may terminate this Lease by written notice within sixty (60) days after the casualty if (a) Landlord's architect estimates that restoration will take more than two hundred ten (210) days from the date of the casualty, (b) the casualty is not covered by the insurance Landlord is required to carry and the uninsured cost of restoration exceeds $150,000, or (c) the casualty occurs in the last twelve (12) months of the Term. Tenant may terminate this Lease by written notice within thirty (30) days after receiving the architect's estimate if the estimate exceeds two hundred ten (210) days, or if restoration is not in fact substantially complete within two hundred seventy (270) days after the casualty.

12.3 Abatement. Monthly Base Rent and Additional Rent shall abate from the date of the casualty until the earlier of the date Tenant reopens and sixty (60) days after Landlord substantially completes its restoration, in proportion to the part of the Premises that Tenant cannot reasonably use.

12.4 Waiver. This Article governs the parties' rights on a casualty. Tenant waives Civil Code sections 1932(2) and 1933(4).

## Article 13. Condemnation

If all of the Premises, or so much of the Premises or the parking area that Tenant cannot reasonably operate, is taken by eminent domain or sold under threat of a taking, this Lease ends on the date the condemning authority takes possession. If part of the Premises is taken and this Lease does not end, Landlord shall restore the remainder to a complete unit and Monthly Base Rent shall be reduced in proportion to the floor area taken. The entire award belongs to Landlord, except that Tenant may claim from the condemning authority a separate award for its trade fixtures, relocation expenses and loss of goodwill. Tenant waives Code of Civil Procedure section 1265.130.

## Article 14. Assignment and Subletting

14.1 Consent Required. Tenant shall not assign this Lease, sublet all or any part of the Premises, grant a license or concession in the Premises, or encumber this Lease (each a "Transfer") without Landlord's prior written consent. Landlord may grant or withhold its consent to any proposed Transfer in Landlord's sole and absolute discretion, for any reason or for no reason, and Landlord has no obligation to consider the financial strength, experience or proposed use of the proposed transferee.

14.2 Change of Control. A transfer, in one transaction or a series, of more than fifty percent (50%) of the membership interests in Tenant, or of the power to direct its management, is a Transfer. A transfer of membership interests among Tenant's existing members or to a member's family trust for estate planning is not a Transfer if the Guarantor remains the manager of Tenant.

14.3 Request. A request for consent must be in writing and must include the name of the proposed transferee, the terms of the proposed Transfer, and the transferee's financial statements and business history. Tenant shall reimburse Landlord's reasonable out-of-pocket legal fees for reviewing a request, not to exceed $1,500 per request.

14.4 No Release. No Transfer releases Tenant or the Guarantor from liability under this Lease. Consent to one Transfer is not consent to another. A Transfer made without Landlord's consent is voidable by Landlord and is a default under this Lease.

14.5 Excess Rent. If Landlord consents to a Transfer, Tenant shall pay Landlord fifty percent (50%) of any rent or other consideration that Tenant receives from the transferee in excess of the Rent payable under this Lease, after Tenant first recovers its reasonable brokerage commissions and legal fees for the Transfer.

## Article 15. Defaults and Remedies

15.1 Events of Default. Each of the following is an "Event of Default":

(a) Tenant fails to pay Rent when due and the failure continues for five (5) days after written notice from Landlord;

(b) Tenant vacates or abandons the Premises, or fails to operate as Section 7.2 requires for more than ten (10) consecutive days without Landlord's consent;

(c) Tenant fails to perform any other obligation under this Lease and the failure continues for thirty (30) days after written notice from Landlord, or, if the failure cannot reasonably be cured within thirty (30) days, Tenant does not begin to cure within that time and diligently complete the cure within ninety (90) days;

(d) Tenant or the Guarantor makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or files a petition in bankruptcy, or a petition in bankruptcy is filed against either of them and is not dismissed within sixty (60) days; or

15.2 Termination. On an Event of Default, Landlord may terminate Tenant's right to possession by any lawful means, in which case this Lease ends and Tenant shall immediately surrender the Premises. Landlord may then recover from Tenant:

(a) the worth at the time of award of the unpaid Rent earned at the time of termination;

(b) the worth at the time of award of the amount by which the unpaid Rent that would have been earned after termination until the time of award exceeds the rental loss that Tenant proves could have been reasonably avoided;

(c) the worth at the time of award of the amount by which the unpaid Rent for the balance of the Term after the time of award exceeds the rental loss that Tenant proves could be reasonably avoided; and

(d) any other amount needed to compensate Landlord for the detriment proximately caused by Tenant's failure to perform, including reasonable costs of recovering possession, reletting, brokerage commissions and necessary repairs.

The "worth at the time of award" of the amounts in clauses (a) and (b) is computed by allowing interest at the Default Rate. The worth at the time of award of the amount in clause (c) is computed by discounting at the discount rate of the Federal Reserve Bank of San Francisco at the time of award plus one percent (1%).

15.3 Continuation of Lease. Landlord has the remedy described in California Civil Code section 1951.4 (a lessor may continue a lease in effect after the lessee's breach and abandonment and recover rent as it becomes due, if the lessee has the right to sublet or assign, subject only to reasonable limitations). Accordingly, if an Event of Default occurs and Tenant abandons the Premises, Landlord may elect not to terminate Tenant's right to possession, and may keep this Lease in effect and enforce all of its rights under this Lease, including the right to recover Rent as it becomes due, for so long as Landlord does not terminate Tenant's right to possession. Acts of maintenance or preservation, efforts to relet the Premises, and the appointment of a receiver to protect Landlord's interest do not terminate Tenant's right to possession.

15.4 Remedies Cumulative. Landlord's remedies under this Lease are cumulative and are in addition to every other remedy allowed by law.

15.5 Landlord's Default. Landlord is in default if it fails to perform an obligation under this Lease within thirty (30) days after written notice from Tenant, or, if the obligation cannot reasonably be performed within thirty (30) days, if Landlord does not begin within that time and diligently complete performance. 15.6 Limitation on Landlord's Liability. ## Article 16. Holding Over

16.1 Without Consent. If Tenant remains in possession after the end of the Term without Landlord's written consent, Tenant is a tenant at sufferance, and Tenant shall pay for each day of the holdover one-thirtieth of one hundred fifty percent (150%) of the Monthly Base Rent in effect in the last month of the Term, plus Additional Rent. The parties understand that a matter heard by a referee is decided by the referee and not by a jury. The parties shall share the referee's fees equally, subject to reallocation under Section 20.2.
