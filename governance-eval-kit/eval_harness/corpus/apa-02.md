THIS ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "Agreement") is made as of March 8, 2027 (the "Effective Date") among WILLOWMERE COURT SENIOR LIVING, LLC, a California limited liability company ("Seller"), THADDEUS OKONKWO-LARRABEE, an individual residing in Corriente County, California ("Owner"), and BRINDLE HOLLOW SENIOR COMMUNITIES, INC., a California corporation ("Buyer").

## BACKGROUND

1. Seller owns and operates a residential care facility for the elderly known as Willowmere Court Assisted Living and Memory Care (the "Community"), located on approximately 4.8 acres at 2275 Saltgrass Lane, Pellham Vista, California. The Community consists of 84 assisted living apartments and a 28-suite secured memory care neighborhood known as the Lantern Wing.

2. The Community is licensed by the California Department of Social Services, Community Care Licensing Division (the "Department"), as a residential care facility for the elderly with a licensed capacity of 118 residents under Facility No. 417609233 (the "Existing License"). The Community does not participate in the Medicare program, and it accepts no payment under the Assisted Living Waiver.

3. Seller employs 92 persons at the Community as of the Effective Date and has employed not fewer than 85 persons there in each of the preceding twelve (12) months.

4. Owner is the sole member and the sole manager of Seller, founded the Community in 2009, and will receive, through Seller, all of the net proceeds of the sale, including the portion of the Purchase Price paid for the goodwill of the Community.

5. The sale of the Community to Buyer will result in the issuance of a new license by the Department in the name of Buyer.

6. Seller wishes to sell, and Buyer wishes to buy, the Community and the assets used in its operation, on the terms set out below.

The parties therefore agree as follows.

## ARTICLE III. PURCHASE PRICE

**Section 3.1 Amount.** The purchase price for the Assets is Twenty-One Million Four Hundred Thousand Dollars ($21,400,000) (the "Purchase Price").

**Section 3.2 Payment.** The Purchase Price is payable as follows:

(a) Buyer shall deposit Six Hundred Thousand Dollars ($600,000) (the "Earnest Money") with Escrow Holder within two (2) Business Days after the Effective Date. Escrow Holder shall invest the Earnest Money in a federally insured interest-bearing account, and interest is part of the Earnest Money. The Earnest Money becomes non-refundable at the end of the Due Diligence Period, except as provided in Sections 5.1, 10.1 and 10.3.

(b) At the Closing, Buyer shall deposit with Escrow Holder the balance of the Purchase Price, plus or minus prorations, in immediately available funds.

(c) Escrow Holder shall retain One Million Dollars ($1,000,000) of the Purchase Price at the Closing (the "Holdback") and hold it under the Holdback Escrow Agreement in the form of Exhibit B for fifteen (15) months after the Closing Date to secure Seller's obligations under Article IX.

**Section 3.3 Allocation.** The Purchase Price is allocated $15,900,000 to the Real Property, $1,350,000 to the Tangible Property and inventory, and $4,150,000 to goodwill and other intangible Assets, as further detailed on Schedule 3.3. Each party shall report the transaction for tax purposes consistently with Schedule 3.3.

**Section 3.4 Prorations and Credits.** The following items shall be apportioned between Seller and Buyer as of the Effective Time:

(a) Resident fees. Monthly fees under the Residency Agreements that have been collected for the month in which the Closing occurs shall be prorated on a per diem basis. Fees for that month collected after the Closing shall be prorated when received. Fees for earlier months that are collected by Buyer after the Closing shall be remitted to Seller within fifteen (15) days after the end of the month of receipt, but only after the resident's current charges have been paid.

(b) Deposits and prepaid fees. Buyer shall receive a credit for all refundable deposits, prepaid monthly fees for periods after the Closing, and the unearned portion of any preadmission or community fee that is refundable under the applicable Residency Agreement or under Law.

(c) Taxes and assessments. Real property taxes and assessments shall be prorated on the basis of the fiscal year for which they are assessed. Supplemental taxes for periods before the Closing are Seller's responsibility, whenever billed.

(d) Utilities and Assigned Contracts. Charges for water, sewer, gas, electricity, waste, telephone and cable service, and amounts due under the Assigned Contracts, shall be prorated on the basis of the most recent invoices.

Escrow Holder shall prepare a settlement statement for approval by both parties at least three (3) Business Days before the Closing Date. The parties shall correct any error or estimate within one hundred twenty (120) days after the Closing Date, and the party owing money shall pay it within ten (10) Business Days after the correction is agreed.

**Section 9.5 Claims Procedure.** The indemnified party shall notify the indemnifying party in writing of any claim within thirty (30) days after learning of it; late notice reduces the indemnity only to the extent of actual prejudice. The indemnifying party may control the defense of a third-party claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified party, who may participate at its own cost. **Section 9.6 Source of Recovery.** Buyer shall look first to the Holdback and then to Seller and Owner. Except for fraud and for equitable remedies, this Article is the sole remedy after the Closing for breach of this Agreement.
