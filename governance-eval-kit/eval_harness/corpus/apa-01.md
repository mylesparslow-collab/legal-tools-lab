## RECITALS

A. Seller owns the land and improvements commonly known as 4410 Quillfeather Road, Tessandra, California, and is the licensed operator of the 99-bed skilled nursing facility located there and known as Harrowgate Pines Care Center (the "Facility").

B. The Facility is licensed by the California Department of Public Health (the "Department") as a skilled nursing facility under License No. 550009914 and is certified to participate in the Medicare program and the Medi-Cal program. All 99 beds are dually certified.

C. As of the Effective Date Seller employs approximately 128 persons at the Facility, including full-time, part-time and per diem personnel, and Seller has employed not fewer than 100 persons at the Facility at all times during the twelve (12) months preceding the Effective Date.

D. Seller desires to sell, and Buyer desires to purchase, the real property, the personal property and substantially all of the other assets used in the operation of the Facility, and the parties desire to provide for an orderly transfer of the operations of the Facility from Seller to Buyer, all on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

E. Principal owns all of the issued and outstanding shares of Seller and will receive a substantial benefit from the transactions contemplated by this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows.
