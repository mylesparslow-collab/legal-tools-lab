# Corrections to the research report

The research report this kit was built from contains citation and description errors. Everything below was checked against the statute or regulation text on leginfo.legislature.ca.gov, ecfr.gov or courts.ca.gov on November 25, 2026. The kit follows the primary text, not the report.

## Errors

1. **SB 706 progressive design-build.** The report cites Pub. Cont. Code §§ 22170–22174. Those sections are SB 991 (local agency water projects, 15 projects, sunset January 1, 2029). SB 706 is §§ 22185–22185.11: up to 10 projects over $5 million, subcontractor rules in § 22185.6, retention in § 22185.7, a report to the Legislature by December 31, 2028 in § 22185.8, sunset January 1, 2030.
2. **Brown Act cybersecurity closed session.** The report says Gov. Code § 54954.5 has cybersecurity safe-harbor agenda wording. It does not; § 54954.5 was last amended in 2012. The cybersecurity language is in § 54957(a), added by AB 2715 (Stats. 2024), and the agenda heading remains "THREAT TO PUBLIC SERVICES OR FACILITIES".
3. **Brown Act remote attendance.** "Just cause" remote attendance continues in Gov. Code § 54953.8.3 until January 1, 2030. The report also omits § 54953.4, which requires two-way remote public access for eligible bodies from July 1, 2026 and was amended by SB 1187 effective September 18, 2026.
4. **Medical staff fair hearings.** Bus. & Prof. Code § 809.1 sets no 30-day minimum to request a hearing; the time is set by the bylaws. The 30 days in § 809.2(d) concerns access to documents and continuances. Witness lists are exchanged at least 10 days before the hearing (§ 809.2(f)). The hearing starts within 60 days of the request (§ 809.2(h)). The "more than 14 days" trigger is the § 805(e) reporting rule for summary suspensions, not § 809.5.
## Things the report leaves out that the deadlines helper needed

The report lists Code Civ. Proc. §§ 10, 12, 12a, 12c, 135, 1005, 1010.6 and 1013 and the 2026–2027 court holidays, without detail. Reading the sections turned up three points that are easy to get wrong:

5. **Summary judgment timing.** Code Civ. Proc. § 437c is not in the report's list. It runs on 81 days' notice, 20 days for the opposition and 11 days for the reply. Older materials show 75/14/5.
6. **Service extensions differ by method.** Under § 1005(b), fax, express mail and overnight delivery add two calendar days to motion notice. Electronic service adds two court days under § 1010.6(a)(3)(B).
7. **Judicial holidays are not the same as state holidays.** § 135 (as amended by AB 268, effective January 1, 2026) excludes Lunar New Year, Diwali, April 24, September 9 and Columbus Day even though they are state holidays under Gov. Code § 6700.

## Things the report describes that this kit does differently

8. **Deadlines helper language.** The report describes a Catala 1.3.0 program verified across 52,312 date combinations. This kit uses plain Python with every step cited and 23 tests with hand-counted expected dates. The 52,312 figure was not reproduced.
9. **Corpus size.** The report describes twelve test documents of 9,400 to 15,000 words. This corpus is twelve documents of roughly 6,200 to 9,500 words each, three per type.
10. **Benchmark numbers.** No AI product was run for this kit, so any performance figures in the report are not reproduced here. The two sample runs are made-up output generated from the answer keys so the scorer can be tried.
11. **Copilot agents.** The packages here follow the declarative agent manifest schema v1.9 and pass local structure tests. They have not been loaded into any Microsoft 365 tenant.

## Please double-check

- **Farmworkers Day 2027.** The Judicial Council's published 2027 court holiday schedule lists the March 31 holiday (renamed Farmworkers Day by AB 2156) as observed on Friday, April 9, 2027. The helper follows the published table. That date is unusual, so confirm it against the court's own calendar before relying on any deadline near it.
- **Pub. Cont. Code § 9204.** By its own terms it is repealed on January 1, 2027 unless extended. The public works checklist flags this.
- **CPRA section numbers.** The checklist uses the recodified §§ 7920.000 and following. If you work from older materials, confirm the old-to-new mapping in the disposition table.
