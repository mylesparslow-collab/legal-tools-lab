Read the planning columns first. They place the fee among the planning figures and give the walk-forward back-test for this matter type at the bands either side: each past matter judged against bands set only from what was known the day it opened. The in-sample columns count the same comps the figures were built from, so they flatter the fee.

Margin at a planning figure: revenue under the scenario if the work came to that figure at standard rates, less cost taken as 32.5% of that figure (the comps' total cost over their total standard value). A phased fee has no single figure to compare and is judged in sample only. Cost uses the cost rates in the input file: PLACEHOLDER values for the demo only. Internal cost per hour by level is an input that finance must supply; these figures are invented and are not estimates of any firm's costs.

  • Fixed fee $150,000: Revenue is the fee on every comp, whatever the work turned out to be.
  • Capped fee, cap $200,000: Billed hourly at standard rates up to the cap; work above the cap is not paid.

Scope assumptions and exclusions from the comp evidence

  • Comps with a hearing (n=16) had a median cost of $231,700, 2.0x the median of comps without one ($117,100, n=50). Quote the hearing as a separate phase, or state that it is excluded.
  • The 17 comps above the P75 cost $277,500 more on average than the other 49. Discovery and diligence accounts for 28% of that gap ($77,600). Set a scope limit or a separate fee for that phase.
  • 9 matters of this type closed abnormally (settled early, transferred or other) and are not in the comps. The figures assume the matter runs to completion; say what happens to the fee if it ends early.
  • The phase split for 4 of 66 comps comes from the date-window fallback, not task codes. Treat per-phase figures as lower confidence; totals are not affected.
  • Across the 66 comps, 12.4% of worked value was written down before billing and 2.5% of invoiced fees was not collected. A fee built from billed amounts would understate the work; the figures here use worked hours.
