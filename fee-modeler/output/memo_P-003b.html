Scope assumptions and exclusions from the comp evidence

  • The 6 comps above the P75 cost $162,900 more on average than the other 16. Discovery and diligence accounts for 51% of that gap ($82,600). Set a scope limit or a separate fee for that phase.
  • 4 matters of this type closed abnormally (settled early, transferred or other) and are not in the comps. The figures assume the matter runs to completion; say what happens to the fee if it ends early.
  • Across the 22 comps, 5.0% of worked value was written down before billing and 3.0% of invoiced fees was not collected. A fee built from billed amounts would understate the work; the figures here use worked hours.

How the comps were chosen

  • Matter type is internal_investigation.
  • Closed before 2026-01-01 (open matters are never comps).
  • Matters that closed abnormally (settled early, transferred, other) are excluded.
  • Closed within the 3 years before 2026-01-01.
  • At least 8 comps are required before any figure is shown.

50 matters of this type on file; 22 selected as comps.

Left out: closed too long ago (21); open matter (5); abnormal close excluded (4).

Rules are set in the proposal file under selection_rules and can be changed.

Inputs: mapping “Synthetic export, shape A (four tidy files)”; rate card 2026; cost rates from the input file. A person decides the price. Run the back-test before relying on any band.
