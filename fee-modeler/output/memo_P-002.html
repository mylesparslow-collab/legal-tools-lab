Read the planning columns first. They place the fee among the planning figures and give the walk-forward back-test for this matter type at the bands either side: each past matter judged against bands set only from what was known the day it opened. The in-sample columns count the same comps the figures were built from, so they flatter the fee.

Margin at a planning figure: revenue under the scenario if the work came to that figure at standard rates, less cost taken as 32.5% of that figure (the comps' total cost over their total standard value). A phased fee has no single figure to compare and is judged in sample only. Cost uses the cost rates in the input file: PLACEHOLDER values for the demo only. Internal cost per hour by level is an input that finance must supply; these figures are invented and are not estimates of any firm's costs.

  • Fixed fee $45,000: Revenue is the fee on every comp, whatever the work turned out to be.
  • Capped fee, cap $55,000: Billed hourly at standard rates up to the cap; work above the cap is not paid.
  • Fixed fee $45,000 with 10% collar, 50% shared: Inside the band the fee stands. Above it the client pays the shared part of the overrun; below it the client gets back the shared part of the underrun.

Collar: 1 of 20 (5%) inside the band, 5 of 20 (25%) above it, 14 of 20 (70%) below it.

Fee for a target chance of staying within the fee

Target 80%. Planning figure: a fixed fee of $71,000 to cover the work at standard rates; $23,100 to cover cost at the input cost rates.

Scope assumptions and exclusions from the comp evidence

  • The 5 comps above the P75 cost $61,200 more on average than the other 15. Drafting and pleadings accounts for 49% of that gap ($30,000). Set a scope limit or a separate fee for that phase.
  • Comps with more than 2 facilities (n=9) had a median cost of $42,300, 1.6x the median of the rest ($25,700, n=11). State the number of facilities the fee covers and price extra ones separately.
  • Comps with more than 2 parties (n=8) had a median cost of $52,200, 2.1x the median of the rest ($25,100, n=12). State the number of parties the fee covers and price extra ones separately.
  • 7 matters of this type closed abnormally (settled early, transferred or other) and are not in the comps. The figures assume the matter runs to completion; say what happens to the fee if it ends early.
  • The phase split for 2 of 20 comps comes from the date-window fallback, not task codes. Treat per-phase figures as lower confidence; totals are not affected.
  • Across the 20 comps, 2.2% of worked value was written down before billing and 1.1% of invoiced fees was not collected. A fee built from billed amounts would understate the work; the figures here use worked hours.

How the comps were chosen

  • Matter type is facility_change_of_ownership.
  • Closed before 2026-01-01 (open matters are never comps).
  • Matters that closed abnormally (settled early, transferred, other) are excluded.
  • Number of units between 2 and 4.
  • At least 8 comps are required before any figure is shown.

62 matters of this type on file; 20 selected as comps.

Left out: number of units out of range (36); abnormal close excluded (7); open matter (2).

Rules are set in the proposal file under selection_rules and can be changed.

Inputs: mapping “Synthetic export, shape A (four tidy files)”; rate card 2026; cost rates from the input file. A person decides the price. Run the back-test before relying on any band.
