Drift study on regenerated synthetic firms, seeds 101 to 120. The planted type is internal_investigation; a step applies to matters opened from 2023-01-01, a gradual drift compounds from the first opening date. Everything else is as shipped.
New change check: scan of every split by opening date (at least 8 matters each side), flagged when at most 1 in 10 of 1000 seeded reshuffles are as lopsided and the medians differ by 1.25x or more.
Old rule: later half of closes vs earlier half differs by 1.3x, at most 0.02 of 1000 rank shuffles as lopsided, and descriptive P75 coverage more than 15 points off nominal with at least 10 tested.
Thresholds were chosen on seeds 101 to 120 (rerun with --seeds 101-120 and --alpha to see the alternatives). Seeds 1 to 20 were not looked at until the thresholds were fixed.
