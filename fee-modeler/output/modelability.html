At the P75 figure, 117 of 385 back-tested matters cost more; the band implies about 96. At the P90 figure, 64 of 385 back-tested matters cost more; the band implies about 39.

Upper bands are judged one-sided with an exact binomial check: a band is said to understate overruns when seeing that many matters above it, or more, would happen less than 1 time in 20 if the band were right. With about a dozen matter types and two bands each, expect the odd flag by chance.

A type is flagged as shifted only when both things hold: later closes differ from earlier ones by 1.3x or more (and a seeded shuffle test says that is unlikely to be noise), and coverage at P75 is more than 15 points off nominal with at least 10 tested matters. These thresholds are settings of this tool, not industry figures.
