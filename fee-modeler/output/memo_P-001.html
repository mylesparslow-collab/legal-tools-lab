In sample and out of sample are different things. In sample counts the same comps the bands were built from, so it flatters the fee. Out of sample is the walk-forward back-test for this matter type: each past matter judged against bands built only from matters closed before it opened. When the two disagree, believe the out-of-sample figure.

At or under the fee: the comp, billed hourly at standard rates, would have cost the client no more than the fee. Cost uses the cost rates in the input file: PLACEHOLDER values for the demo only. Internal cost per hour by level is an input that finance must supply; these figures are invented and are not estimates of any firm's costs. Margin in the P90 case: 9 in 10 comps would have done better than this.

  • Fixed fee $90,000: Revenue is the fee on every comp, whatever the work turned out to be.
  • Capped fee, cap $120,000: Billed hourly at standard rates up to the cap; work above the cap is not paid.
  • Phased fixed fee, $127,000 if every fixed phase occurs: Each phase fee is charged only on comps where that phase happened.
  • Fixed fee $90,000 with 15% collar, 50% shared: Inside the band the fee stands. Above it the client pays the shared part of the overrun; below it the client gets back the shared part of the underrun.

Scope assumptions and exclusions from the comp evidence

  • Comps with a hearing (n=24) had a median cost of $81,200, 2.3x the median of comps without one ($34,800, n=19). Quote the hearing as a separate phase, or state that it is excluded.
  • The 11 comps above the P75 cost $135,100 more on average than the other 32. Hearing accounts for 59% of that gap ($80,200). Set a scope limit or a separate fee for that phase.
  • Comps with more than 2 parties (n=17) had a median cost of $85,500, 1.6x the median of the rest ($53,400, n=26). State the number of parties the fee covers and price extra ones separately.
  • 11 matters of this type closed abnormally (settled early, transferred or other) and are not in the comps. The figures assume the matter runs to completion; say what happens to the fee if it ends early.
  • The phase split for 5 of 43 comps comes from the date-window fallback, not task codes. Treat per-phase figures as lower confidence; totals are not affected.
  • Across the 43 comps, 3.9% of worked value was written down before billing and 1.4% of invoiced fees was not collected. A fee built from billed amounts would understate the work; the figures here use worked hours.

How the comps were chosen

  • Matter type is public_records_litigation.
  • Closed before 2026-01-01 (open matters are never comps).
  • Matters that closed abnormally (settled early, transferred, other) are excluded.
  • Same forum as proposed (superior_court).
  • Number of parties within 2 of proposed (2).
  • Amount band the same as or next to proposed (B; order A < B < C < D).
  • At least 8 comps are required before any figure is shown.

85 matters of this type on file; 43 selected as comps.

Left out: different forum (19); abnormal close excluded (11); amount band out of range (7); number of parties out of range (5); open matter (5).

Rules are set in the proposal file under selection_rules and can be changed.

Inputs: mapping “Synthetic export, shape A (four tidy files)”; rate card 2026; cost rates from the input file. A person decides the price. Run the back-test before relying on any band.
